Ombudsman, Lokpal & Sector Ombudsmen

Critical Asked in 12 of 12 papers · avg 15 marks

The institution of Ombudsman, its characteristics and Indian perspective, Lokpal (role, structure, jurisdiction, rationale), and Banking, SEBI and Telecom ombudsmen.

In your IGNOU bookBlock 3 · Unit 9 Complaint to Ombudsman

Key points

  • Ombudsman: an independent officer who investigates citizens' complaints against maladministration; began in Sweden (1809). Characteristics: independence and impartiality, easy and free access, informal procedure, power to investigate, recommends remedies, reports publicly.
  • Indian perspective: sector ombudsmen for banking, insurance, taxes and electricity, and the Lokpal and Lokayuktas for corruption.
  • Lokpal (Lokpal and Lokayuktas Act 2013): a chairperson and up to 8 members, half of them judicial. Jurisdiction covers the Prime Minister (with safeguards), ministers, MPs, central government officials of all groups, and bodies funded by government or receiving foreign contributions above a limit. It has inquiry and prosecution wings. The first Lokpal was appointed in 2019. Rationale: an independent body to tackle corruption and restore public trust.
  • Banking Ombudsman: now the RBI Integrated Ombudsman Scheme 2021 (banks, NBFCs, digital payments). Complain to the bank first; if there is no reply in 30 days or it is rejected, file online at cms.rbi.org.in within one year; no fee; grounds — any deficiency in service; award up to ₹20 lakh plus ₹1 lakh for harassment; appeal to the RBI appellate authority.
  • SEBI: the SEBI (Ombudsman) Regulations 2003 listed investor grievances such as non-receipt of refunds, allotment, dividend, interest or transfer. Investor complaints are now handled through SCORES and SEBI's online dispute resolution system.
  • Telecom: consumers first go to the service provider's complaint centre, then its appellate authority under TRAI's grievance regulations; disputes go to TDSAT.

Worked example

Example: a banking complaint under the Integrated Ombudsman Scheme

A bank charges a customer ₹2,000 in fees it had never disclosed. She first complains to the bank. The bank rejects the complaint (or does not reply within 30 days), so she files it online on the RBI's complaint portal under the Integrated Ombudsman Scheme 2021, within one year of the bank's reply. The ombudsman can order a refund and compensation.

How to write the answer

Meaning → features or structure as headings → one sector example → conclusion.

Common mistakes

  • Describing the old Banking Ombudsman Scheme 2006 only — mention the Integrated Ombudsman Scheme 2021.
