Redressal of Consumer Grievances: Role of Various Stakeholders

CPI-102 · Year 3 · 3 hours · 100 marks

Three compulsory parts, all theory: Part A — 4 of 5 short notes (5 marks, about 150 words); Part B — 5 of 7 (10 marks, about 300 words); Part C — 2 of 4 (15 marks, about 500 words).

Exam tips

  • The same institutions come back every session: National Consumer Helpline, Grahak Suvidha Kendras, Lokpal and ombudsmen, ASCI and misleading ads, Consumer Welfare Fund, Consumers International and Consumer Interpol.
  • Mediation, conciliation and arbitration are asked every year — learn the differences in a table.
  • Match length to marks: about 150 words for Part A, 300 for Part B, 500 for Part C.
