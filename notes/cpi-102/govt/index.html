Government Initiatives: DoCA, Jago Grahak Jago & Welfare Fund

High Asked in 11 of 12 papers · avg 11 marks

The Department of Consumer Affairs and its tasks, Jago Grahak Jago, the Consumer Welfare Fund and its eligibility, CORE, Jagriti Shivir Yojana and consumer information centres.

In your IGNOU bookBlock 1 · Unit 3 Role of the State and the Government (3.3–3.4, 3.11)Block 1 · Unit 4 Government Initiatives

Key points

  • Department of Consumer Affairs (DoCA), Government of India: consumer policy; administers the Consumer Protection Act, BIS Act and Legal Metrology Act; monitors prices of essential commodities; runs consumer awareness, the National Consumer Helpline and the Consumer Welfare Fund; acts against misleading ads through the CCPA and the GAMA portal.
  • Jago Grahak Jago ('Wake up, consumer'): DoCA's awareness campaign since 2005 on TV, radio, print and social media — themes such as checking MRP, ISI mark and hallmark, weights and measures, and consumer rights.
  • Consumer Welfare Fund: set up under the Central Excise and Salt Act 1944 (Sec 12C) from duties that cannot be refunded to manufacturers; used for consumer welfare — grants to VCOs, consumer clubs, research, product testing labs, awareness. Eligibility: VCOs registered for at least 3 years under the relevant law, working in consumer welfare with a good record, and other bodies as the rules allow.
  • CORE (Consumer Online Resource and Empowerment Centre): an online grievance and information centre run by the Consumer Coordination Council with government support.
  • Jagriti Shivir Yojana: awareness camps for consumers, especially in rural areas.
  • Consumer information centres give consumers direct information on products, rights and redressal.

Worked example

Example: government initiatives a consumer meets

A 'Jago Grahak Jago' TV spot reminds viewers to check the MRP, the ISI mark and the hallmark on gold. A village awareness camp under the Jagriti Shivir Yojana explains how to complain. A consumer group that runs a testing lab gets a grant from the Consumer Welfare Fund.
