Consumerism, Consumer Rights & Consumer Protection Machinery

Moderate Asked in 9 of 12 papers · avg 8 marks

Consumerism, consumer rights and their expansion, consumer education and awareness, consumer protection councils, and jurisdiction of consumer commissions.

In your IGNOU bookBlock 1 · Unit 3 Role of the State and the Government (3.5–3.9 Consumer protection machinery)Block 2 · Unit 7 Role of Voluntary Consumer Organisations (VCOs) (7.6 Consumer Protection Councils)

Key points

  • Consumerism: the organised movement of consumers and others to protect consumer interests and make sellers accountable.
  • Consumer rights (CPA 2019, Sec 2(9)): safety; information; choice; to be heard; to seek redressal; consumer awareness (education). They grew from President Kennedy's four rights (1962) through the UN Guidelines (1985) and now include a healthy environment and basic needs.
  • Consumer education and awareness: knowing rights, reading labels, keeping bills, using redressal forums — the base of consumer protection.
  • Consumer Protection Councils (CPA 2019): Central (chaired by the Minister of Consumer Affairs), State and District councils — advisory bodies to promote and protect consumer rights.
  • Consumer commissions (CPA 2019): District, State and National Consumer Disputes Redressal Commissions. Pecuniary jurisdiction (from 2021): District up to ₹50 lakh; State ₹50 lakh–₹2 crore; National above ₹2 crore. Appeals: District → State → National → Supreme Court. Complaints can be filed online (e-Daakhil).
  • The Central Consumer Protection Authority (CCPA) acts on unfair trade practices, misleading ads and class actions.

Worked example

Example: which consumer commission?

Under the 2021 rules: a defective refrigerator worth ₹45,000 → District Commission (up to ₹50 lakh). A flat costing ₹1.2 crore → State Commission (₹50 lakh to ₹2 crore). A ₹5 crore insurance claim → National Commission (above ₹2 crore). The complaint should normally be filed within two years of the cause of action, and can be filed online.

The consumer here uses the rights to be heard and to seek redressal.

Common mistakes

  • Quoting only the old CPA 1986 names (District Forum) — use the 2019 Act's commissions.
