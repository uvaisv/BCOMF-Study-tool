Standards, BIS & Labelling

Moderate Asked in 6 of 12 papers · avg 6 marks

The Bureau of Indian Standards — activities, making of Indian standards, the BIS Act 2016 — labelling, and sectors lacking quality-of-service benchmarks.

In your IGNOU bookBlock 1 · Unit 3 Role of the State and the Government (3.10 Legal Metrology, 3.12 BIS)

Key points

  • Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS): the national standards body under the BIS Act 2016. Activities: formulating standards, product certification (ISI mark), hallmarking of gold and silver, laboratory testing, compulsory registration for electronics, management system certification, consumer awareness.
  • Making Indian standards: a proposal → sectional technical committee of experts, industry and consumers → draft widely circulated for comments → consensus → approval and publication; reviewed periodically; international standards are adopted where suitable.
  • BIS Act 2016 features: BIS as the national standards body; wider conformity assessment schemes; government can make certification compulsory for health, safety or environment; mandatory hallmarking; recall of non-conforming products; higher penalties.
  • Labelling: Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules 2011 require name and address of the maker, net quantity, MRP, date of manufacture or packing, and customer care details; food labels also follow FSSAI rules.
  • Quality-of-service benchmarks are still weak in sectors such as healthcare, education, real estate and some transport services, making it hard for consumers to prove deficiency.

Worked example

Example: reading marks and labels

Gold jewellery must carry a BIS hallmark with a 6-digit HUID code, which can be checked on the BIS Care app. An electric iron must carry the ISI mark when certification is compulsory for it.

A packet of biscuits must show the maker's name and address, net quantity, MRP (inclusive of all taxes), date of manufacture or packing, and a customer-care contact. If the net weight is less than stated, the consumer can complain to the Legal Metrology department.
