Consumers International & Consumer Interpol

Critical Asked in 12 of 12 papers · avg 13 marks

Consumers International — status, scope, objectives, activities, charter for consumer action and coordination — and Consumer Interpol's origin, growth and aims.

In your IGNOU bookBlock 4 · Unit 13 International Consumer Organisations

Key points

  • Consumers International (CI): the world federation of consumer organisations, founded in 1960 as the International Organisation of Consumers Unions (IOCU), based in London; has consultative status with UN bodies.
  • Objectives and activities: campaigns on global consumer issues, World Consumer Rights Day (15 March), supporting member groups, research, advocacy with UN, WHO and other bodies; helped bring the UN Guidelines for Consumer Protection (1985).
  • Charter for consumer action: CI's statement of consumer rights and responsibilities that guides member groups' campaigns.
  • Coordination: works with UN agencies, governments, regional offices and member organisations (including Indian VCOs).
  • Consumer Interpol: a network set up by IOCU in 1981 to alert countries about hazardous products, drugs and technologies. Aims: exchange information quickly, warn consumers, stop dumping of banned products in developing countries, press for action.

Worked example

Example: Consumer Interpol at work

A consumer group in one country finds that a brand of baby powder contains a harmful substance and is being withdrawn. Through the Consumer Interpol network it alerts consumer groups in other countries where the same product is still sold, so they can warn people and press their governments to act. Every 15 March, member groups also mark World Consumer Rights Day with a shared theme.

How to write the answer

Meaning → objectives or activities as headed points → relevance to Indian consumers → conclusion.
