Industry Associations: FICCI, CII, ASSOCHAM, PHDCCI

High Asked in 10 of 12 papers · avg 10 marks

FICCI, CII, ASSOCHAM and the PHD Chamber — status, functions and services, allied organisations, and their role in consumer grievance redressal; response from manufacturers and service providers.

In your IGNOU bookBlock 2 · Unit 5 Role of Industry Bodies

Key points

  • FICCI (Federation of Indian Chambers of Commerce and Industry, 1927): the oldest apex business body — policy advocacy, industry voice to government, networking, research and events. Allied organisations include the FICCI Ladies Organisation (FLO) and sector councils.
  • CII (Confederation of Indian Industry): an industry-led, non-government body — policy input, competitiveness and quality programmes, CSR and skill development, consumer and quality initiatives.
  • ASSOCHAM (Associated Chambers of Commerce and Industry of India, 1920): policy advocacy, knowledge reports, events, member services.
  • PHD Chamber of Commerce and Industry (1905): represents industry, especially in north India; policy work, trade promotion, member services.
  • Role in consumer protection: promote ethical codes and self-regulation, set up consumer affairs cells, encourage members to handle complaints, support standards and quality.
  • Response from manufacturers and service providers: companies join the NCH convergence programme, appoint grievance officers, run customer care, and settle complaints to protect their reputation.

Worked example

Example: industry bodies and self-regulation

An industry association drafts a voluntary code asking member e-commerce companies to show the seller's details and return policy clearly and to answer complaints within a fixed time. It sets up a consumer-affairs cell that members' customers can approach, and runs workshops on quality standards — self-regulation that complements the law.

Status → functions as headings → role for consumers → conclusion.
