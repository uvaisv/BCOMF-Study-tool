National Consumer Helpline & Grahak Suvidha Kendras

Critical Asked in 11 of 12 papers · avg 16 marks

The National Consumer Helpline — role, functions and stages of resolving consumer issues — and Grahak Suvidha Kendras.

In your IGNOU bookBlock 3 · Unit 8 National Consumer Helpline (NCH)Block 1 · Unit 4 Government Initiatives (4.5 NCH, 4.7 Grahak Suvidha Kendras)

Key points

  • National Consumer Helpline (NCH): run by the Department of Consumer Affairs; toll-free number 1915 (earlier 1800-11-4000), SMS, WhatsApp, the NCH app and the INGRAM portal; handles grievances before litigation.
  • Role and functions: advice and guidance to consumers, register grievances, forward them to companies under its convergence programme, follow up, help consumers approach consumer commissions (e-Daakhil portal) if unresolved, collect data on problem sectors.
  • Stages in resolving a consumer issue: grievance registered and docket number given → sent to the company (convergence partner) or authority → company responds within a set time → consumer informed → if unresolved, guidance on filing a complaint in the consumer commission → case closed with feedback.
  • Grahak Suvidha Kendras (consumer facilitation centres): set up by the Department of Consumer Affairs in states to give consumers face-to-face advice, help draft and file complaints, and guide them to the right forum — a one-stop help point, especially for those not online.
  • Importance: quick, free, pre-litigation settlement; reduces the load on consumer commissions; builds consumer confidence.

Worked example

Example: a complaint through the National Consumer Helpline

A customer's new washing machine is defective and the company ignores her emails. She calls 1915 (or uses the NCH app or WhatsApp) and gets a docket number. NCH forwards the grievance to the company, a convergence partner, which must respond within a set time. If it is not resolved, NCH guides her to file a case before the District Consumer Commission, online through e-Daakhil.

In her district, a Grahak Suvidha Kendra helps her draft the complaint face to face.
