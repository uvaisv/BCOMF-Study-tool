Media & Technology for NGOs

Critical Asked in 8 of 12 papers · avg 7 marks

Use of mobile phones, radio, social media and information technology by NGOs in communication, education and field work.

In your IGNOU bookUnit 10 IT and Web Application

Key points

  • Mobile phones: SMS and voice alerts, WhatsApp groups, apps for data collection, mobile banking for beneficiaries, health and farm advisories — cheap and far-reaching.
  • Radio: community radio reaches rural and non-literate audiences in local languages; used for education, awareness and dialogue.
  • Social media: builds visibility, mobilises volunteers and donors, runs campaigns and crowdfunding; risks — misinformation, privacy.
  • IT in the office: accounting and donor databases, email, document management, video meetings. IT in the field: mobile data collection, GIS mapping, digital training material, monitoring dashboards.

Worked example

Example: one campaign, several media

An NGO promoting vaccination sends voice messages in the local language to mothers' phones, runs a weekly community-radio programme with a doctor answering questions, posts short videos on social media to attract volunteers, and records each vaccination on a mobile data app that feeds a district dashboard.
