NGOs: Concept, Types & Role

High Asked in 7 of 12 papers · avg 6 marks

Meaning of NGO and why 'non-government', types of NGOs, how NGOs work, NGOs as change agents, and the role of donors.

In your IGNOU bookUnit 1 Concepts and Functions of NGOs

Key points

  • NGO: a voluntary, non-profit organisation formed by citizens, independent of government, working for social welfare or development. 'Non-government' stresses independence from the state even if it receives government grants.
  • Types: by orientation — charitable, service, participatory, empowerment; by level — community-based, city-wide, national, international; by function — operational and advocacy NGOs.
  • How NGOs work: identify needs → plan projects → mobilise funds and communities → implement → monitor and report → evaluate.
  • NGOs as change agents: raise awareness, reach the unreached, test new models, influence policy, build community capacity, respond quickly in crises (e.g. relief during the pandemic).
  • Role of donors: provide funds and expertise, set reporting standards; risk of donor-driven agendas.

Worked example

Examples: types of NGOs

A charitable NGO distributes food and blankets in winter. A service NGO runs a free clinic. A participatory NGO helps villagers build a check-dam with their own labour. An empowerment NGO trains women's groups to demand their rights from local government. An advocacy NGO campaigns for a change in law.

How one works: it surveys needs, designs a project, raises funds, implements with the community, monitors progress and reports to donors and the people it serves.

How to write the answer

Definition → types or roles as headings with Indian examples (e.g. SEWA, Pratham) → conclusion.
