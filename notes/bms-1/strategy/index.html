Strategic Planning, Vision & Mission

Critical Asked in 10 of 12 papers · avg 9 marks

Elements of a strategic plan, strategic framework, implementation, vision and mission statements, core values, organisational culture and social values in strategy.

In your IGNOU bookUnit 5 Elements of Strategy

Key points

  • Strategic planning: deciding where the NGO wants to be in the long term and how to get there.
  • Elements of a strategic plan: vision, mission, core values, environmental analysis (SWOT), strategic goals, objectives, strategies and programmes, resources and budget, implementation plan, monitoring and evaluation.
  • Strategic framework process: review mission → analyse environment and organisation → set strategic directions → choose strategies → plan actions → implement → monitor and revise.
  • Implementing a new plan effectively: leadership commitment, clear responsibilities, action plans and budgets, communication to staff and stakeholders, capacity building, regular review.
  • Vision: the long-term dream of what the NGO wants to see (e.g. 'a world without poverty'). Mission: what the NGO does, for whom and how. Good statements are short, clear, inspiring and realistic.
  • Core values and culture: shared beliefs (integrity, participation, equity) shape how people in the NGO behave — its organisational culture.
  • Social values (justice, equality, service) guide strategy and policy in NGOs and keep them close to the communities they serve.

Worked example

Example: vision, mission and a strategic goal

Vision: 'Every child in our district reads with understanding.'

Mission: 'We run community reading centres and train local volunteers to support children aged 6–12 in government schools.'

Strategic goal (from a SWOT): 'Open 50 reading centres in three years, funded by at least four donors' — with yearly targets, a budget and a review every six months.

How to write the answer

Meaning → elements as a numbered list → an NGO example → conclusion.
