Environmental Analysis for NGOs

Critical Asked in 9 of 12 papers · avg 8 marks

Mega and micro environment, general environment indicators, PEST/PESTLE analysis, opportunities and threats, frameworks and matrices, social change and technology.

In your IGNOU bookUnit 2 NGO Environment

Key points

  • Environment: the external forces affecting an NGO. Mega (macro) environment — broad national and global forces; micro environment — immediate stakeholders (beneficiaries, donors, government agencies, other NGOs, local community).
  • General environment indicators: population, literacy (including women's literacy), income and poverty levels, health indicators, employment, infrastructure, political stability, technology.
  • PESTLE: Political, Economic, Social, Technological, Legal and Environmental factors (PEST without the last two) — helps an NGO spot opportunities and threats.
  • Opportunities (favourable trends, e.g. new CSR funds) and threats (unfavourable trends, e.g. stricter foreign funding rules).
  • Frameworks: the opportunity matrix (attractiveness × probability of success) and the threat matrix (seriousness × probability of occurrence) rank opportunities and threats; SWOT combines them with strengths and weaknesses.
  • Social change with strategic implications: literacy, urbanisation, changing family structures, gender roles, technology use — each changes the needs NGOs serve.
  • Technology advances, for example in agriculture (drip irrigation, mobile advisories, improved seeds), open new areas of NGO work.

Worked example

Example: PESTLE and the opportunity–threat matrices

Political: a new state scheme for girls' education. Economic: new CSR funds from companies nearby. Social: rising mobile use among rural youth. Technological: cheap tablets. Legal: stricter FCRA rules on foreign funds. Environmental: frequent floods disrupt classes.

The CSR funds are high in attractiveness and probability → top opportunity. Stricter foreign-funding rules are high in seriousness and probability → top threat, so the NGO builds local funding.

How to write the answer

Meaning → framework (PESTLE or matrix) as a diagram or table → an example → conclusion.
