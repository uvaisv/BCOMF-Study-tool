Issues, Challenges & Voluntary Effort

Critical Asked in 8 of 12 papers · avg 7 marks

Problems and issues NGOs face, ethical challenges, issues in decision-making, sustainability, and promoting and strengthening voluntary effort.

In your IGNOU bookUnit 4 Problem Identification (4.2–4.4, 4.9)

Key points

  • Problems and challenges: dependence on donors and irregular funds, lack of trained staff and high turnover, weak management and governance, poor accountability, government regulation (FCRA), duplication of effort, limited reach.
  • Ethical challenges: misuse of funds, donor-driven agendas, conflict of interest, lack of transparency, exploiting beneficiaries' stories.
  • Issues in decision-making: whose needs come first, donor vs community priorities, limited information, participation of stakeholders, long-term vs short-term goals.
  • Sustainability: the NGO's ability to continue its work — financial (diverse income), organisational (systems, people) and programme sustainability (community ownership).
  • Promoting and strengthening voluntary effort: government support and a clear legal framework, training, networking among NGOs, CSR partnerships, recognising volunteers; in health — community health workers, awareness camps, partnerships with hospitals.
  • Tackling challenges: diversify funding, build capacity, adopt good governance and transparency, network and partner.

Worked example

Example: building sustainability

An NGO depends on one foreign donor for 80% of its funds; when that grant ends, staff leave and programmes stop. It responds by seeking CSR partners and local donations, training community leaders to run its centres (programme sustainability), setting up proper accounts and a governing board (organisational sustainability), and starting a small paid training service (financial sustainability).
