MSMEs, MSMED Act & Schemes

Critical Asked in 9 of 9 papers · avg 21 marks

Role of MSMEs in entrepreneurship and economic development, MSMED Act 2006, government initiatives, MUDRA Yojana and KVIC.

In your IGNOU bookBlock 4 · Unit 13 Entrepreneurship Development and MSMEs

Key points

  • Role of MSMEs: large-scale employment at low capital cost, balanced regional growth, use of local resources and skills, exports, ancillaries to large industries, a nursery for new entrepreneurs, promote an entrepreneurial culture.
  • MSMED Act, 2006: first law covering the whole MSME sector (including services) — classification of enterprises, National Board for MSME, advisory committee, measures for promotion and development, credit facilities, procurement preference, protection against delayed payments (buyers must pay within 45 days; compound interest at three times the RBI bank rate on delay), MSE Facilitation Councils for disputes, filing of the memorandum (now Udyam Registration).
  • Classification: the 2006 Act used investment only — manufacturing: micro up to ₹25 lakh, small up to ₹5 crore, medium up to ₹10 crore (plant and machinery); services: ₹10 lakh, ₹2 crore, ₹5 crore (equipment). Since 2020 it is a composite test of investment and turnover; the limits from 1 April 2025 are: micro — investment up to ₹2.5 crore and turnover up to ₹10 crore; small — ₹25 crore and ₹100 crore; medium — ₹125 crore and ₹500 crore.
  • Government initiatives: Udyam Registration, PMEGP, CGTMSE credit guarantee, MUDRA, Stand-Up India, ZED certification, TReDS for invoice discounting, the Samadhaan portal for delayed payments, GeM procurement, the Emergency Credit Line Guarantee Scheme (2020), Champions portal, the 25% public procurement policy for MSEs.
  • MUDRA Yojana (PMMY, 2015): collateral-free loans to non-farm micro enterprises in three categories — Shishu (up to ₹50,000), Kishore (₹50,000–₹5 lakh), Tarun (₹5–10 lakh; Tarun Plus up to ₹20 lakh from 2024) — through banks, NBFCs and MFIs.
  • KVIC (Khadi and Village Industries Commission, 1957): promotes khadi and village industries for rural employment; nodal agency for PMEGP.

Worked examples

Example: which category? (limits from 1 April 2025)

A garment unit with investment of ₹2 crore and turnover of ₹8 crore → micro (both within ₹2.5 crore and ₹10 crore).

The same investment with turnover of ₹15 crore → small, because turnover crosses the micro limit (the composite test needs both conditions).

Example: delayed payment and MUDRA

A micro supplier delivers goods to a large company, which pays after 90 days. The MSMED Act requires payment within 45 days; for the delay the buyer owes compound interest at three times the RBI bank rate. The supplier can file a claim on the Samadhaan portal to go to the MSE Facilitation Council.

A tailor who needs ₹3 lakh for machines gets a collateral-free Kishore loan under MUDRA.

How to write the answer

Meaning of MSME → classification table → role/provisions as headed points → schemes list → conclusion.

Common mistakes

  • Giving only the 2006 investment limits — add the current investment + turnover limits.
