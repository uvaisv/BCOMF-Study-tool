Entrepreneurship & Economic Development

Critical Asked in 7 of 9 papers · avg 13 marks

Concept of entrepreneurship, its role in economic and social development, factors affecting entrepreneurship development.

In your IGNOU bookBlock 1 · Unit 1 An Introduction to Entrepreneurship (1.2–1.5)

Key points

  • Entrepreneurship: the process of creating a new enterprise by identifying opportunities, mobilising resources and bearing risk — innovation, risk-bearing and organisation are its core.
  • Role in economic development: capital formation, employment generation, balanced regional development, higher per-capita income and standard of living, innovation and productivity, exports and foreign exchange, reduces concentration of economic power, links rural and urban markets.
  • Social development: solves social problems (social enterprises), empowers women and marginalised groups, creates local jobs — 'need of the hour' for India's youth population.
  • Factors affecting entrepreneurship development: economic (capital, labour, raw materials, market), social (family background, caste, education, social attitudes), psychological (need for achievement, risk-taking), political and legal (government policy, stability, regulation), infrastructure and technology, cultural values.
  • Pick one factor as most important if asked (e.g. government policy or access to finance) and justify with examples.

Worked example

Example: one dairy entrepreneur and economic development

A graduate starts a milk-chilling and packaging unit in her village with a bank loan (capital formation). She buys milk from 300 farmers at fair prices (higher rural incomes) and employs 20 people (employment). Packaged milk and curd reach the nearby town (linking rural and urban markets), and the village no longer loses its young people to city jobs (balanced regional development).

How to write the answer

Meaning → role as 8–10 headed points → factors in groups → conclusion.
