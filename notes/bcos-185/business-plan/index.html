Business Plan & DPR

Critical Asked in 9 of 9 papers · avg 19 marks

What a business plan is, why it matters, its elements, the Detailed Project Report (DPR), marketing plan and location/layout.

In your IGNOU bookBlock 2 · Unit 7 Business Plan PreparationBlock 2 · Unit 9 Business Plan ImplementationBlock 2 · Unit 6 Market Research (6.5 Marketing plan)

Key points

  • Business plan: a written document describing the business, its goals and how they will be achieved — a road map for the entrepreneur and a selling document for investors and banks.
  • Relevance: clarifies the idea, tests feasibility, raises finance, sets targets, reduces risk, guides operations and helps attract partners and employees.
  • Elements: executive summary; company description; industry and market analysis; product or service; marketing and sales plan; operations plan (location, layout, technology, suppliers); management and organisation; financial plan (project cost, means of finance, projected P&L, cash flow, break-even); risk analysis; appendices.
  • Detailed Project Report (DPR): the full technical and financial report submitted to banks and agencies — promoter profile, project description, market potential, technical aspects (process, machinery, capacity, raw material, utilities), location, manpower, project cost and means of finance, profitability and cash-flow projections, break-even, DSCR, implementation schedule, SWOT.
  • Marketing plan steps: situation analysis → set objectives → identify target market → decide the 4Ps → budget → implementation and control.
  • Location and layout: choose a site by nearness to market and raw materials, labour, transport, power, cost and government incentives; plan the layout (process, product, fixed-position) for smooth flow of material and people.

Worked examples

Example: the financial plan of a small printing unit

Project cost ₹20 lakh: machinery 12, renovation of the shed 3, preliminary expenses 1, working-capital margin 4.

Means of finance: promoter's contribution ₹5 lakh; bank term loan ₹15 lakh.

Debt service coverage ratio (DSCR) for year 1 = (profit after tax 3 + depreciation 1.5 + interest 1.5) ÷ (loan instalment 3 + interest 1.5) = 6 ÷ 4.5 = 1.33 — the unit earns 1.33 times what it must repay, which banks generally find acceptable.

Example: choosing location and layout

A cashew-processing unit is set up near the growing area (raw material) and a highway to the port (market and transport). Inside, machines are arranged in the order of the work — roasting, shelling, peeling, grading, packing — a product layout, so material moves in one direction without back-tracking.

How to write the answer

Meaning → relevance (5 points) → elements as headings, each with one example line (e.g. a tiffin service or a small bakery) → conclusion.

Common mistakes

  • Writing only the meaning — most marks are for the elements with examples.
