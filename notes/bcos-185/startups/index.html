Start-ups & Start-up India

Critical Asked in 8 of 9 papers · avg 14 marks

Concept of start-ups, challenges, Start-up India (2016) objectives and support measures, success stories.

In your IGNOU bookBlock 3 · Unit 10 Start-up InitiativesBlock 4 · Unit 15 Success Stories

Key points

  • Start-up (DPIIT definition): an entity incorporated for up to 10 years, turnover not above ₹100 crore in any year, working on innovation or a scalable business model with high potential for employment or wealth creation.
  • Challenges: funding, finding the right talent, regulatory compliance, market acceptance, competition from established firms, infrastructure, scaling, cash-flow management, high failure rate.
  • Start-up India (launched 16 January 2016): objectives — build a strong ecosystem for innovation, generate employment, promote entrepreneurship, especially among youth, women and in Tier-2/3 cities.
  • Support measures: self-certification under labour and environmental laws, a single-window Startup India Hub, fast-tracked patents with 80% fee rebate, Fund of Funds for Startups (₹10,000 crore via SIDBI), Credit Guarantee Scheme for Startups, income-tax holiday for 3 of 10 years (Sec 80-IAC), easier public procurement, faster exit, Startup India Seed Fund Scheme, incubators (Atal Innovation Mission).
  • Success stories: Zomato, Flipkart, Paytm, Nykaa (Falguni Nayar), OYO (Ritesh Agarwal), PhysicsWallah (Alakh Pandey), Zerodha (bootstrapped, no outside funding).
  • Other support in the book: the national incubation network and the Atal Innovation Mission (Atal Tinkering Labs and Atal Incubation Centres). Unit 15 tells success stories of first-generation entrepreneurs and small business owners.

Worked example

Example: is it a start-up under DPIIT rules?

An agri-tech company incorporated in 2019, with turnover of ₹40 crore, that uses sensors to advise farmers on irrigation → yes: under 10 years old, turnover within ₹100 crore, and working on innovation.

Once recognised, it can self-certify under certain labour and environmental laws, get an 80% rebate on patent fees, and apply for the income-tax holiday for 3 of its first 10 years (Sec 80-IAC).

How to write the answer

Meaning (DPIIT definition) → objectives → measures as headed points → one success story in 5–6 lines → conclusion.
