Business Ideas & Opportunities

Moderate Asked in 7 of 9 papers · avg 9 marks

Sources of business ideas, methods and techniques of idea generation, sources for identifying business opportunities.

In your IGNOU bookBlock 2 · Unit 5 Business Opportunity Identification and Selection

Key points

  • Sources of business ideas: consumers (complaints, needs), existing products and services, distribution channels, government (policies, patents, schemes), research and development, hobbies and personal experience, trade fairs and exhibitions, media and the internet, changes in technology and society.
  • Idea-generation techniques: brainstorming, focus groups, surveys, problem inventory analysis, checklist method, mind mapping, SCAMPER, attribute listing, the Delphi technique, reverse brainstorming, gap analysis.
  • Opportunity identification: from market gaps, changing customer needs, demographic and lifestyle trends, new technology, government policy changes (e.g. Digital India opening fintech), import substitution, export demand.
  • Idea vs opportunity: an idea is a thought; an opportunity is an idea with real market demand and viability — screen ideas by feasibility.

Worked examples

Example: from a complaint to an opportunity

Problem inventory: office workers complain their home-packed lunch is cold by noon. Idea: an insulated, electrically heated lunch box. Screening: is there demand? Market research shows working people will pay ₹1,200. The idea has become an opportunity.

Example: SCAMPER on an umbrella

Substitute (lighter fabric), Combine (umbrella + torch), Adapt (a wind-resistant design from car-wash brushes), Modify (a larger canopy for two people), Put to another use (a sunshade for vendors), Eliminate (the straps), Reverse (an inverted umbrella that folds inside-out so it stays dry).
