Creativity & Innovation

Moderate Asked in 5 of 9 papers · avg 7 marks

Creativity and innovation in entrepreneurship, how they relate, steps of the creative process, challenges.

In your IGNOU bookBlock 1 · Unit 4 Entrepreneurs Competencies (4.4–4.5)

Key points

  • Creativity: generating new and useful ideas. Innovation: turning a creative idea into a product, process or service that creates value in the market — 'they go hand in hand'.
  • Importance for entrepreneurship: finds new opportunities, competitive advantage, solves customer problems, improves productivity, drives growth and survival.
  • Creative process (Wallas): preparation → incubation → illumination (insight) → verification/evaluation → implementation — give one example for each step.
  • Types of innovation: product, process, marketing, organisational; incremental vs radical/disruptive.
  • Challenges: fear of failure, lack of resources and finance, resistance to change, rigid organisation, time pressure, lack of skills, IP protection issues.

Worked example

Example: Wallas's creative process

Preparation: an entrepreneur studies the problem of plastic plates at weddings — waste, cost, health.

Incubation: they stop thinking about it for a while.

Illumination: seeing fallen areca palm leaves in their village, they realise the leaves could be pressed into plates.

Verification: they test heat-pressing, strength and hygiene, and get feedback from caterers.

Implementation: they start a unit that buys leaves from farmers and sells compostable plates.

How to write the answer

Meaning of both → how they relate → process steps as a flow chart → challenges → conclusion.
