Entrepreneurship

BCOS-185 · Year 2 · 3 hours · 100 marks

8 questions, attempt any 5, each carries 20 marks. All theory. Q8 is always short notes (any 2 of 4). The same questions come back almost word for word — business plan, MSMEs, start-ups and sources of finance appear in nearly every paper.

Exam tips

  • The business-plan question appears in every paper — prepare it fully with examples.
  • MSMEs (role, MSMED Act 2006, government schemes, MUDRA) and Start-up India are the next safest questions.
  • Q8 short notes: pick the two you know best from ecosystem, feasibility analysis, MUDRA, start-ups and rural/social entrepreneurship.
  • Use Indian examples — Tata and Godrej for family business, Zomato or Nykaa for start-ups, Amul for social entrepreneurship.
