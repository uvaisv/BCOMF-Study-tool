Data Analysis

BECS-184 · Year 3 · 3 hours · 100 marks

Section A: answer 2 of 4 questions (20 marks each). Section B: answer 5 of 7 or 8 questions (12 marks each); the last question is three short notes of 4 marks. The same paper is set for BA, B.Com and B.Sc. Expect one or two short sums (correlation, mean and SD, histogram).

Exam tips

  • Composite index (steps, ranking method, PQLI, HDI) was asked in 7 of the 8 papers — prepare it first.
  • Central tendency, data collection tools, hypothesis testing, correlation and graphs come up in almost every paper.
  • In Section B write about 300 words per 12-mark answer; for short notes, about 100 words each with one example.
  • Draw diagrams (skewness, kurtosis, normal curve, histogram, ogive) wherever they fit — they earn marks.
  • Practise: correlation coefficient with its t-test, sample mean and SD, harmonic/geometric mean, percentile rank, and drawing a histogram on graph paper.
  • Show the working table (X, Y, X², Y², XY) — method marks are given even if arithmetic slips.
  • For the sample SD divide by n − 1 and say so.
