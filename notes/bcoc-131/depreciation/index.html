Depreciation

High Asked in 6 of 11 papers · avg 13 marks

Mostly a 20-mark theory question on meaning and methods; sometimes a Machinery Account numerical.

In your IGNOU bookBlock 3 · Unit 8 Depreciation

Key points

  • Depreciation is a permanent, gradual fall in the value of a fixed asset through use and passage of time; it is the expired cost charged to the Profit and Loss Account.
  • Without it, profit is overstated and the balance sheet does not show the true financial position.
  • Related terms: depletion (wasting assets such as mines), amortisation (intangible assets), obsolescence (becoming out of date).
  • The amount depends on the asset's cost, estimated useful life and estimated salvage (scrap) value.
  • Two ways of recording: credit depreciation directly to the Asset Account each year, or credit a Provision for Depreciation Account (transferred to the asset when it is sold or retired). The balance sheet shows the asset at depreciated value either way.
  • The book's eight methods: fixed instalment, diminishing balance, annuity, depreciation fund, insurance policy, revaluation, depletion and machine hour rate. The first two are the most common.
  • Fixed instalment charges the same amount each year; diminishing balance charges less each year. The book prefers diminishing balance because depreciation plus repairs stays roughly even over the asset's life.
  • A change of method with retrospective effect needs depreciation worked out both ways and the difference adjusted.

Worked examples

Example: the two common methods

Machine bought on 1 April for ₹90,000 plus installation ₹10,000 = cost ₹1,00,000. Scrap value ₹10,000, life 5 years.

Fixed instalment: (1,00,000 − 10,000) ÷ 5 = ₹18,000 every year.

Diminishing balance at 20%: year 1 ₹20,000, year 2 ₹16,000 (20% of 80,000), year 3 ₹12,800 (20% of 64,000) — less each year.

Part year: had it been bought on 1 October, the first year's charge would be for 6 months only (₹9,000 under fixed instalment).

Example: sale of the machine (fixed instalment)

Sold after 2 full years for ₹70,000. Book value = 1,00,000 − 2 × 18,000 = ₹64,000.

Profit on sale = 70,000 − 64,000 = ₹6,000, transferred to Profit and Loss.

Diagram

How to solve

  1. Machinery A/c: purchase cost + installation; depreciation each year (pro rata for part years).
  2. On sale: find book value on date of sale; profit/loss = sale price − book value; transfer to P&L.

Formulas

SLM: (Cost − Scrap) ÷ Life
WDV: Book value × Rate%

Common mistakes

  • Not charging depreciation for the part year before sale.
  • Leaving out installation charges from cost.
