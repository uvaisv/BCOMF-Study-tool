Joint Venture

Critical Asked in 10 of 11 papers · avg 19 marks

Recently as a full numerical (Memorandum JV + JV with co-venturer's account) and as theory on methods.

In your IGNOU bookBlock 5 · Unit 17 Joint Venture Accounts

Key points

  • A joint venture is a temporary partnership of two or more persons for a specific project; it ends automatically when the project is complete.
  • It differs from a partnership (temporary, one project, no firm name) and from a consignment (co-venturers are co-owners sharing profit, not principal and agent).
  • The book's four methods: (1) one co-venturer records everything; (2) every co-venturer records all transactions; (3) each records only his own transactions and a Memorandum Joint Venture Account is prepared at the end; (4) separate books for the venture.
  • Under methods 1 and 2 the Joint Venture Account works as the venture's profit and loss account; under method 3 each keeps a 'Joint Venture with …' personal account instead.
  • With separate books: Joint Venture Account, Joint Bank Account and the co-venturers' personal accounts — kept strictly on double entry.

Worked example

Example: A and B, profit shared 3 : 2, each keeps only his own transactions

A buys goods ₹50,000 and pays expenses ₹5,000. B pays carriage ₹3,000, sells all but a little stock for ₹80,000 and takes over the unsold stock at ₹4,000.

Memorandum JV A/c: debit 50,000 + 5,000 + 3,000 = ₹58,000; credit 80,000 + 4,000 = ₹84,000. Profit ₹26,000 → A ₹15,600, B ₹10,400.

In A's books, 'JV with B A/c': debit purchases 50,000 + expenses 5,000 + profit 15,600 = ₹70,600, no credits → B owes A ₹70,600.

Check from B's side: sales 80,000 + stock 4,000 − carriage 3,000 − profit 10,400 = ₹70,600. B pays A this amount to settle.

How to solve

  1. Memorandum JV Account (= a P&L of the venture): Debit all expenses and goods bought by all venturers; credit sales and stock/assets taken over. Balance = profit, shared in ratio.
  2. 'Joint Venture with X Account' in A's books: Debit A's own purchases/expenses and A's share of profit; credit A's own sales and goods A takes over. Balance = amount due to/from X, settled by cash.
  3. Separate books: Joint Bank A/c (contributions in, payments out), JV A/c (all expenses, sales, profit), Co-venturers' A/cs (contribution + profit share − withdrawals).

Formulas

Profit share = JV profit × agreed ratio

Common mistakes

  • Recording the other venturer's transactions in 'JV with X' account under the Memorandum method.
  • Forgetting stock or plant taken over by a venturer on the credit side.
