Nature, Scope & Users of Accounting

Moderate Asked in 8 of 11 papers · avg 7 marks

Mostly 10-mark theory, usually as Q1(a).

In your IGNOU bookBlock 1 · Unit 1 Nature and Scope of Accounting

Key points

  • Accounting is the process of identifying, measuring, recording, classifying, summarising, analysing, interpreting and communicating financial transactions and events.
  • Need: a business has too many transactions to remember, and both insiders and outsiders need systematic information about it.
  • Objectives: keep systematic records, find the profit or loss, show the financial position, and give information to interested parties for decisions.
  • Book-keeping is the record-keeping part of accounting and stops at classifying; accounting goes on to summarise, analyse and report. Accountancy is the body of knowledge; accounting is its practice.
  • Users: owners, management, lenders, creditors, investors, tax authorities, employees, customers and the public — each wants different information.
  • Branches: financial, cost and management accounting grew as business and management became more complex.
  • Qualitative characteristics of accounting information: understandability, relevance, reliability and comparability.
  • Bases of accounting: cash basis (record only when cash is received or paid) and accrual basis (record when earned or incurred) — the book calls accrual the more rational basis.
  • Limitations: records only money-measurable events, uses historical cost, involves estimates and personal judgement.

Worked examples

Example: what each user looks for in a shop's accounts

The owner: profit for the year. A bank asked for a loan: can the business pay interest and repay? A supplier: will its bills be paid on time? The tax department: taxable income. Employees: can the business pay salaries and bonus?

Example: cash basis vs accrual basis

Goods worth ₹20,000 are sold on credit in March and the money comes in April. Accrual basis records the sale in March, when it was earned; cash basis records it only in April, when cash is received — so March profit looks lower on a cash basis.

How to write the answer

Definition → objectives or users as headed points (one line each, what each user wants) → conclusion.
