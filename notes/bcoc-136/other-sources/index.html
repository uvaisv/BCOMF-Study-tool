Income from Other Sources

High Asked in 7 of 9 papers · avg 10 marks

Chargeable incomes, casual winnings, dividends, interest on securities, composite letting, bond washing, allowed deductions.

In your IGNOU bookBlock 4 · Unit 13 Income from Other Sources

Key points

  • Residual head: dividends, interest on securities and bank deposits, casual winnings, gifts above limits, composite letting of building with machinery, director's sitting fees, family pension, ground rent.
  • Winnings from lottery, races, card games: grossed up if received net of TDS (net ÷ 70%) and taxed at 30%; losses from such games cannot be set off.
  • Dividends: taxable in the shareholder's hands (from AY 2021-22); dividend from a co-operative society is taxable.
  • Deductions allowed: collection charges, interest on borrowed money, depreciation and repairs on let machinery/building, standard deduction on family pension.
  • Bond washing (Sec 94): selling securities just before interest is due and buying them back after — the interest is taxed in the seller's hands.
  • Exempt: share of HUF income, agricultural income, salary of an MLA is taxed under Other Sources (not Salaries).

Worked examples

Example: grossing up a lottery win

A person receives ₹70,000 from a lottery after TDS. Gross winning = 70,000 ÷ 0.70 = ₹1,00,000, taxed at 30% (₹30,000), against which the TDS of ₹30,000 is given credit. No expense or loss can be set against it.

Example: dividend and letting of machinery with a building

Dividend ₹50,000; interest paid on a loan taken to buy those shares ₹15,000. The interest deduction is capped at 20% of the dividend (₹10,000) → taxable dividend ₹40,000.

A factory building is let along with its machinery for ₹2,00,000 (inseparable letting); repairs ₹20,000 and depreciation ₹30,000 → income from other sources ₹1,50,000.

How to solve

  1. List each receipt: taxable/exempt with section → deduct allowed expenses → income from other sources.

Formulas

Gross winning = Net received ÷ 0.70

Common mistakes

  • Allowing expenses on lottery tickets against winnings.
