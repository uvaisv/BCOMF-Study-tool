Deductions (80C, 80D, 80G) & Total Income

Critical Asked in 8 of 9 papers · avg 19 marks

Computing total income across heads, and Chapter VI-A deductions — 80C, 80D, 80G, political donations.

In your IGNOU bookBlock 5 · Unit 15 Deductions from Gross Total IncomeBlock 5 · Unit 16 Assessment of Individuals

Key points

  • Compute income under each head, add (after clubbing and set-off) → GTI → less deductions → total income.
  • 80C: LIC premium, provident fund, PPF, NSC, ELSS, tuition fees, home-loan principal — up to the overall limit.
  • 80D: medical insurance premium for self/family and parents (higher limit for senior citizens); must not be paid in cash (except preventive health check-up).
  • 80G: donations to funds and charities — 100% or 50%, with or without a qualifying limit (e.g. PM National Relief Fund 100% without limit).
  • 80GGC: donations to political parties — 100%, not in cash.
  • Casual winnings, LTCG and STCG u/s 111A are taxed at special rates — Chapter VI-A deductions are not allowed against them.
  • Income from growing mushrooms is not agricultural income; share of profit from a firm is exempt.

Worked examples

Example: from heads of income to total income (old tax regime)

Salary (after deductions u/s 16) ₹8,00,000 + house property ₹41,000 + bank interest ₹20,000 = GTI ₹8,61,000.

Chapter VI-A deductions: 80C — PPF ₹1,00,000 + LIC premium ₹30,000 = ₹1,30,000 (within the ₹1.5 lakh limit); 80D — health insurance ₹20,000 paid by cheque; 80G — ₹5,000 to the PM National Relief Fund (100%, no limit).

Total deductions ₹1,55,000 → total income = ₹7,06,000.

Example: deductions do not reduce special-rate income

A person's GTI is ₹3,00,000, of which ₹2,00,000 is a lottery winning. 80C investments of ₹1,50,000 can be set only against the other ₹1,00,000. So total income is ₹2,00,000 (the lottery), taxed at 30%.

Diagram

How to solve

  1. Head-wise schedule: Salary, House Property, PGBP, Capital Gains, Other Sources → GTI.
  2. Less Chapter VI-A deductions (show each with its section) → total income.

Formulas

Total income = Σ heads − deductions

Common mistakes

  • Allowing 80C against capital gains or lottery income.
