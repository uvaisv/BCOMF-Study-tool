Assessment of Firms & Partners

Low Asked in 3 of 9 papers · avg 5 marks

Partnership deed, conditions u/s 184, working and non-working partners, interest and remuneration to partners.

In your IGNOU bookBlock 5 · Unit 17 Assessment of Firms

Key points

  • A firm is assessed as a firm (Sec 184) if there is a written partnership deed, a certified copy is filed with the first return, and partners' individual shares are specified.
  • Deed contents: names, business, capital, profit-sharing ratio, interest on capital, remuneration to working partners, duration.
  • Working partner: actively engaged in the business; non-working (sleeping) partner is not.
  • Sec 40(b): interest to partners allowed up to 12% simple p.a.; remuneration only to working partners within prescribed limits based on book profit.
  • Partner's share of profit from the firm is exempt (Sec 10(2A)); interest and remuneration are taxed as business income in the partner's hands.

Worked example

Example: interest to a partner under Section 40(b)

The deed allows 15% interest on capital; a partner's capital is ₹5,00,000, so the firm pays ₹75,000.

The firm may deduct only 12% = ₹60,000; ₹15,000 is disallowed and added back to the firm's income.

In the partner's hands, the ₹60,000 allowed to the firm is taxed as business income, while their share of the firm's profit is exempt u/s 10(2A).
