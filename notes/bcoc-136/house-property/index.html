Income from House Property

Critical Asked in 6 of 9 papers · avg 13 marks

Chargeability (Sec 22), annual value, let-out and self-occupied property, deductions u/s 24, interest on borrowed capital.

In your IGNOU bookBlock 3 · Unit 8 Income from House Property

Key points

  • Sec 22: annual value of buildings or land appurtenant, owned by the assessee, not used for own business or profession.
  • Gross annual value: expected rent (higher of municipal value and fair rent, but not more than standard rent) compared with actual rent received — the higher is GAV.
  • Net annual value = GAV − municipal taxes actually paid by the owner.
  • Self-occupied property: annual value nil (for one or two houses); interest deduction limited.
  • Deductions u/s 24: (a) standard deduction 30% of NAV; (b) interest on borrowed capital (pre-construction interest in 5 equal instalments).
  • Part let-out, part self-occupied, part used for own profession: split the property and compute each part separately; the professional-use part is not taxed under this head.

Worked examples

Example: income from a let-out house

Municipal value ₹1,20,000; fair rent ₹1,50,000; standard rent ₹1,40,000; actual rent ₹11,000 a month (₹1,32,000). Municipal tax paid by the owner ₹10,000; interest on a housing loan ₹50,000.

Expected rent = higher of municipal value and fair rent (₹1,50,000), capped at standard rent → ₹1,40,000.

GAV = higher of expected rent and actual rent = ₹1,40,000. NAV = 1,40,000 − 10,000 = ₹1,30,000.

Less 30% standard deduction ₹39,000 and interest ₹50,000 → income from house property = ₹41,000.

Example: pre-construction interest

Interest of ₹1,00,000 was paid on the loan before the house was completed. It is allowed in five equal instalments of ₹20,000, from the year construction is completed, on top of that year's own interest.

How to solve

  1. Expected rent = max(municipal value, fair rent), capped at standard rent.
  2. GAV = max(expected rent, actual rent).
  3. NAV = GAV − municipal tax paid by owner.
  4. Income = NAV − 30% − interest on loan.

Formulas

NAV = GAV − municipal tax
Income = NAV − 30% of NAV − interest u/s 24(b)

Common mistakes

  • Deducting municipal tax paid by the tenant.
