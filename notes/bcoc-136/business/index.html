Profits & Gains of Business or Profession

Moderate Asked in 6 of 9 papers · avg 7 marks

Conditions for chargeability, depreciation, Sec 43B, Sec 37(1) general deduction, inadmissible expenses, presumptive tax u/s 44AD.

In your IGNOU bookBlock 3 · Unit 9 Profits and Gains of Business or Profession-IBlock 3 · Unit 10 Profits and Gains of Business or Profession-IIBlock 3 · Unit 11 Profits and Gains of Business or Profession-III

Key points

  • Conditions: business or profession carried on by the assessee during the PY; income is taxed on accrual (mercantile) or cash basis.
  • Depreciation (Sec 32): block-of-assets, WDV method; asset used for less than 180 days in the year of purchase gets half the rate.
  • Sec 43B: certain expenses allowed only when actually paid (by the return due date) — taxes and duties, employer's PF/ESI contribution, bonus, commission, interest to banks/financial institutions, leave encashment.
  • Sec 37(1) general deduction: expenditure wholly and exclusively for business, not capital, not personal — e.g. advertisement, repairs, staff welfare, legal expenses.
  • Not allowed: income tax, personal expenses, penalties for breaking the law, capital expenditure, cash payments above the limit (Sec 40A(3)), interest/salary to partners beyond Sec 40(b) limits.
  • Sec 44AD presumptive taxation: eligible small businesses declare profit at 8% of turnover (6% for digital receipts), no books needed.
  • Deemed profits (Sec 41): a loss, expense or liability allowed earlier that is later recovered or remitted is taxed as business income in the year of recovery, even if the business has closed.
  • The book also covers compulsory books of account (Sec 44AA), tax audit (Sec 44AB) and presumptive (estimated) income schemes.

Worked examples

Example: depreciation on a block of assets

Plant and machinery block (15%): opening WDV ₹10,00,000. A new machine costing ₹4,00,000 is bought in October and used for fewer than 180 days in the year.

Depreciation = 15% × 10,00,000 + 7.5% (half rate) × 4,00,000 = 1,50,000 + 30,000 = ₹1,80,000.

Example: presumptive income u/s 44AD

Turnover ₹1.5 crore: ₹90 lakh received by bank or digital means, ₹60 lakh in cash.

Presumed profit = 6% × 90 lakh + 8% × 60 lakh = 5.4 + 4.8 = ₹10.2 lakh. No detailed books of account need to be kept.

Example: Section 43B

Bonus for the year is paid to staff after the year ends but before the return due date → allowed in the same year. If it is paid after the return due date, it is allowed only in the year it is actually paid.
