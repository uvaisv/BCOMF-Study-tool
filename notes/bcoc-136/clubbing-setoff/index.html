Clubbing of Income & Set-off of Losses

Critical Asked in 7 of 9 papers · avg 15 marks

When another person's income is clubbed; set-off and carry forward of business, capital, speculative and lottery losses.

In your IGNOU bookBlock 4 · Unit 14 Aggregation of Incomes (Clubbing) and Set-off of Losses

Key points

  • Clubbing (Secs 60–64): transfer of income without transfer of asset; revocable transfer of assets; remuneration of spouse from a concern where the individual has substantial interest (unless due to technical skill); assets transferred to spouse or son's wife without adequate consideration; income of a minor child (clubbed with the parent with the higher income; exemption up to ₹1,500 per child; not clubbed if from the child's own skill or if the child is disabled).
  • Set-off within the same head first, then against other heads.
  • Speculative business loss: only against speculative income; carried forward 4 years.
  • Short-term capital loss: against STCG or LTCG. Long-term capital loss: only against LTCG. Both carried forward 8 years.
  • Non-speculative business loss: against any head except salary; carried forward 8 years (only against business income).
  • Losses from lottery, card games, races: cannot be set off or carried forward.
  • House property loss: inter-head set-off limited; carried forward 8 years.

Worked examples

Example: clubbing

Mr A gifts ₹5 lakh to his wife, who puts it in a fixed deposit and earns ₹40,000 interest → clubbed with Mr A's income.

Their minor child earns ₹10,000 interest on a deposit made by the parents → ₹1,500 is exempt and ₹8,500 is clubbed with the parent whose income is higher. If the child had earned it by their own skill (say, as a child actor), it would not be clubbed.

Example: set-off and carry forward

Salary ₹6,00,000; income from house property ₹1,00,000; STCG ₹50,000; non-speculative business loss ₹3,00,000; long-term capital loss ₹40,000.

The business loss cannot be set off against salary, so it is set off against house property ₹1,00,000 and STCG ₹50,000; the remaining ₹1,50,000 is carried forward 8 years against business income only.

The long-term capital loss cannot be set off against STCG; it is carried forward 8 years, to be set off only against LTCG.
