Index Numbers

Critical Asked in 11 of 11 papers · avg 17 marks

Every session: Fisher's ideal index with time and factor reversal tests, or theory on types, tests and construction problems.

In your IGNOU bookBlock 6 · Unit 17 Index Numbers

Key points

  • Index number: measures relative change in price, quantity or value compared with a base year.
  • Types: price, quantity, value; simple and weighted (Laspeyres, Paasche, Fisher).
  • Problems in construction: purpose, choice of base year, selection of items, price quotations, choice of average, weights, formula.
  • Tests: unit test, time reversal test (P₀₁ × P₁₀ = 1), factor reversal test (P₀₁ × Q₀₁ = Σp₁q₁/Σp₀q₀), circular test.
  • Fisher's index satisfies both time reversal and factor reversal tests — hence 'ideal'.
  • Consumer price (cost of living) index: measures change in prices of goods consumed by a particular group; weights come from a family budget survey. Used for dearness allowance, wage settlements, price and tax policy.

Worked example

Example: Laspeyres, Paasche and Fisher

Item A: p₀ 10, q₀ 5, p₁ 12, q₁ 6. Item B: p₀ 8, q₀ 10, p₁ 10, q₁ 8. Item C: p₀ 5, q₀ 4, p₁ 6, q₁ 5.

Σp₀q₀ = 50 + 80 + 20 = 150; Σp₁q₀ = 60 + 100 + 24 = 184; Σp₀q₁ = 60 + 64 + 25 = 149; Σp₁q₁ = 72 + 80 + 30 = 182.

Laspeyres = 184/150 × 100 = 122.67. Paasche = 182/149 × 100 = 122.15.

Fisher = √(122.67 × 122.15) = 122.41 — prices rose about 22.4%.

Time reversal: P₀₁ × P₁₀ = √(184/150 × 182/149) × √(149/182 × 150/184) = 1 ✓.

How to solve

  1. Make a table: p₀q₀, p₁q₀, p₀q₁, p₁q₁ for each item; total each column.
  2. Laspeyres = Σp₁q₀/Σp₀q₀ × 100; Paasche = Σp₁q₁/Σp₀q₁ × 100.
  3. Fisher = √(L × P).
  4. Expenditure given instead of quantity: q = expenditure ÷ price.

Formulas

P₀₁ (Fisher) = √[(Σp₁q₀/Σp₀q₀) × (Σp₁q₁/Σp₀q₁)] × 100
TRT: P₀₁ × P₁₀ = 1
FRT: P₀₁ × Q₀₁ = Σp₁q₁ / Σp₀q₀

Common mistakes

  • Multiplying by 100 before checking the reversal tests (tests use ratios without ×100).
