E-Commerce

BCOS-184 · Year 2 · 3 hours · 100 marks

8–10 questions, attempt any 5, each carries 20 marks. All theory, with many 'distinguish between', 'comment on' and 'short notes' questions (4–5 parts of 4–5 marks). Use Indian examples: UPI, Flipkart, Digital India, GeM.

Exam tips

  • Cyber law (IT Act 2000 and the 2008 amendment, offences and penalties) and e-payments (NEFT, RTGS, IMPS, UPI, payment gateways) come up in every paper.
  • Website hosting and web server vs application server are asked almost every session — short, easy marks.
  • The 'distinguish between' questions reuse the same pairs: Internet vs WWW, Windows vs Linux, symmetric vs asymmetric encryption, digital vs electronic signature.
