E-Tailing & E-Services

Critical Asked in 8 of 9 papers · avg 19 marks

E-tailing and its models, 7 Cs of e-retail mix, e-services, e-publishing, online entertainment, e-learning and SWAYAM, OTT.

In your IGNOU bookBlock 5 · Unit 13 E-TailingBlock 5 · Unit 14 E-Services

Key points

  • E-tailing: selling goods to consumers online. Models: pure-play (Amazon), brick-and-click (Reliance Digital), direct sales by manufacturer (D2C), marketplace, subscription.
  • Advantages for retailers: wider reach, lower costs, 24×7, customer data. For buyers: convenience, variety, price comparison, home delivery. Disadvantages: no touch and feel, delivery delays, returns, fraud risk.
  • 7 Cs of the e-retail mix (Rayport and Jaworski's customer interface): context (site layout and design), content (products and information), community (reviews, user interaction), customisation (personalised pages), communication (messages to customers), connection (links to other sites), commerce (cart, payment, order tracking).
  • E-services: services delivered online (banking, ticketing, education, government). Advantages: speed, 24×7, lower cost, transparency.
  • E-publishing: publishing e-books, e-journals and news online; approaches — self-publishing, online platforms (Kindle), print-on-demand, open access.
  • Online entertainment and OTT: streaming platforms (Netflix, Hotstar) delivering content over the internet.
  • E-learning: learning over the internet. SWAYAM: Government of India's free MOOC platform with credit transfer.

Worked examples

Example: the 7 Cs for an online bookstore

Context: clean layout and quick search. Content: book details, sample chapters, prices. Community: reader reviews and ratings. Customisation: 'recommended for you' based on past buys. Communication: order and delivery messages, offers by e-mail. Connection: links from author pages and blogs. Commerce: cart, several payment options, order tracking and easy returns.

Example: e-services in daily life

Booking a train ticket online, attending a free SWAYAM course for credit, reading a newspaper's e-edition, and watching a film on an OTT platform are all services delivered over the internet — no counter, no queue, available 24×7.

How to write the answer

Meaning → models or advantages as headed points with examples → conclusion.
