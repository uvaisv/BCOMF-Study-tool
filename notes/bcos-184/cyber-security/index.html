Cyber Security & Threats

High Asked in 8 of 9 papers · avg 13 marks

Cyber security and its types and trends, threats and malware, spoofing, sniffing, phishing, internet vulnerabilities, cyber-physical systems.

In your IGNOU bookBlock 4 · Unit 10 Cyber SecurityBlock 4 · Unit 11 Cyber Security Measures

Key points

  • Cyber security: protecting systems, networks and data from digital attacks. Information security protects all information (paper and digital); cyber security focuses on cyberspace.
  • Types/categories: network, application, information/data, cloud, endpoint, operational security, disaster recovery, user education.
  • Trends: AI-driven attacks and defence, ransomware, IoT vulnerabilities, cloud security, zero-trust, mobile threats, remote-work risks.
  • Malware: virus, worm, Trojan, ransomware, spyware, adware, rootkit, keylogger.
  • Spoofing: disguising as a trusted source. Sniffing: capturing data travelling on a network. Phishing: fake messages to steal credentials. Spyware: secretly monitors the user.
  • Internet vulnerabilities: weak passwords, unpatched software, insecure Wi-Fi, social engineering, misconfiguration.
  • Cyber-physical systems: computing controlling physical processes (smart grids, medical devices) — need security across sensors, networks and controllers.
  • Impact on e-commerce: builds trust, protects payments and customer data, avoids losses and legal liability.

Worked examples

Example: a ransomware attack

A hospital employee opens an e-mail attachment that looks like an invoice. Ransomware encrypts patient records and demands payment. Defences that would have helped: user education (don't open unknown attachments), endpoint security (antivirus), regular offline backups (disaster recovery), and patched software.

Examples: spoofing, sniffing, phishing

Spoofing: an e-mail appears to come from the CEO's address, asking accounts to transfer money. Sniffing: someone on the same public café Wi-Fi captures unencrypted login data. Phishing: a fake 'refund' page collects card details.

How to write the answer

Meaning → types or threats as headed points with examples → prevention → conclusion.
