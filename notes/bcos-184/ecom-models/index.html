E-Commerce & Business Models

Critical Asked in 8 of 9 papers · avg 21 marks

B2B, B2C, C2C, D2C, P2P and other models; business and revenue models; e-auctions, drop shipping, labelling; how major players work.

In your IGNOU bookBlock 1 · Unit 2 E-Commerce Business ModelsBlock 1 · Unit 3 Technology used in E-Commerce (3.4 App vs web-based business)

Key points

  • Transaction models: B2B (IndiaMART), B2C (Amazon), C2C (OLX, eBay), C2B, B2G (GeM), D2C (brands selling directly — boAt), P2P (peer-to-peer lending or sharing).
  • Business model elements: value proposition, revenue model, market opportunity, competitive environment, competitive advantage, market strategy, organisational development, management team.
  • Revenue models: advertising, subscription, transaction fee/commission, sales, affiliate.
  • E-auction: online real-time bidding (forward auction to sell, reverse auction to buy from pre-qualified suppliers).
  • Drop shipping: seller does not stock goods; the supplier ships directly to the customer.
  • Private labelling (product made for and sold under the retailer's brand) vs white labelling (generic product rebranded by many sellers).
  • App-based vs web-based business: mobile-first, push notifications vs browser-based, wider reach.
  • How players work: Flipkart/Amazon (marketplace + logistics), BigBasket (online grocery with warehouses), Zomato (restaurant aggregator and delivery), BookMyShow (ticketing), FirstCry (baby products, online + stores), eBay (C2C auctions).

Worked examples

Examples: classify the model

A factory buying packaging from sellers on IndiaMART → B2B. A student selling an old cycle on OLX → C2C. A government office buying laptops on GeM → B2G. An earphone brand selling on its own website → D2C. A freelance designer quoting a price to a company for a logo → C2B.

Revenue models: a free video site earns from advertising; a streaming service from subscriptions; a marketplace from commission on each sale.

Example: drop shipping and private labels

A seller lists phone covers online but keeps no stock; when an order comes in, the supplier ships directly to the buyer and the seller keeps the margin — drop shipping.

A supermarket sells rice made for it under its own brand → private label. A generic power bank sold by many sellers, each under their own brand → white label.

How to write the answer

Meaning → models with one Indian example each (a table) → merits and limits → conclusion.
