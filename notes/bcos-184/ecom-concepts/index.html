E-Commerce: Meaning, Evolution & Impact

High Asked in 9 of 9 papers · avg 13 marks

Meaning, evolution, advantages and disadvantages, e-commerce vs traditional commerce and e-business, 24×7 service, impact of AI and IoT.

In your IGNOU bookBlock 1 · Unit 1 Introduction to E-commerceBlock 1 · Unit 3 Technology used in E-Commerce (3.2–3.3)

Key points

  • E-commerce: buying and selling goods and services over electronic networks. E-business is wider — all business processes online (supply chain, HR, CRM).
  • Evolution: EDI and EFT (1970s–80s) → WWW and early online stores (1990s) → dot-com boom → mobile and social commerce → AI-driven, omnichannel commerce.
  • Advantages: global reach, 24×7, lower costs, personalisation, easy comparison. Disadvantages: security and privacy concerns, no physical inspection, delivery and return problems, digital divide.
  • E-commerce vs traditional: online vs physical; 24×7 vs limited hours; global vs local; digital vs cash payments.
  • Architectural framework: network infrastructure, publishing and multimedia content, messaging, business services (security, payment), applications.
  • Emerging technologies: AI (recommendations, chatbots), IoT (smart devices ordering automatically), AR/VR, blockchain, big data.

Worked example

Example: a spice shop goes online

A family spice shop in Kerala sells only to walk-in customers from 9 to 8. It opens a web store and lists on a marketplace. Now it sells 24×7 across India and abroad, takes UPI and card payments, and sees which products sell best.

New problems follow: customers cannot smell the spices before buying, deliveries can be late, and returns and online fraud must be handled.

How to write the answer

Meaning → headed points (advantages vs disadvantages table) → conclusion.
