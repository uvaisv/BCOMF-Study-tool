E-Governance & Digital India

Critical Asked in 8 of 9 papers · avg 15 marks

E-governance vs e-government, Gartner's four-phase model, India's e-governance journey, Digital India and its nine pillars, e-NAM, GeM, MyGov.

In your IGNOU bookBlock 1 · Unit 4 Electronic Governance

Key points

  • E-government: using ICT to deliver government services. E-governance: wider — ICT for government, citizens and businesses to interact, making governance transparent and participative.
  • Gartner's model: Phase 1 information (publish information online), Phase 2 interaction (downloadable forms, e-mail), Phase 3 transaction (pay bills, file returns online), Phase 4 transformation (integrated, citizen-centric services).
  • India's journey: 1990s computerisation (NIC, railway reservation), 2006 National e-Governance Plan (Mission Mode Projects), 2015 Digital India, then Aadhaar, UPI, DigiLocker, UMANG.
  • Digital India (2015) aims: digital infrastructure, governance and services on demand, digital empowerment. Nine pillars: broadband highways, universal mobile access, public internet access, e-governance, e-Kranti, information for all, electronics manufacturing, IT for jobs, early harvest programmes.
  • e-NAM: online national agricultural market. GeM: Government e-Marketplace for public procurement. MyGov: citizen participation platform.
  • E-commerce (business transactions for profit) vs e-governance (government services for citizens).

Worked examples

Example: Gartner's four phases for paying property tax

Phase 1 (information): the municipality's website lists tax rates and due dates.

Phase 2 (interaction): citizens download the form and e-mail queries.

Phase 3 (transaction): citizens log in, see their bill and pay online, and get a receipt.

Phase 4 (transformation): one login links property tax, water bill and building permits, and receipts are saved automatically in DigiLocker.

Examples: e-governance platforms

A farmer sells produce to a buyer in another state through e-NAM. A government department buys office chairs through GeM. Citizens comment on a draft policy on MyGov.

How to write the answer

Meaning → phases or pillars as a numbered list → examples → conclusion.
