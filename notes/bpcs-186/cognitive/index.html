Cognitive Restructuring (ABCDE) & Problem Solving

Critical Asked in 7 of 9 papers · avg 8 marks

Cognitive restructuring and the ABCDE technique of REBT, and problem solving — steps and strategies.

In your IGNOU bookBlock 2 · Unit 7 Stress Management Techniques-II (7.2 Cognitive restructuring)Block 2 · Unit 8 Stress Management Techniques-III (8.3 Problem solving)

Key points

  • Cognitive restructuring: identifying and changing irrational or negative thoughts that cause stress.
  • ABCDE technique (Albert Ellis, REBT): A — activating event; B — belief about it; C — emotional and behavioural consequence; D — disputing the irrational belief; E — effective new belief and its effect.
  • Example: failing a test (A) → 'I am a failure' (B) → feeling hopeless and skipping classes (C) → 'one test does not define me; I can prepare better' (D) → feeling motivated and studying (E).
  • Problem solving steps: define the problem → gather information → generate alternatives → evaluate them → choose the best → implement → review the result.
  • Strategies: trial and error, algorithms, heuristics (rules of thumb), means–end analysis, working backwards, brainstorming.

Worked example

Example: ABCDE at work

A: the manager returns a report with many corrections. B: 'I'm useless; I'll be fired.' C: anxiety and avoiding the manager. D: 'Corrections are normal; my last two reports were praised; one report doesn't decide my job.' E: calmer, the employee asks the manager how to improve and fixes the report.
