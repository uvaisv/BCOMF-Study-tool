Interpersonal Skills: Communication, Self-awareness, EI

High Asked in 6 of 9 papers · avg 6 marks

Effective communication, self-awareness, emotional intelligence and conflict resolution as interpersonal skills for managing stress.

In your IGNOU bookBlock 2 · Unit 8 Stress Management Techniques-III (8.2 Interpersonal skills)

Key points

  • Effective communication: clear messages, active listening, feedback, appropriate non-verbal signals, empathy and assertiveness — reduces misunderstandings and conflict.
  • Self-awareness: knowing one's own emotions, strengths, weaknesses and values (Johari window) — the base for managing oneself and relationships.
  • Emotional intelligence (Goleman; Salovey and Mayer): self-awareness, self-regulation, motivation, empathy and social skills — helps handle pressure and people.
  • Conflict resolution: styles — competing, collaborating, compromising, avoiding, accommodating (Thomas–Kilmann); steps — understand the issue, listen to both sides, find common ground, agree a solution, follow up.
  • The book's conflict-resolution strategies: avoiding (withdraw), competing (assertive, not cooperative — win–lose), accommodating (cooperative, not assertive — lose–win), collaborating (both — win–win) and compromising.

Worked example

Example: conflict styles in a group project

Two members disagree on the topic. Competing: one insists and overrules. Accommodating: one gives in to keep peace. Avoiding: they stop talking about it. Compromising: each topic gets half the presentation. Collaborating: they combine both ideas into a stronger topic — best when the issue and the relationship both matter.

Emotional intelligence helps: noticing one's own anger (self-awareness), pausing before replying (self-regulation) and understanding the other's worry (empathy).
