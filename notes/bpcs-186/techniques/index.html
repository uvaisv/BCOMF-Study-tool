Stress Management Techniques: Relaxation, Meditation, Yoga

Critical Asked in 9 of 9 papers · avg 10 marks

Relaxation techniques, meditation, yoga and asanas, mindfulness and biofeedback.

In your IGNOU bookBlock 2 · Unit 6 Stress Management Techniques-I

Key points

  • Relaxation techniques: deep (diaphragmatic) breathing, progressive muscle relaxation (Jacobson — tense and relax muscle groups), autogenic training, guided imagery.
  • Meditation: training attention to calm the mind — concentrative (focus on breath, mantra), mindfulness, transcendental. Benefits: lower BP and heart rate, less anxiety, better focus.
  • Yoga: asanas (postures), pranayama (breath control) and dhyana (meditation) — improves flexibility, calms the nervous system.
  • Types of asanas: standing (Tadasana), sitting (Padmasana, Vajrasana), lying on the back (Shavasana), lying on the stomach (Bhujangasana), balancing and inverted poses.
  • Mindfulness: paying attention to the present moment without judging (Kabat-Zinn's mindfulness-based stress reduction).
  • Biofeedback: electronic monitoring of heart rate, muscle tension or skin temperature so a person learns to control these responses.
  • Asanas described in the book: Padmasana (lotus), Sukhasana (easy cross-legged), Siddhasana, Vajrasana and Trikonasana.

Worked example

Example: a 15-minute daily routine

Five minutes of deep diaphragmatic breathing; progressive muscle relaxation — tense the shoulders for 5 seconds, release, move to the arms, legs and face; then five minutes of mindfulness, noticing the breath without judging thoughts; end in Shavasana. Done daily before study, it lowers heart rate and anxiety.
