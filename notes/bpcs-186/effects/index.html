Effects of Stress: Health, Performance & Relationships

Critical Asked in 9 of 9 papers · avg 10 marks

Effects of stress on physical and mental health, on performance and productivity, and on relationships.

In your IGNOU bookBlock 1 · Unit 4 Effects of Stress

Key points

  • Health: heart disease and high blood pressure, weaker immunity, headaches, digestive problems, sleep disorders; anxiety, depression and burnout.
  • Performance (Yerkes–Dodson law): performance rises with arousal up to an optimum, then falls — moderate stress helps, too much hurts.
  • Productivity: high stress brings errors, accidents, absenteeism, low morale, turnover and poor decisions.
  • Relationships: irritability, conflict, poor communication, withdrawal, less time and patience for family and friends; stress spreads to others.
  • Managing the effects: early recognition, healthy lifestyle, coping skills, support at home and work.

Worked example

Example: the Yerkes–Dodson curve in a call centre

With no targets, agents are bored and slow (low arousal). With a reasonable daily target and support, they are alert and perform best (optimum). When targets are doubled and leave is cancelled, errors, absenteeism and resignations rise (too much arousal) — and irritability spills over into their family lives.
