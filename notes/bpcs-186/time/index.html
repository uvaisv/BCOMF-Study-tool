Time Management

Critical Asked in 6 of 9 papers · avg 7 marks

Effective and systematic time management, the time management matrix, and techniques of time management.

In your IGNOU bookBlock 2 · Unit 7 Stress Management Techniques-II (7.3 Time management)

Key points

  • Time management: planning and controlling how time is spent on activities to work effectively and reduce stress.
  • Time management matrix (Covey, from Eisenhower): Quadrant I — urgent and important (do now); II — important, not urgent (plan; the key quadrant); III — urgent, not important (delegate); IV — neither (eliminate).
  • Techniques: set SMART goals; prioritise (ABC or matrix); to-do lists; plan and schedule (time blocking); avoid procrastination; delegate; the 80/20 (Pareto) rule; handle interruptions; learn to say no; review daily.
  • Benefits: less stress, higher productivity, better balance between work and life.
  • The book's worked method: list all tasks, mark each A (must do), B (should do) or C (could do), then number them within each group (A1, A2…) and work in that order.

Worked example

Example: Covey's matrix for a student's week

I (urgent, important): assignment due tomorrow → do now.

II (important, not urgent): revising for exams a month away, exercise → schedule it; this is where planning pays.

III (urgent, not important): most phone calls and group-chat replies → keep short or delegate.

IV (neither): endless scrolling → cut.

The book's ABC method: A1 finish assignment, A2 revise unit 3, B1 buy stationery, C1 sort photos.

How to write the answer

Meaning → matrix as a 2×2 diagram → techniques as headings → conclusion.
