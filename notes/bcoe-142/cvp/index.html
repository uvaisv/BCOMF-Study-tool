Cost–Volume–Profit & Break-Even Analysis

Critical Asked in 9 of 9 papers · avg 16 marks

Contribution, P/V ratio, break-even point, margin of safety, angle of incidence, sales for a target profit and the effect of changes in price and costs.

In your IGNOU bookBlock 4 · Unit 14 Cost Volume Profit Analysis

Key points

  • CVP analysis studies how costs, volume and profit are related — it answers questions like 'what sales are needed to break even or to earn ₹X profit?'.
  • Contribution = sales − variable cost; it covers fixed cost first, then gives profit.
  • P/V ratio = contribution ÷ sales; a higher ratio means profit grows faster with sales.
  • Break-even point: sales at which there is no profit or loss. Margin of safety: sales above break-even — a measure of safety.
  • Angle of incidence: the angle at which the sales line cuts the total cost line on a break-even chart; a large angle shows high profit potential.
  • Uses: pricing, planning profit, choosing products, make or buy, effect of cost changes. Assumptions: costs split into fixed and variable, constant price and efficiency, one product or constant mix.

Worked examples

Example: P/V ratio, break-even and margin of safety

Sales ₹10,00,000; variable cost ₹6,00,000; fixed cost ₹3,00,000.

Contribution ₹4,00,000; P/V ratio 40%; profit ₹1,00,000.

BEP = 3,00,000 ÷ 40% = ₹7,50,000. Margin of safety = 10,00,000 − 7,50,000 = ₹2,50,000 (check: profit ÷ P/V = 1,00,000 ÷ 40%).

Sales needed for a profit of ₹2,00,000 = (3,00,000 + 2,00,000) ÷ 40% = ₹12,50,000.

Example: two years' figures given

Year 1: sales ₹8,00,000, profit ₹50,000. Year 2: sales ₹10,00,000, profit ₹1,00,000.

P/V ratio = change in profit ÷ change in sales = 50,000 ÷ 2,00,000 = 25%.

Fixed cost = contribution − profit = 25% × 8,00,000 − 50,000 = ₹1,50,000. BEP = 1,50,000 ÷ 25% = ₹6,00,000.

Diagram

How to solve

  1. Find contribution per unit (or total) and the P/V ratio.
  2. If two years' figures are given: P/V ratio = change in profit ÷ change in sales; then fixed cost = contribution − profit.
  3. BEP = fixed cost ÷ P/V ratio (₹) or ÷ contribution per unit (units).
  4. Sales for target profit = (fixed cost + profit) ÷ P/V ratio.
  5. For a change in price or cost, recompute contribution and P/V ratio, then the new BEP.

Formulas

P/V ratio = Contribution ÷ Sales × 100 = Change in profit ÷ Change in sales × 100
BEP (₹) = Fixed cost ÷ P/V ratio
BEP (units) = Fixed cost ÷ Contribution per unit
Margin of safety = Actual sales − BEP sales = Profit ÷ P/V ratio
Required sales = (Fixed cost + Desired profit) ÷ P/V ratio

How to write the answer

Write the formula, substitute, answer with units (₹ or units) — one line per part.

Common mistakes

  • Mixing P/V ratio as a percentage and as a decimal in the same calculation.
