Management Accounting

BCOE-142 · Year 3 · 3 hours · 100 marks

Section A: answer 2 of 3 (theory; Q3 is short notes, any 4 of 6). Section B: answer 3 of 5 or 6 — mostly sums on ratios, budgets, variances, CVP and marginal costing. Each question 20 marks.

Exam tips

  • Section A: Q3 short notes is usually the easiest 20 marks — you choose 4 of 6.
  • Section B: budgets, variances and CVP sums appear in almost every paper; marginal-costing decisions (make or buy, sales mix) are frequent.
  • Many Section B questions are half theory, half sum — pick those if your numericals are weaker.
  • Write each formula before substituting.
  • Mark every variance F or A and check that the parts add up to the total.
  • Show fixed and variable costs separately in budgets and marginal cost statements.
