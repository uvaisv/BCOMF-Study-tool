Ratio Analysis

High Asked in 8 of 9 papers · avg 11 marks

Liquidity, activity, solvency and profitability ratios, the DuPont model, working back figures from given ratios, and limitations of ratio analysis.

In your IGNOU bookBlock 1 · Unit 4 Techniques of Financial Analysis (Ratio analysis)

Key points

  • Liquidity ratios: current ratio (ideal about 2:1), quick or acid-test ratio (ideal about 1:1).
  • Activity (turnover) ratios: inventory turnover, debtors turnover and average collection period, creditors turnover and average payment period, working capital turnover.
  • Solvency ratios: debt–equity, capital gearing, interest coverage.
  • Profitability ratios: gross and net profit ratios, operating ratio, return on capital employed.
  • DuPont model: return on equity = net profit margin × asset turnover × equity multiplier — shows what drives returns.
  • Limitations: based on historical and accounting figures, affected by window dressing and inflation, no standard ratio for all firms, ignores qualitative factors.

Worked examples

Example: liquidity ratios

Current assets ₹4,00,000 (including stock ₹1,50,000 and prepaid expenses ₹10,000); current liabilities ₹2,00,000.

Current ratio = 4,00,000 ÷ 2,00,000 = 2 : 1. Quick ratio = (4,00,000 − 1,60,000) ÷ 2,00,000 = 1.2 : 1 — both are comfortable.

Example: working backwards from ratios

Sales ₹12,00,000; gross profit 25% → cost of goods sold ₹9,00,000. Stock turnover 6 times → average stock = 9,00,000 ÷ 6 = ₹1,50,000. If closing stock is ₹20,000 more than opening: opening ₹1,40,000, closing ₹1,60,000.

Debtors turnover 6 times → average collection period = 365 ÷ 6 ≈ 61 days.

DuPont: net profit margin 10% × asset turnover 2 × equity multiplier 1.5 → ROE = 30%.

How to solve

  1. Write down all given ratios and figures.
  2. Work back step by step (e.g. sales → gross profit → cost of goods sold → average stock from the stock turnover ratio).
  3. Use opening/closing relations (closing stock = opening + ₹X) to split average stock.
  4. Compute each asked ratio and comment.

Formulas

Current ratio = Current assets ÷ Current liabilities
Quick ratio = (Current assets − Inventory − Prepaid expenses) ÷ Current liabilities
Inventory turnover = Cost of goods sold ÷ Average inventory
Average collection period = 365 ÷ Debtors turnover
Interest coverage = EBIT ÷ Interest
Debt–equity = Long-term debt ÷ Shareholders' funds
Capital gearing = (Preference capital + Long-term loans) ÷ Equity shareholders' funds
Operating ratio = (Cost of goods sold + Operating expenses) ÷ Net sales × 100
ROE (DuPont) = Net profit margin × Asset turnover × Equity multiplier

How to write the answer

Formula → substitution → answer → one-line comment on what the ratio means.
