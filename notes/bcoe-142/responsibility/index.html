Responsibility Accounting & Performance

Moderate Asked in 7 of 9 papers · avg 8 marks

Responsibility accounting, cost, revenue, profit and investment centres, how the centre type affects budgeting and reporting, performance measurement, ROI and residual income.

In your IGNOU bookBlock 5 · Unit 17 Responsibility Accounting

Key points

  • Responsibility accounting: organising accounting around managers' responsibilities — each manager is judged on costs and revenues they control.
  • Centres: cost centre (responsible for costs), revenue centre (revenues), profit centre (both), investment centre (profit and capital invested).
  • Differs from conventional cost accounting: organised by people and responsibility, not products; only controllable items; used for performance evaluation rather than just product costing.
  • Choice of centre: decides what is budgeted and reported — cost budgets and variances for cost centres; sales targets for revenue centres; profit statements for profit centres; ROI and residual income for investment centres.
  • Performance measurement: budget vs actual, variances, ROI, residual income, EVA; it motivates managers and fixes accountability.
  • Residual income (RI) = profit − (investment × cost of capital). Unlike ROI, it encourages any project earning more than the cost of capital.

Worked example

Example: ROI vs residual income

Division X: profit ₹3,00,000, investment ₹15,00,000 → ROI 20%. Division Y: profit ₹1,50,000, investment ₹5,00,000 → ROI 30%. Cost of capital 15%.

RI: X = 3,00,000 − 2,25,000 = ₹75,000; Y = 1,50,000 − 75,000 = ₹75,000.

Y is offered a project: investment ₹2,00,000, profit ₹50,000 (25%). It would cut Y's ROI to 2,00,000 ÷ 7,00,000 = 28.6%, so a manager judged on ROI may reject it. But it adds 50,000 − 30,000 = ₹20,000 to RI, and it is good for the company because 25% is above the 15% cost of capital.

How to solve

  1. ROI = profit ÷ investment × 100 for each division.
  2. Charge on capital = investment × cost of capital; RI = profit − charge.
  3. Compare divisions on both measures and comment.

Formulas

ROI = Profit ÷ Investment × 100
Residual income = Profit − (Investment × Cost of capital)

How to write the answer

Definition → types of centres (table) → importance → conclusion; for divisions, compute ROI and RI and rank them.
