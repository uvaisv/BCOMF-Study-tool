HRA, Social & Environmental Accounting

Moderate Asked in 8 of 9 papers · avg 6 marks

Human resource accounting, social accounting and social audit, environmental accounting and inflation accounting.

In your IGNOU bookBlock 5 · Unit 18 Contemporary Issues in Management Accounting-I (18.3–18.7)

Key points

  • Human resource accounting (HRA): measuring and reporting the value of people in an organisation. Methods: historical cost, replacement cost, opportunity cost, present value of future earnings (Lev and Schwartz).
  • HRA suits people-intensive industries — IT, consulting, banking, education, healthcare. In India, companies such as BHEL and Infosys have reported it; it is relevant but voluntary.
  • Social accounting: measuring and reporting a firm's social costs and benefits. Social audit: steps — set objectives, identify stakeholders, collect data, assess performance, report, follow up; linked with CSR (Sec 135) and business responsibility reporting.
  • Environmental accounting: recording environmental costs, liabilities and benefits (pollution control, waste, resource use).
  • Inflation accounting: adjusting accounts for changing prices — current purchasing power (CPP) and current cost accounting (CCA) methods.
  • The book also covers international accounting and IT developments in accounting.

Worked examples

Example: valuing an employee (Lev and Schwartz)

An employee will earn ₹6 lakh a year for the 3 years left before retirement; discount rate 10%.

Value = 6 × (1/1.1 + 1/1.1² + 1/1.1³) = 6 × 2.4869 = ₹14.92 lakh — the present value of future earnings used as the value of this human asset.

Example: inflation and environmental accounting

A machine bought for ₹10 lakh when the price index was 100 is shown at ₹10 lakh at cost; with the index now at 150, current purchasing power accounting restates it at ₹15 lakh, so depreciation and profit are not overstated in real terms.

A factory that spends on effluent treatment and pays a pollution fine records both separately as environmental costs, so management can see the cost of its environmental impact.
