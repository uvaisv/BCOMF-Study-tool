Product Life Cycle

Critical Asked in 7 of 9 papers · avg 12 marks

Stages of the PLC and marketing strategies at each stage.

In your IGNOU bookBlock 2 · Unit 6 New Product Development and Product Life Cycle (6.5–6.6)

Key points

  • PLC: stages a product passes through in the market — introduction, growth, maturity, decline.
  • Introduction: low sales, high costs, losses; strategy — create awareness, trial offers, skimming or penetration pricing, limited distribution.
  • Growth: sales rise fast, competitors enter, profits rise; strategy — improve quality, add features, enter new segments, widen distribution, brand-preference advertising.
  • Maturity: sales peak and slow, intense competition; strategy — modify market, product and marketing mix, price cuts, promotions.
  • Decline: sales and profits fall; strategy — cut costs, harvest, reposition or withdraw.
  • Examples: feature phones (decline), electric vehicles (growth), toothpaste (maturity).

Worked example

Example: a smartwatch brand through the life cycle

Introduction: few buyers, heavy spending on awareness and demo units; sold in selected electronics stores and online; losses.

Growth: sales rise sharply and rivals enter; the brand adds health features, cuts prices a little and widens distribution.

Maturity: sales level off with many competitors; the brand offers new straps, trade-in offers and targets older users.

Decline: phones take over many functions and sales fall; the brand cuts costs, stops weak models, or repositions as a fitness band.

How to write the answer
