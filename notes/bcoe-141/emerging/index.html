Digital, Green, Social & Other New Marketing

High Asked in 7 of 9 papers · avg 10 marks

Digital marketing and its tools, e-retailing, green, social, relationship, experiential and direct marketing.

In your IGNOU bookBlock 6 · Unit 19 Emerging Issues in Marketing-IBlock 6 · Unit 20 Emerging Issues in Marketing-II

Key points

  • Digital marketing: promoting through digital channels. Tools: SEO, SEM/pay-per-click, social media, content, e-mail, affiliate and influencer marketing, mobile apps. Advantages: low cost, targeting, measurable, two-way. Limits: clutter, privacy concerns, needs skills, digital divide.
  • E-retailing: selling to consumers online (Amazon, Flipkart).
  • Green marketing: promoting environmentally safe products and practices (eco-labels, recyclable packaging).
  • Social marketing: using marketing to promote social good (Swachh Bharat, anti-smoking).
  • Relationship marketing: building long-term customer relationships (loyalty programmes, CRM).
  • Experiential marketing: engaging customers through memorable brand experiences (product trials, pop-up events).
  • Consumerism (from the 1960s): the movement to protect consumers' rights against unfair business practices.
  • Other new approaches in the book: face-to-face, internal (marketing to one's own employees), location-based, and augmented and virtual reality marketing.

Worked examples

Example: a home bakery goes digital

It posts cake reels on Instagram (social media and content marketing), runs small ads targeted within 5 km (paid, measurable targeting), appears in 'bakery near me' searches (SEO), and sends a WhatsApp offer on each customer's birthday (relationship marketing).

It switches to paper boxes and advertises 'plastic-free packing' (green marketing).

Example: social marketing

A state health department uses posters, radio jingles and influencers to persuade people to wash their hands and get vaccinated. It uses marketing tools, but the 'product' is a behaviour and the aim is public good, not profit.

How to write the answer

Meaning → features or tools as headed points → Indian example → merits and limits → conclusion.
