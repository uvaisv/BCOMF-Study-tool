Services Marketing

High Asked in 7 of 9 papers · avg 10 marks

Goods vs services, characteristics of services, challenges in marketing services.

In your IGNOU bookBlock 6 · Unit 17 Services Marketing

Key points

  • Service: an intangible activity offered for sale (banking, hotels, education).
  • Characteristics: intangibility, inseparability (produced and consumed together), variability/heterogeneity, perishability, no ownership.
  • Goods vs services: tangible vs intangible, can be stored vs perishable, standardised vs variable, ownership transfers vs does not.
  • Challenges: showing quality before purchase, keeping service consistent, matching supply with fluctuating demand, pricing intangibles, training staff, managing customer participation.
  • Extended mix (7 Ps): product, price, place, promotion + people, process, physical evidence.

Worked examples

Example: service characteristics in a hotel

Intangible: a guest cannot try the stay before paying — photos and reviews act as evidence. Inseparable: the service is produced while the guest is there. Variable: a rude receptionist on one shift changes the experience. Perishable: an empty room tonight is revenue lost for ever — hence cheaper rates on weekdays. No ownership: the guest uses the room but owns nothing.

Example: the 7 Ps of a coaching centre

Product: exam-preparation courses. Price: fees with a scholarship test. Place: centres near colleges plus online classes. Promotion: results posters and free demo classes. People: qualified, friendly teachers. Process: admission, timetable and doubt-clearing sessions. Physical evidence: clean classrooms, study material, a website with toppers' results.

How to write the answer

Meaning → characteristics with examples → challenges → 7 Ps → conclusion.
