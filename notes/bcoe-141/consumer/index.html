Consumer Behaviour & Buying Process

High Asked in 5 of 9 papers · avg 11 marks

Factors influencing consumer behaviour and the stages of the buying decision (e.g. buying a washing machine or TV).

In your IGNOU bookBlock 1 · Unit 4 Consumer Behaviour

Key points

  • Factors: cultural (culture, sub-culture, social class), social (reference groups, family, roles and status), personal (age, life-cycle stage, occupation, income, lifestyle, personality), psychological (motivation, perception, learning, beliefs and attitudes).
  • Buying decision process: need recognition → information search → evaluation of alternatives → purchase decision → post-purchase behaviour.
  • Example (washing machine): the old one breaks (need) → asks friends, reads reviews, visits stores (search) → compares capacity, brand, price, warranty (evaluation) → buys a front-load model during a sale (purchase) → satisfaction or cognitive dissonance (post-purchase).
  • Buyer vs user: the person who buys may not be the one who uses the product; marketers must reach both.

Worked example

Example: a college student buys a scooter

Cultural: the family belongs to a middle-class background where a two-wheeler is expected at college age. Social: friends recommend a brand; parents pay, so they are key decision-makers. Personal: a limited budget and a student lifestyle favour fuel efficiency. Psychological: an ad showing the scooter as 'smart and safe' shapes perception.

Process: need (daily college travel) → search (friends, reviews, showrooms) → evaluation (mileage, price, looks, resale) → purchase on a festival offer → post-purchase (joins the owners' group; any doubts are calmed by a free service).

How to write the answer

Common mistakes

  • Describing stages without applying them to the product the question names.
