New Product Development

Moderate Asked in 6 of 9 papers · avg 10 marks

Concept and steps of NPD.

In your IGNOU bookBlock 2 · Unit 6 New Product Development and Product Life Cycle (6.2–6.4)

Key points

  • New product: original, improved or modified product, or a new brand.
  • Steps: idea generation → idea screening → concept development and testing → marketing strategy → business analysis → product development → test marketing → commercialisation.
  • Why it is complex: high cost, high failure rate, long time, changing customer needs, competition.
  • Example: Maggi Oats Noodles or a new flavour — screened, test-marketed in a few cities before national launch.
  • New products fail because of poor market research, weak product, wrong timing or price, weak promotion, or strong competition.

Worked example

Example: launching a millet biscuit

Idea generation: the R&D team and customer feedback suggest healthy snacks. Screening: of ten ideas, millet biscuits fit the firm's ovens and brand.

Concept testing: 'a crunchy millet biscuit with no maida, at ₹20' is shown to consumers. Marketing strategy and business analysis: target health-conscious urban families; projected sales and profit are acceptable.

Product development: recipes and packs are made. Test marketing: sold in two cities for three months. Commercialisation: national launch with TV and digital ads.

How to write the answer

Meaning → steps as a numbered flow chart with one line each → example → conclusion.
