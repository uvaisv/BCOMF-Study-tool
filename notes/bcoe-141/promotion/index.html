Promotion Mix & IMC

Critical Asked in 9 of 9 papers · avg 22 marks

Role of promotion, components of the promotion mix, choosing the best tool, integrated marketing communication.

In your IGNOU bookBlock 5 · Unit 14 Promotion MixBlock 5 · Unit 15 Personal Selling and Sales PromotionBlock 5 · Unit 16 Advertising and Publicity

Key points

  • Promotion: communicating with customers to inform, persuade and remind them about a product.
  • Components: advertising (paid, non-personal, mass reach — costly, one-way), personal selling (face-to-face, flexible — expensive per contact), sales promotion (short-term incentives: coupons, discounts, contests — quick sales, can hurt brand), publicity/PR (unpaid media coverage — credible, not controllable), direct marketing (catalogues, e-mail, telemarketing — targeted, measurable).
  • Factors in choosing the mix: nature of product (consumer vs industrial), stage of PLC, target market size and spread, push vs pull strategy, budget, competition.
  • Integrated marketing communication: coordinating all promotion tools to deliver one consistent message.
  • Criticism of advertising: raises prices, misleading claims, promotes materialism, creates unnecessary wants.
  • Selling theories: AIDA, right set of circumstances, buying formula, behavioural equation.
  • Communication process: sender → encoding → message → media → decoding → receiver → response → feedback, with noise in between.
  • Advertising media decisions: choosing the media type (press, TV, radio, outdoor, internet) and vehicle by reach, frequency, cost and fit with the audience.

Worked example

Example: a promotion mix for a new cold-pressed juice brand

Advertising: Instagram and YouTube ads, plus hoardings near gyms, to build awareness in the city.

Sales promotion: 'buy 2, get 1 free' for the launch month and free tasting at supermarkets.

Personal selling: sales staff sign up gyms, cafés and offices for bulk supply.

Publicity: a local newspaper story on the founders' farm-to-bottle model.

Direct marketing: WhatsApp offers to customers who have ordered before.

IMC: every tool uses the same line — 'fresh, nothing added' — and the same look, so the message builds up instead of scattering.

How to write the answer

Meaning → each component with merits and limits (a table works well) → selection factors → Indian example → conclusion.
