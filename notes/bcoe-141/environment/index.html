Marketing Environment

Critical Asked in 6 of 9 papers · avg 13 marks

Meaning, importance of environmental scanning, micro and macro environment.

In your IGNOU bookBlock 1 · Unit 2 Marketing Environment

Key points

  • Marketing environment: all forces outside marketing management that affect its ability to build customer relationships.
  • Why scan: spot opportunities and threats, plan strategy, adapt to change, beat competitors.
  • Micro environment (close to the company): company's internal departments, suppliers, marketing intermediaries, customers, competitors, publics.
  • Macro environment (PESTLE-type forces): demographic, economic, natural/ecological, technological, political-legal, socio-cultural.
  • Examples: rising incomes and smartphones (economic, technological) driving e-commerce; GST (legal) changing pricing; health awareness (cultural) boosting organic foods.
  • Government regulations affecting marketing in India include the Competition Act, the Consumer Protection Act, the Essential Commodities Act and laws on weights, measures and advertising.

Worked example

Example: an electric-scooter maker scans its environment

Micro: battery suppliers (supply and price of cells), dealers (service network), customers (young commuters), competitors (petrol and electric brands), publics (media reviews).

Macro: economic — high petrol prices raise demand; political-legal — government subsidies and charging rules; technological — cheaper, longer-life batteries; natural — pollution concerns; socio-cultural — acceptance of app-based, connected vehicles.

The firm turns these into action: it stresses running cost per km in ads and ties up with apartment complexes for charging points.

How to write the answer

Meaning → micro and macro forces in two groups, one Indian example each → importance of scanning → conclusion.
