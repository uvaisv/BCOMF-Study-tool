Marketing Concept & Marketing Mix

Low Asked in 3 of 9 papers · avg 3 marks

Marketing vs selling, relevance of marketing, the 4 Ps.

In your IGNOU bookBlock 1 · Unit 1 Nature and Scope of Marketing

Key points

  • Marketing: identifying and satisfying customer needs profitably.
  • Marketing vs selling: customer needs vs seller's need to sell; long term vs short term; integrated marketing vs sales push.
  • Marketing mix (4 Ps): product, price, place, promotion — extended to 7 Ps for services.
  • Relevance: creates demand, satisfies customers, builds brands, generates revenue and employment, raises living standards.
  • Marketing concepts evolved in stages: production → product → selling → marketing → societal marketing.

Worked example

Example: the 4 Ps of ready-to-cook idli batter

Product: fresh batter in 1 kg packs, no preservatives, 5-day shelf life. Price: ₹60, a little above loose batter but cheaper than eating out. Place: supermarkets and local stores through daily delivery vans. Promotion: in-store sampling and ads showing a working mother saving time.

Marketing vs selling: the firm started by finding that working families lacked time to grind batter (a need), instead of making a product first and then pushing it.

How to write the answer

Meaning → comparison table (marketing vs selling) → 4 Ps with one example each → conclusion.
