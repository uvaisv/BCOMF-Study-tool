Pricing

Critical Asked in 9 of 9 papers · avg 19 marks

Relevance and objectives of pricing, factors affecting price, pricing methods, geographical pricing, break-even.

In your IGNOU bookBlock 3 · Unit 8 Objectives and MethodsBlock 3 · Unit 9 Discounts and AllowancesBlock 3 · Unit 10 Regulation of Prices

Key points

  • Price is the only element of the marketing mix that brings revenue; it signals quality and affects demand.
  • Objectives: profit maximisation, target return, market share, survival, market skimming, penetration, meeting competition, product-quality leadership.
  • Factors: internal (costs, objectives, marketing mix, product life-cycle stage) and external (demand, competition, government regulation, economic conditions, buyers' perception).
  • Methods: cost-based (cost-plus, mark-up, break-even/target profit), demand-based (perceived value, price discrimination), competition-based (going-rate, sealed bid).
  • Geographical pricing: FOB origin, uniform delivered, zone pricing, basing-point, freight absorption.
  • Break-even point = fixed costs ÷ (price − variable cost per unit).
  • International price quotations: EXW, FOB, CIF and other Incoterms.
  • Discounts and allowances off the list price: quantity, trade, cash and seasonal discounts, and promotional allowances.
  • Pricing a new product: market skimming (high price first, lowered later) or market penetration (low price to win a large share quickly). Also fixed vs flexible price policy and unit pricing.
  • Price regulation in India: Competition Act, 2002 (resale price maintenance, price discrimination, collective price fixing, predatory pricing, bargain and deceptive pricing, unreasonably high prices), Consumer Protection Act, 2019 (excessive and deceptive pricing), Essential Commodities Act 1955, Drugs (Price Control) Order 2013, IDR Act 1951 and Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules 2011.

Worked examples

Example: cost-plus pricing and break-even

A pressure-cooker maker's cost is ₹200 a unit; with a 25% mark-up on cost the price is ₹250.

Fixed costs ₹5,00,000; variable cost ₹150 a unit. Break-even = 5,00,000 ÷ (250 − 150) = 5,000 units — sales above this earn profit.

Example: skimming vs penetration

Skimming: a new premium smartphone launches at a high price for early buyers, and the price is cut as rivals appear.

Penetration: when Reliance Jio launched its mobile service in 2016 it offered free calls and data for months to win a huge customer base quickly.

How to write the answer

Meaning → objectives or methods as headed points with examples → conclusion.
