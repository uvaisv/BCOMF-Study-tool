Product Classification, Packaging & Branding

Low Asked in 4 of 9 papers · avg 6 marks

Classification of products, product mix, packaging functions and strategies, branding.

In your IGNOU bookBlock 2 · Unit 5 Product Concepts and ClassificationBlock 2 · Unit 7 Branding and Packaging

Key points

  • Consumer products: convenience, shopping, speciality, unsought. Industrial products: materials and parts, capital items, supplies and services. Also durable vs non-durable.
  • Product mix: all product lines a company sells — width, length, depth, consistency.
  • Packaging functions: protection, convenience, information, promotion ('silent salesman'), identification.
  • Packaging strategies: family packaging, reusable packaging, multiple packaging, kit packaging, changing the package.
  • Branding: name, term, sign or design that identifies a seller's product. Benefits: recognition, loyalty, premium pricing, easier new launches. Types: individual, family, private (store) brands.
  • Product-mix strategies: expanding or contracting the mix, stretching a line up or down, and product diversification into new lines.

Worked examples

Example: measuring a product mix

A company sells soaps (4 brands), shampoos (3) and toothpastes (2).

Width = 3 product lines. Length = 4 + 3 + 2 = 9 items. Depth of one shampoo = 2 variants × 3 pack sizes = 6.

Consistency is high, because all are personal-care products sold through the same shops.

Example: packaging as a silent salesman

A spice brand switches to a resealable, see-through pouch with recipes on the back — it protects the spice, is convenient, informs and stands out on the shelf. A tin that can be reused as a kitchen container is reusable packaging.

How to write the answer

Meaning → classification chart or functions as headed points → examples → conclusion.
