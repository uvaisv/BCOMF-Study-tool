Rural Marketing

Moderate Asked in 6 of 9 papers · avg 6 marks

Meaning, features and challenges of rural markets, 4 A's of rural marketing.

In your IGNOU bookBlock 6 · Unit 18 Rural Marketing

Key points

  • Rural marketing: marketing goods and services to rural consumers (and rural produce to urban markets).
  • Features: large and scattered population, agriculture-dependent income, low literacy, seasonal demand, strong traditions, growing incomes and aspirations.
  • Challenges: poor roads and storage, low and irregular incomes, communication barriers, many languages, spurious products, credit needs.
  • 4 A's: availability (reach every village), affordability (small packs — sachets), acceptability (products suited to rural needs), awareness (vernacular media, haats, melas).
  • Examples: HUL's Project Shakti, ITC e-Choupal, sachet shampoos.

Worked example

Example: the 4 A's for a solar lamp in villages

Availability: stocked with local kirana shops and sold at weekly haats. Affordability: a small model at a low price, or payment in instalments through self-help groups. Acceptability: a sturdy lamp that also charges a mobile phone, which villagers need. Awareness: demonstrations at melas, wall paintings and ads on local-language radio.

Small sachets of shampoo sold for a rupee or two make branded products affordable for daily-wage earners in the same way.

How to write the answer

Meaning → features/challenges → 4 A's with examples → conclusion.
