Machine Hour Rate

Moderate Asked in 4 of 8 papers · avg 9 marks

Computing a machine hour rate from standing charges and machine expenses.

In your IGNOU bookUnit 10 Machine Hour Rate

Key points

  • Machine hour rate: the overhead cost of running a machine for one hour, used to absorb overheads where machines do most of the work.
  • Standing (fixed) charges: rent, lighting, supervisor's salary, insurance — shared by the machine's share of area or time; spread over the machine's working hours.
  • Machine expenses (variable): depreciation, power, repairs, consumables — worked out per hour directly.
  • Depreciation per hour = (cost + installation − scrap value) ÷ total working hours in the machine's life.

Worked example

Example: machine hour rate

Machine cost ₹5,00,000 + installation ₹20,000; scrap ₹20,000; life 10,000 hours → depreciation ₹50 an hour.

Power: 10 units an hour × ₹8 = ₹80 an hour. Repairs ₹24,000 a year over 2,000 working hours → ₹12 an hour.

Standing charges for the machine's share: rent ₹15,000 + supervisor ₹40,000 + lighting ₹5,000 = ₹60,000 a year ÷ 2,000 hours = ₹30 an hour.

Machine hour rate = 30 + 50 + 80 + 12 = ₹172.

How to solve

  1. Find working hours per period (month or year).
  2. Standing charges for the machine's share (area, time) for the period ÷ working hours = standing charges per hour.
  3. Depreciation per hour = (cost + installation − scrap) ÷ life hours.
  4. Power per hour = units per hour × rate per unit; repairs and consumables ÷ hours.
  5. Machine hour rate = standing charges per hour + machine expenses per hour.

Formulas

Machine hour rate = Standing charges per hour + Machine expenses per hour
Depreciation per hour = (Cost + Installation − Scrap value) ÷ Life in hours

How to write the answer

Table: standing charges per period → per hour; machine expenses per hour → total rate per hour.

Common mistakes

  • Charging all rent or the whole supervisor's salary to one machine — use its share.
