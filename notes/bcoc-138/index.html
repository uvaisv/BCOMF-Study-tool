Cost Accounting

BCOC-138 · Year 3 · 3 hours · 100 marks

Usually 8 questions, answer any 5, each 20 marks — a mix of theory and sums (stores ledger, process accounts, contract accounts, operating costs, overheads, reconciliation).

Exam tips

  • A stores ledger (FIFO or LIFO), process accounts and an operating cost sum come up in almost every paper — practise one of each.
  • Theory on purchasing, labour turnover and the objectives of cost accounting gives safe marks.
  • Pick sums you can finish; in costing, a correct format earns marks even with an arithmetic slip.
  • Show working notes and units beside amounts.
  • Use the standard formats: stores ledger columns, process account in T-form, operating cost sheet.
  • State the basis you use for every apportionment.
