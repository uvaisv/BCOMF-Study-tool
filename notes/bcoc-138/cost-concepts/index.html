Cost Concepts, Classification, Methods & Cost Sheet

High Asked in 6 of 8 papers · avg 10 marks

Meaning and characteristics of cost, classification of costs, methods of costing and the industries using them, and the cost sheet.

In your IGNOU bookUnit 2 Cost Concepts and its AscertainmentUnit 12 Unit Costing

Key points

  • Classification: by element (materials, labour, expenses — direct and indirect); by function (production, administration, selling, distribution); by behaviour (fixed, variable, semi-variable); by controllability; by normality; by time (historical, predetermined); for decisions (sunk, opportunity, marginal, differential).
  • Methods of costing and industries: job (printing, repairs), contract (construction), batch (medicines, toys), process (chemicals, textiles, oil), operating or service (transport, hospitals, power), unit or output (mines, cement, bricks), multiple (cars, radios).
  • Cost sheet: direct materials + direct labour + direct expenses = prime cost; + factory overheads (± work in progress) = works cost; + office and administration overheads = cost of production; ± finished goods = cost of goods sold; + selling and distribution = cost of sales; + profit = sales.
  • Unit (output) costing suits a single product made in uniform units (bricks, cement); a cost sheet and a statement of quotation or tender are prepared from it.

Worked examples

Example: cost sheet

Materials: opening 10,000 + purchases 60,000 − closing 15,000 = 55,000; direct wages 30,000; direct expenses 5,000 → prime cost ₹90,000.

Factory overheads 20,000 + opening WIP 6,000 − closing WIP 8,000 → works cost ₹1,08,000.

Office and administration 12,000 → cost of production ₹1,20,000 (10,000 units → ₹12 each).

Opening finished goods 10,000 − closing 14,000 → cost of goods sold ₹1,16,000.

Selling and distribution 9,000 → cost of sales ₹1,25,000. Sales ₹1,50,000 → profit ₹25,000.

Examples: classify the cost

Factory rent → fixed, indirect (factory overhead). Raw cotton in a textile mill → variable, direct material. Telephone bill with a fixed rental plus call charges → semi-variable. Money already spent on a machine that is now useless → sunk cost, ignored in decisions.

How to solve

  1. Prime cost = direct materials consumed (opening stock + purchases − closing stock) + direct wages + direct expenses.
  2. Add factory overheads; adjust opening and closing work in progress → works cost.
  3. Add office and administration overheads → cost of production; adjust finished goods stock.
  4. Add selling and distribution overheads → cost of sales; profit = sales − cost of sales.
  5. Show per-unit figures beside totals when output is given.

Formulas

Prime cost = Direct materials + Direct labour + Direct expenses
Works cost = Prime cost + Factory overheads + Opening WIP − Closing WIP
Cost of sales = Cost of production ± Finished goods + Selling and distribution overheads

How to write the answer

Pro forma cost sheet with headings and imaginary figures.
