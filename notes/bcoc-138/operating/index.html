Operating (Service) Costing

High Asked in 7 of 8 papers · avg 11 marks

Service costing for transport and similar services: cost units, operating cost sheet, cost per km, per passenger-km and per ton-km.

In your IGNOU bookUnit 18 Service Costing

Key points

  • Operating or service costing: used by transport, hospitals, hotels, power and water supply; the cost unit is a composite unit such as passenger-km, ton-km, bed-day or kilowatt-hour.
  • Operating cost sheet: standing (fixed) charges — salaries of staff, garage rent, insurance, taxes, depreciation, interest; maintenance charges — repairs, tyres, servicing; running charges — fuel and oil, drivers' wages (if paid per trip).
  • Passenger-km = distance × number of passengers carried, where passengers = seats × occupancy %; count both directions and all trips and days.
  • Ton-km = distance × load carried, including return loads.

Worked example

Example: cost per passenger-km of a bus

A 50-seat bus runs a 20 km route, 4 round trips a day, 25 days a month, 80% full.

Km per month = 20 × 2 × 4 × 25 = 4,000. Passenger-km = 4,000 × 50 × 80% = 1,60,000.

Monthly costs: standing charges (salaries, insurance, tax, depreciation, garage) ₹60,000; maintenance ₹12,000; diesel 4,000 km ÷ 5 km per litre × ₹90 = ₹72,000; oil and lubricants ₹16,000 → ₹1,60,000.

Cost per passenger-km = 1,60,000 ÷ 1,60,000 = ₹1.00. For profit of 20% on takings, the fare = 1.00 ÷ 0.80 = ₹1.25 per passenger-km.

How to solve

  1. List standing, maintenance and running charges for the period (convert annual figures to monthly).
  2. Find total km = distance × 2 (round trip) × trips per day × days × number of vehicles.
  3. Find passenger-km = total km × seats × occupancy % (or ton-km = km × load).
  4. Cost per unit = total operating cost ÷ passenger-km (or ton-km or km).
  5. If a fare is asked, add profit and taxes as instructed.

Formulas

Passenger-km = Distance × Trips × Days × Vehicles × Seats × Occupancy %
Cost per passenger-km = Total operating cost ÷ Total passenger-km

How to write the answer

Operating cost sheet (standing, maintenance, running) → divide by total km, passenger-km or ton-km.

Common mistakes

  • Counting a round trip as one journey — a round trip is two journeys.
