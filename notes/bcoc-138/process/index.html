Process Costing & Equivalent Production

High Asked in 6 of 8 papers · avg 13 marks

Process accounts, normal and abnormal loss and gain, scrap, and equivalent production statements.

In your IGNOU bookUnit 15 Process CostingUnit 17 Valuation of Work-in-Progress

Key points

  • Process costing: used where production passes through continuous processes (chemicals, textiles, oil, food); the output of one process is the input of the next.
  • Process account: debit — units introduced, materials, labour, direct expenses, overheads; credit — normal loss at scrap value, abnormal loss, output transferred to the next process.
  • Normal loss: expected and unavoidable; its scrap value is credited; the cost is carried by good units. Abnormal loss: loss beyond normal, valued like good units and charged to costing profit and loss. Abnormal gain: actual loss below normal.
  • Equivalent production: work in progress expressed as completed units for each element (materials, labour, overheads) using its percentage of completion.
  • Three statements: equivalent production, cost per equivalent unit, and evaluation of output and work in progress.

Worked examples

Example: normal and abnormal loss

1,000 units introduced at ₹20 (₹20,000); labour ₹15,000; overheads ₹10,500 → total ₹45,500. Normal loss 10% (100 units), sold as scrap at ₹5 = ₹500. Actual output 880 units.

Cost per unit = (45,500 − 500) ÷ (1,000 − 100) = ₹50.

Abnormal loss = normal output 900 − actual 880 = 20 units × 50 = ₹1,000. Output transferred = 880 × 50 = ₹44,000.

Process account credits: 500 + 1,000 + 44,000 = ₹45,500 ✓.

Abnormal loss account: debit 1,000; credit scrap 20 × 5 = 100; the balance ₹900 goes to costing profit and loss.

Example: equivalent production

1,000 units started; 800 completed; 200 in progress — 100% complete for materials, 50% for labour and overheads. Costs: materials ₹10,000, labour ₹4,500, overheads ₹2,700.

Equivalent units: materials 1,000; labour 800 + 100 = 900; overheads 900.

Cost per equivalent unit: materials ₹10, labour ₹5, overheads ₹3 → ₹18.

Completed units 800 × 18 = ₹14,400. Work in progress = 200 × 10 + 100 × 5 + 100 × 3 = ₹2,800. Total ₹17,200 ✓.

How to solve

  1. Normal loss units = input × normal loss %; its value = units × scrap rate.
  2. Cost per unit = (total cost − scrap value of normal loss) ÷ (input units − normal loss units).
  3. Abnormal loss units = normal output − actual output (abnormal gain if negative).
  4. Value output and abnormal loss at the cost per unit; transfer output to the next process.
  5. In the abnormal loss account, credit its scrap value and take the balance to costing P&L.
  6. For WIP: statement of equivalent production → cost per equivalent unit → evaluation.

Formulas

Cost per unit = (Total cost − Scrap value of normal loss) ÷ (Input − Normal loss units)
Equivalent units = Completed units + (WIP units × % complete)

How to write the answer

Working table for normal loss and cost per unit → Process I account → Process II account → Abnormal loss/gain account.

Common mistakes

  • Valuing abnormal loss at scrap value — it is valued at the cost per good unit.
