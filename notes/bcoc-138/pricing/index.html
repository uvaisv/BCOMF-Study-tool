Pricing of Material Issues (FIFO, LIFO)

Critical Asked in 8 of 8 papers · avg 20 marks

Stores ledger under FIFO and LIFO, other pricing methods, returns and shortages, material cost from a supplier's invoice, problems in pricing issues.

In your IGNOU bookUnit 5 Pricing the Issue of Materials

Key points

  • FIFO: materials are issued at the price of the oldest lot in stock; closing stock is at the latest prices. In rising prices it shows lower cost and higher profit.
  • LIFO: issues at the price of the latest lot; closing stock at old prices; charges current prices to production. (Not allowed for financial statements under AS 2, but asked in cost accounts.)
  • Other methods: simple average, weighted average, standard price, replacement price.
  • Returns to supplier: taken out at their purchase price. Returns from production (materials received back): treated as a fresh receipt at the price they were issued, or reissued first.
  • Shortage found on verification: normal shortage is absorbed in the cost of good units; abnormal shortage is written off to costing profit and loss.
  • Material cost from an invoice: price less trade discount + freight, insurance, non-recoverable taxes and charges for returnable containers minus the credit expected for their return; cash discount is ignored (a financial item).
  • Problems in pricing issues: prices change between lots, a method has to be chosen, returns and shortages, many small lots, effect on stock value and profit.

Worked example

Example: FIFO and LIFO on the same receipts and issues

Opening stock 100 units @ ₹10. Received 200 @ ₹12. Issued 250. Received 100 @ ₹13. Issued 120.

FIFO: first issue = 100 × 10 + 150 × 12 = ₹2,800; second issue = 50 × 12 + 70 × 13 = ₹1,510; closing stock 30 × 13 = ₹390.

LIFO: first issue = 200 × 12 + 50 × 10 = ₹2,900; second issue = 100 × 13 + 20 × 10 = ₹1,500; closing stock 30 × 10 = ₹300.

Check: goods available ₹4,700 = 4,310 + 390 (FIFO) = 4,400 + 300 (LIFO). With rising prices, LIFO charges more to production and values closing stock lower.

How to solve

  1. Draw the stores ledger with receipt, issue and balance columns.
  2. Enter the opening stock and each receipt as a separate lot with its rate.
  3. For each issue, take units lot by lot — oldest first (FIFO) or latest first (LIFO) — and show each lot's quantity × rate.
  4. Enter returns from production as a receipt at the original issue price; returns to supplier as an issue at purchase price.
  5. Record shortages as an issue (normal) or separately (abnormal).
  6. After every entry, show the balance lots and total value.

Formulas

Value of issue = Σ (units taken from each lot × that lot's rate)

How to write the answer

Stores ledger with columns Receipts (qty, rate, amount) | Issues (qty, rate, amount) | Balance (qty, rate, amount).

Common mistakes

  • Pricing an issue from one lot when it needs units from two lots — split it.
