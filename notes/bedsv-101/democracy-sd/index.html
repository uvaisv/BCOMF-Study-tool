Democracy & Sustainable Development

Critical Asked in 2 of 4 papers · avg 10 marks

Principles of democracy, democracy in education, sustainable development and the role of education.

In your IGNOU bookBlock 1 · Unit 4 Pillars of Democracy: Living in Peace and HarmonyBlock 2 · Unit 4 Values for Sustainable Development

Key points

  • Democracy is not just a form of government but a way of life, based on equality, justice (fairness) and freedom from restraints that limit growth.
  • Principles of democracy in the book: respect for individuality, confidence in human intelligence, and the right and responsibility to take part in decisions.
  • Democracy in education: equality means fairness or justice in the classroom; freedom means both 'freedom to' (what students may do) and 'freedom from' (restraints).
  • Sustainable development: development that meets present needs without harming the ability of future generations to meet theirs; it has economic, social and environmental pillars.
  • Unsustainable growth has degraded land, water and forests, wiped out species and pushed many people to the margins while a few benefit.
  • Education for sustainable development builds values, not just knowledge: knowing is not enough without the right attitude. The core value is respect — for others, future generations, diversity and the planet.

Worked example

Example: democracy in a classroom and sustainability in a school

A class elects its monitor, decides its picnic spot by vote and lets every student speak in discussions — equality, freedom and participation practised as a way of life.

The same school starts rain-water harvesting, composts canteen waste and replaces plastic with steel tumblers — students learn respect for future generations by doing, not only by reading.

How to write the answer

Meaning → principles or role as headed points → conclusion.
