Peers & Media

Critical Asked in 3 of 4 papers · avg 14 marks

Value development through peers; role, functions, evolution and negative effects of media.

In your IGNOU bookBlock 5 · Unit 2 School and PeerBlock 5 · Unit 4 Influence of Media on Value Development

Key points

  • A peer group is people of about the same age; as children grow, peers increasingly reduce the family's influence on their attitudes and behaviour.
  • Peers can build values (cooperation, sharing, teamwork) or harm them (peer pressure — smoking, drugs); schools can use peer learning to inculcate values.
  • Media means all the channels through which people express themselves to others — print, broadcast, digital, mass media.
  • Growth of media: from print to radio, cinema and television, and now the internet, mobile phones and social media.
  • Functions of media: information, education and entertainment.
  • Media is one of many influences on values but a very powerful one; it can spread violence, materialism and bad role models.
  • Answer: media literacy — the ability to access, analyse, evaluate and process media, so children use it consciously and think critically. Parents should know what children watch and do on social networks.

Worked example

Example: peer pressure and media literacy

A 15-year-old's friends start vaping and tease him for refusing. A school peer-mentor programme pairs him with older students who refuse confidently — peers used for good.

The same student sees an influencer advertise a 'miracle' weight-loss drink. A media-literacy class teaches him to ask: who paid for this? What is the evidence? What is left out? — so he uses media consciously instead of being used by it.

How to write the answer

Meaning → role or effects as headed points → how parents and schools can guide → conclusion.
