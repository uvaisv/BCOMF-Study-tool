Personality & Character

Critical Asked in 4 of 4 papers · avg 13 marks

Characteristics of personality, character–personality patterns, personality disorders.

In your IGNOU bookBlock 3 · Unit 4 Character and Personality

Key points

  • Personality is the whole pattern of a person's traits and behaviour; character is its moral or ethical side.
  • Early classifications: by body build (somatotype), by body chemistry, and the Ayurvedic temperaments (rakta, pitta, kapha, vayu). Modern psychologists use trait clusters.
  • Personality disorders are lasting, inflexible patterns (traced to adolescence) in thinking, emotion and relationships that cause distress. The book describes antisocial, borderline and narcissistic disorders.
  • Peck et al. (1962) — six vectors of character: moral stability, ego strength, super-ego strength, spontaneity, friendliness and the hostility–guilt complex.
  • Five character–personality patterns: amoral, expedient, conforming, irrational-conscientious and rational-altruistic (the most mature).

Worked example

Example: Peck's character types in one situation

A cashier finds ₹500 extra in the till.

Amoral: pockets it without any thought. Expedient: keeps it unless someone may find out. Conforming: returns it because 'that is the rule'. Irrational-conscientious: returns it out of rigid guilt, without understanding why. Rational-altruistic: returns it because they understand it belongs to a customer who may need it — the most mature level.

How to write the answer

Definition → characteristics or types as headed points → conclusion.
