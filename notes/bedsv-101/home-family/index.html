Home, Parents & Community in Value Development

Critical Asked in 2 of 4 papers · avg 13 marks

Home as the first learning place, role of parents, family and community, socialisation, ways to build values at home.

In your IGNOU bookBlock 5 · Unit 1 Value Development through Social Institutions: Family and NeighbourhoodBlock 5 · Unit 3 Role of Parents

Key points

  • Values are part of every culture; most of us learn them at home, at school and in the neighbourhood — from parents, teachers and religious leaders.
  • Values develop in three stages: imprint, modelling and socialisation.
  • Parents are the primary agents who pass on personal and social values; children copy the behaviour they see.
  • What builds morality at home: reasoning with the child (induction), nurture and support, setting limits, modelling moral behaviour, and open, democratic family discussion.
  • Role of parents: keep discipline with love, compassion and sympathy; understand the child's development and need for security; protect children from peer pressure; identify and cherish the family's values.
  • The neighbourhood and community also teach values through shared festivals, cooperation and service.

Worked example

Example: imprint, modelling and socialisation

A toddler copies her parents greeting elders with folded hands (imprint). As a child she admires an aunt who volunteers at a shelter and starts helping too (modelling). As a teenager she joins the school's community service club and adopts its shared values (socialisation).

Parents who explain why a rule matters ('we don't take others' things because they feel hurt') — induction — build stronger values than parents who only punish.

How to write the answer

Meaning → role or ways as headed points with a home example → conclusion.
