English Communication Skills

BEGAE-182 · Year 2 · 2 hours · 50 marks

All 4 questions are compulsory. Q1: answer 4 of 6 short questions (5 marks each, about 100 words). Q2–Q4: 10 marks each — usually a letter, a group discussion, and a presentation, dialogue or essay — often with an OR choice.

Exam tips

  • A formal letter and a group discussion have come up in almost every paper — learn one format for each and practise it.
  • In Q1 pick the four you can answer with a definition and an example; homophones, reading levels and barriers to communication repeat often.
  • Where there is an OR, choose the writing task (letter, dialogue, presentation) if you know its format — format earns marks.
