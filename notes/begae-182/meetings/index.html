Meetings

Moderate Asked in 6 of 11 papers · avg 2 marks

Meeting etiquette, language and tone, preparing for and taking part in meetings, making a meeting successful, and ending it well.

In your IGNOU bookBlock 2 · Unit 3 Group Discussions and Meetings (Meetings)

Key points

  • Before the meeting: read the agenda and last minutes, gather facts and figures, prepare your points and questions.
  • Etiquette: be on time, phone on silent, address the chair, don't interrupt, use formal, polite, positive language and a calm tone.
  • Active participation: listen, contribute relevant points with facts, ask for clarification, support or disagree politely, take notes of decisions and your actions.
  • A successful meeting: a clear purpose and agenda, the right people, time kept, a chairperson guiding discussion, decisions recorded in minutes, follow-up.
  • Ending a meeting: summarise the decisions, confirm who does what by when, fix the next meeting, thank everyone.

Worked example

Model phrases for a meeting

To the chair: 'Madam Chair, may I add a point?'

Contributing with facts: 'Our sales fell 8% last quarter, mainly in the South.'

Disagreeing politely: 'I see the logic, but I'm concerned about the cost. Could we look at a smaller trial first?'

Asking for clarity: 'Could you explain what you mean by "restructure"?'

Closing (chair): 'To sum up, we've agreed to run the trial in two stores. Priya will send the plan by Friday. We'll meet again on the 20th. Thank you, everyone.'

How to write the answer

Headed points with one line each → conclusion.
