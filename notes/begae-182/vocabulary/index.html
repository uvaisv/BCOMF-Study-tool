Words: Homophones, Word Formation & Idioms

High Asked in 9 of 11 papers · avg 4 marks

Homonyms, homophones and homographs; word formation by affixation, compounding and word shift; jargon, acronyms, idioms and ambiguity.

In your IGNOU bookBlock 3 · Unit 2 Vocabulary Development

Key points

  • Homonyms: same spelling and sound, different meanings (bank — river bank / a bank). Homophones: same sound, different spelling and meaning (their/there, write/right, sea/see). Homographs: same spelling, different meaning and often sound (lead — metal / to guide; tear — cry / rip).
  • Affixation: adding prefixes (un-happy, re-write, dis-agree) or suffixes (kind-ness, develop-ment, care-ful).
  • Compounding: joining two words (blackboard, notebook, well-known, mother-in-law).
  • Word shift (conversion): using a word as a new part of speech without change (email a report, a good read, to Google something).
  • Jargon: specialist words of a field (bandwidth, liquidity). Acronyms: words formed from initials (NASA, UNESCO); abbreviations read letter by letter (RBI).
  • Idioms with 'feet': find one's feet (settle in), put one's feet up (relax), fall on one's feet (be lucky after trouble), have both feet on the ground (be practical), be back on one's feet (recover).
  • Ambiguity: a sentence with two meanings, from a word (lexical) or from its structure ('I saw the man with the telescope').

Worked examples

Examples: sound and spelling

Homophones: 'They're putting their bags over there.' Homonym: 'We sat on the river bank before going to the bank.' Homograph: 'Lead pipes can lead to health problems.'

Word formation: un + happy (prefix); care + ful (suffix); note + book (compound); 'Please e-mail me the file' (conversion — a noun used as a verb).

Ambiguity: 'I saw the man with the telescope' — who had the telescope?

Examples: 'feet' idioms in context

After a month in the new city she found her feet. On Sunday he just put his feet up. The company lost its biggest client but fell on its feet with a new contract. Our manager has both feet on the ground. After the surgery he was back on his feet in two weeks.

How to write the answer

Definition → two or three examples of each → conclusion.
