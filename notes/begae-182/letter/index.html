Formal Letters

Critical Asked in 11 of 11 papers · avg 8 marks

Writing formal letters: job applications, accepting an offer, letters to the editor, enquiries, orders, leave applications and letters to a client.

In your IGNOU bookBlock 3 · Unit 4 The Language of Formal Letters

Key points

  • Layout: sender's address → date → receiver's name, designation and address → subject line → salutation (Dear Sir/Madam) → body → complimentary close (Yours faithfully with 'Sir/Madam'; Yours sincerely with a name) → signature and name.
  • Body in three short paragraphs: why you are writing; the details; what you want done, with a polite close.
  • Job application: the post and where you saw it; qualifications and experience; why you suit the post; availability for interview; mention the enclosed résumé.
  • Accepting an offer: thank them; accept the post and terms; confirm the joining date (or the time needed to join); say you look forward to joining.
  • Letter to the editor: 'The Editor, <newspaper>'; subject naming the problem; describe the problem and who it affects; suggest solutions; request publication.
  • Enquiry or order letters: exact items or information needed, quantities, delivery and payment terms. Leave application: dates, reason, arrangements for your work.
  • Tone: formal, polite, gender-neutral (Dear Sir/Madam, 'the candidate', they), short sentences, no slang or abbreviations.

Worked examples

Model: letter to the editor (short)

12, Lake Road, Kochi 682011 · 5 March 2026

The Editor, The City Times, Kochi

Subject: Broken streetlights on Lake Road

Sir/Madam,

Through your newspaper I wish to draw attention to the broken streetlights on Lake Road, which have been dark for three weeks.

Women, students returning from evening classes and elderly walkers feel unsafe, and two minor accidents have already happened. The potholes are invisible at night.

I request the Corporation to repair the lights at once and to check them every month. I hope you will publish this letter.

Yours faithfully,

Anu Joseph

Model: opening and closing of a job application

'I wish to apply for the post of Accounts Assistant advertised in The Hindu on 2 March 2026.'

… (qualifications, experience, why you suit the post) …

'I have enclosed my résumé and would be glad to attend an interview at your convenience. Yours faithfully,' — use 'Yours sincerely' only when you began with the person's name ('Dear Ms Rao').

How to write the answer

Write the full layout even in the exam — marks are given for format, content and language.

Common mistakes

  • Mixing 'Yours faithfully' and 'Yours sincerely', or leaving out the subject line.
