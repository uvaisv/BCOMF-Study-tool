Presentations & Visual Aids

Critical Asked in 10 of 11 papers · avg 7 marks

Structuring a presentation, making a five-minute presentation on a topic, visual aids and their functions, symbols and graphs, and essentials of presentation skills.

In your IGNOU bookBlock 2 · Unit 4 Presentation Skills: Essentials of Presentation Skills

Key points

  • Structure: introduction (greet, topic, purpose, outline) → body (3–4 main points, each with an example or figure) → conclusion (summary, one take-away, thanks, invite questions).
  • A five-minute presentation: about 1 minute introduction, 3 minutes for three points, 1 minute conclusion. Write it as slide headings with 2–3 bullet points each plus what you would say.
  • Preparing for a job interview (asked often): research the organisation; know your résumé; practise common questions; dress and body language; documents to carry; reach early; follow up with thanks.
  • Visual aids: slides, charts, graphs, tables, pictures, videos, handouts. Functions: catch attention, simplify complex data, show trends and comparisons, help memory, save time.
  • Good visuals: one idea per slide, large readable text, few words, clear labels; use symbols and graphs to show numbers and trends at a glance.
  • Essentials of presentation skills: know your audience; practise; clear voice and good pace; eye contact and open posture; manage time; handle questions calmly.

Worked examples

Model: a five-minute presentation — 'Digital payments in India'

Slide 1 (about 1 min): greeting; topic; 'I will cover growth, benefits and risks.'

Slide 2 — Growth: UPI in shops and homes; payments by QR code; a simple bar chart of monthly transactions.

Slide 3 — Benefits: speed, no need for change, a record of every payment.

Slide 4 — Risks: fraud by OTP sharing; the digital divide for elderly users.

Slide 5 (about 1 min): summary; take-away — 'Pay digitally, but never share your PIN or OTP'; thank the audience and invite questions.

Model: preparing for a job interview

Read the company's website and recent news; revise your résumé; practise answers to 'Tell me about yourself' and 'Why this job?'; keep certificates in a folder; dress formally; reach 15 minutes early; send a short thank-you e-mail the same day.

How to write the answer

Title → introduction → three headed points → conclusion; mention one visual aid you would use for each point.
