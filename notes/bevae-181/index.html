Exam tips

  • The questions follow the order of the syllabus: environment basics first, laws and ethics at the end.
  • Many questions repeat word for word — go through the 'Asked again and again' list on the course page first.
  • Answer every question: leave nothing blank on the OMR sheet.
  • For 'which is NOT' questions, cross out the three that fit — the one left is the answer.
  • Make a one-page list of years (Acts, conventions), places (NGT benches, wetlands, biogeographic zones) and names (movements) and revise it on exam day.
