Exam tips

  • The company secretary, directors' liabilities, the Memorandum or Articles, and membership appear in almost every paper — often word for word.
  • The June 2025 and June 2026 papers were nearly identical, so recent papers are the best practice.
  • Quote the section of the Companies Act, 2013 and one case (Salomon, Turquand, Ashbury) — it lifts a law answer.
