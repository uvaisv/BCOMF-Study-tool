Accounts, Dividend & Audit

High Asked in 5 of 9 papers · avg 11 marks

Books of account and records, financial statements, dividend and interim dividend, auditors — appointment, disqualifications, rights, audit report, and e-filing.

In your IGNOU bookBlock 5 · Unit 17 DividendBlock 5 · Unit 18 AccountsBlock 5 · Unit 19 Audit

Key points

  • Books of account (Sec 128): kept at the registered office on accrual basis by double entry, showing cash received and paid, sales and purchases, assets and liabilities, and cost records where required; preserved for 8 years; directors can inspect. Responsible: managing director, whole-time director, CFO or person charged by the board. Default: imprisonment up to 1 year and/or fine ₹50,000–₹5 lakh.
  • Financial statements (Sec 2(40)): balance sheet, profit and loss account, cash flow statement, statement of changes in equity, and notes.
  • Dividend (Sec 123): only out of current or past profits after depreciation (or government money); deposited in a separate bank account within 5 days and paid within 30 days of declaration; unpaid dividend moved to an Unpaid Dividend Account, and after 7 years to the Investor Education and Protection Fund. Interim dividend: declared by the board during the year out of profits.
  • Auditors (Sec 139): first auditor by the board within 30 days; later at the AGM for 5 years. Cannot be appointed (Sec 141): a body corporate other than an LLP, an officer or employee, a partner or employee of an officer, a person indebted to the company beyond limits, relatives holding securities, those with business relationships, anyone auditing more than 20 companies, anyone convicted of fraud (for 10 years).
  • Rights of an auditor: access to books and vouchers at all times, to call for information and explanations, to attend general meetings and be heard, to receive notices, to remuneration.
  • Audit report (Sec 143): whether the statements give a true and fair view; information and explanations obtained; proper books kept; agreement with books; compliance with accounting standards; adverse remarks; directors' disqualification; internal financial controls; pending litigation; transfers to IEPF; CARO matters.
  • E-filing: documents filed online on the MCA21 portal with digital signatures — e.g. AOC-4 (financial statements), MGT-7 (annual return), DIR-12, INC forms.

Worked examples

Example: paying a declared dividend

The AGM declares a dividend on 10 September. The money must be put into a separate bank account within 5 days (by 15 September) and paid to shareholders within 30 days (by 10 October).

Dividend still unpaid after that goes to an Unpaid Dividend Account, and whatever stays unclaimed there for 7 years goes to the Investor Education and Protection Fund.

Example: who cannot be appointed auditor?

A chartered accountant who is an employee of the company, a partner of one of its officers, or who already audits 20 companies cannot be appointed. Nor can an audit firm organised as a body corporate other than an LLP.
