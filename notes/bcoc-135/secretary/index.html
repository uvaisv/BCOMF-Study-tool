Company Secretary

High Asked in 7 of 9 papers · avg 13 marks

Who a company secretary is, appointment and removal, position, duties, and statutory, contractual and criminal liabilities.

In your IGNOU bookBlock 4 · Unit 15 Company Secretary

Key points

  • Company secretary (Sec 2(24)): a member of the Institute of Company Secretaries of India appointed by the company to perform the functions of a secretary.
  • Appointment (Sec 203): a whole-time company secretary is key managerial personnel (KMP), compulsory for listed companies and public companies with paid-up capital of ₹10 crore or more. Appointed by a board resolution setting the terms; filed with the Registrar. Removal also by board resolution, following the terms of appointment.
  • Position: an officer and employee of the company, KMP, and an 'officer in default' for compliance failures; not an agent who can bind the company in contracts beyond authority.
  • Duties (Sec 205): report to the board on compliance with the Act and other laws; ensure secretarial standards are followed; advise directors on governance; convene board and general meetings, draft notices and minutes; maintain statutory registers; file returns and forms with the Registrar; secretarial audit support.
  • Statutory liabilities: penalties as officer in default for not filing returns, not keeping registers and minutes, false statements (Sec 448), fraud (Sec 447), failure to comply with provisions on meetings, share transfers and dividends.
  • Contractual liabilities: to the company for negligence, breach of the service contract, secret profits or misuse of position and confidential information.
  • Criminal liability: imprisonment and fine where offences such as fraud, falsification of books or false returns are proved.

Worked example

Example: must the company appoint one, and who is liable?

A public company has paid-up capital of ₹12 crore. Because this is ₹10 crore or more, it must appoint a whole-time company secretary as key managerial personnel, by a board resolution.

The company fails to file its annual return with the Registrar. The company secretary, as an officer in default, can be penalised along with the company. If the secretary had also knowingly signed a false return, Section 448 (false statement) would apply as well.

How to write the answer

Definition → appointment → duties or liabilities as headings with the section number → conclusion.

Common mistakes

  • Mixing up the company secretary's duties (Sec 205) with the directors' duties (Sec 166).
