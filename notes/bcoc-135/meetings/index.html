Company Meetings & Resolutions

High Asked in 7 of 9 papers · avg 16 marks

Kinds of meetings, AGM and EGM, requisites of a valid meeting, notice, quorum, chairman, proxy, board meetings, and ordinary and special resolutions.

In your IGNOU bookBlock 4 · Unit 16 Meetings of Shareholders and Board

Key points

  • Kinds: shareholders' meetings (annual general meeting, extraordinary general meeting, class meetings), board and committee meetings, creditors' meetings.
  • AGM (Sec 96): every company except a one-person company; first AGM within 9 months of the end of the first financial year, later ones within 6 months of year-end and not more than 15 months apart; in business hours (9 a.m. to 6 p.m.) on any day that is not a national holiday, at the registered office or elsewhere in the same city. Ordinary business: financial statements, dividend, directors retiring by rotation, auditors.
  • EGM (Sec 100): called by the board, or on requisition by members holding at least one-tenth of paid-up capital; if the board does not act within 21 days, the requisitionists may call it within 3 months; the Tribunal can also order one (Sec 98).
  • Valid meeting: called by proper authority, proper notice (21 clear days, shorter with consent — Sec 101) with an explanatory statement for special business (Sec 102), quorum, a chairman, agenda, resolutions passed properly, minutes kept.
  • Quorum (Sec 103): private company 2 members; public company 5, 15 or 30 members depending on whether it has up to 1,000, up to 5,000 or more members. No quorum within half an hour: the meeting is adjourned to the same day next week (or as the board decides); a requisitioned meeting is cancelled.
  • Chairman (Sec 104): elected by members present; conducts the meeting, keeps order, decides points of order, may have a casting vote under the articles.
  • Proxy (Sec 105): a person appointed by a member to attend and vote on a poll; cannot speak; one proxy for up to 50 members and 10% of capital; form lodged 48 hours before the meeting.
  • Resolutions: ordinary (simple majority); special (votes for at least three times votes against — Sec 114), e.g. altering memorandum or articles, changing name, reducing capital, buyback above 10%, sweat equity, voluntary winding up; special notice (Sec 115) for removing a director or auditor.
  • Board meetings (Sec 173): first within 30 days of incorporation, then at least 4 a year with no more than 120 days between them; 7 days' notice; quorum one-third or 2 directors, whichever is higher; video conferencing allowed.

Worked examples

Example: has the special resolution passed?

At a general meeting, 300 votes are cast for a resolution to change the company's name and 100 against. For a special resolution, votes for must be at least three times the votes against: 300 ≥ 3 × 100, so it passes.

With 290 for and 100 against it would fail — though 290 would easily pass an ordinary resolution (simple majority).

Example: AGM timing and quorum

A company's financial year ends on 31 March 2025, so its AGM must be held by 30 September 2025 (within 6 months), and within 15 months of the previous AGM. It must be held between 9 a.m. and 6 p.m. on a day that is not a national holiday.

It is a public company with 3,000 members, so the quorum is 15 members present in person. If there is no quorum within half an hour, the meeting stands adjourned to the same day next week.
