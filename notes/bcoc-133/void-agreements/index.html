Void Agreements & Public Policy

Low Asked in 6 of 11 papers · avg 7 marks

Agreements in restraint of trade, opposed to public policy, unlawful object or consideration, wagering agreements.

In your IGNOU bookBlock 2 · Unit 5 Consideration and Legality of Object (5.7–5.8)Block 2 · Unit 6 Void Agreements and Contingent Contracts (6.2 Void agreements)

Key points

  • Restraint of trade (Sec 27) is void. Exceptions: seller of goodwill agreeing not to compete within local limits, partners' agreements, trade combinations regulating business, service agreements during employment.
  • Opposed to public policy: trading with enemy, stifling prosecution, interference with justice, marriage brokerage, restraint of marriage (Sec 26), restraint of legal proceedings (Sec 28), sale of public offices.
  • Unlawful object or consideration (Sec 23): forbidden by law, defeats law, fraudulent, injurious to person or property, immoral, opposed to public policy.
  • Wagering agreement (Sec 30): promise to pay on an uncertain event with no other interest — void; collateral transactions are valid (except in Maharashtra and Gujarat). Not wagers: insurance, lotteries under licence, prize competitions of skill.

Worked examples

Example: restraint of trade

The seller of a bakery in Kochi, along with its goodwill, agrees not to run a bakery in Kochi for five years → valid (exception for the sale of goodwill, within reasonable local limits).

An employee agrees never to work for any competitor after leaving the job → void: a restraint after employment is not protected.

Example: wager and unlawful object

A and B agree that A pays B ₹1,000 if India wins a match and B pays A if it loses → a wagering agreement, void; neither can sue. Life insurance is not a wager, because the insured has an insurable interest.

A promises B ₹50,000 to get him a government job by bribing an official → void, because the object is unlawful.

How to write the answer
