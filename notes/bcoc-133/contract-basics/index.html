Nature & Essentials of a Contract

High Asked in 9 of 11 papers · avg 14 marks

Contract and agreement, essentials of a valid contract, types of contracts, void/voidable/illegal.

In your IGNOU bookBlock 1 · Unit 1 Essentials of a Contract

Key points

  • Contract = agreement enforceable by law (Sec 2(h)); agreement = offer + acceptance. 'All contracts are agreements but all agreements are not contracts.'
  • 'Law of contract is not the whole law of agreements nor the whole law of obligations' (Salmond): it covers only agreements that create legal obligations, and only obligations arising from agreements.
  • Essentials (Sec 10): offer and acceptance, intention to create legal relations, free consent, competent parties, lawful consideration, lawful object, not expressly declared void, certainty, possibility of performance, legal formalities.
  • By creation: express, implied, quasi. By execution: executed, executory. By enforceability: valid, void, voidable, illegal, unenforceable.
  • Void agreement: not enforceable from the start. Voidable contract: enforceable at the option of one party (e.g. consent by coercion). Illegal agreement: forbidden by law — void, and collateral agreements are also void.

Worked examples

Example: agreement vs contract

Friends agree to meet for dinner and one does not turn up — an agreement, but not a contract, because there was no intention to create legal relations.

A agrees to sell his used car to B for ₹3 lakh, both freely, both adults — a contract, enforceable by law.

Example: void, voidable and illegal

An agreement with a 15-year-old to buy his cycle → void from the start.

A contract signed because of a threat → voidable: the threatened party may cancel it or keep it.

An agreement to smuggle gold → illegal; a loan given knowingly to finance the smuggling is also void.
