Corporate Accounting

BCOC-137 · Year 3 · 3 hours · 100 marks

9 questions, answer any 5, each carries 20 marks. About a third are numerical (share issue and forfeiture, redemption, final accounts, cash flow, consolidation, valuation); the rest are theory, including banking and NPAs.

Exam tips

  • NPAs, holding companies, cash flow and valuation of goodwill or shares appear in almost every paper — as theory or a sum.
  • Theory questions on banking and NPAs are safe marks if numericals are not your strength.
  • Pick a numerical only if you can finish it; a half-done sum earns little.
  • Show working notes separately and refer to them in the final statement.
  • Use the Schedule III vertical format for company accounts.
  • Write narrations for journal entries.
