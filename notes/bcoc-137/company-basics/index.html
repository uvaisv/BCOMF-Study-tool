Company & Company Accounts: Basics

Moderate Asked in 5 of 9 papers · avg 6 marks

Features of a company, formation and documents for incorporation, the Memorandum of Association, and the concept and importance of company accounts.

In your IGNOU bookUnit 1 General Introductions

Key points

  • Features: separate legal entity, perpetual succession, limited liability, transferable shares, common seal (optional), artificial person, separation of ownership and management.
  • Formation steps: promotion → name reservation → filing incorporation documents (SPICe+ with memorandum and articles) → certificate of incorporation → commencement of business declaration.
  • Documents for incorporation: memorandum and articles, declaration of compliance by a professional, affidavits and identity proofs of subscribers and first directors, directors' consent, proof of registered office, name approval.
  • Memorandum of Association: the charter — name, registered office, objects, liability, capital and subscription clauses.
  • Company accounts: books and financial statements kept under Sec 128–129 and Schedule III following accounting standards. Importance: true and fair view for shareholders, basis for dividend and tax, investor and lender decisions, control by management, legal compliance and audit.

Worked example

Example: forming a private company

Three friends form 'Green Leaf Organics Private Limited'. They reserve the name and file SPICe+ with the memorandum and articles, the professional's declaration, their identity proofs and consent to act as directors, and proof of the registered office.

The Registrar issues the certificate of incorporation — from that date the company is a separate legal person. Within 180 days it files its declaration for commencement of business before it starts business or borrows.
