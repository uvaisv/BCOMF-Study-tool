Internal Reconstruction & Capital Reduction

High Asked in 7 of 9 papers · avg 11 marks

Internal reconstruction, alteration and reduction of share capital, the capital reduction (reconstruction) account, steps and approvals, and treatment of special items.

In your IGNOU bookUnit 15 Internal Reconstruction

Key points

  • Internal reconstruction: reorganising a company's capital without winding it up — reducing capital to write off accumulated losses and fictitious or overvalued assets, with sacrifices by shareholders and creditors.
  • Alteration of share capital (Sec 61): increase, consolidate, sub-divide, convert shares into stock, cancel unissued shares — by ordinary resolution if the articles allow; no Tribunal approval.
  • Reduction of share capital (Sec 66): special resolution + confirmation by the Tribunal; creditors can object; order registered with the Registrar.
  • Steps: prepare the scheme; board approval; special resolution; Tribunal application and notice to creditors, Central Government and SEBI; confirmation; registration; accounting entries.
  • Capital reduction (reconstruction) account: credited with the reduction in capital and sacrifices by creditors; debited with accumulated losses, fictitious assets and fall in asset values; any surplus goes to capital reserve.
  • Special items: arrears of preference dividend are not a liability — if the scheme pays part, it is charged to the reconstruction account; surrender of shares — shares surrendered by shareholders are reissued to creditors, the rest cancelled through the reconstruction account.
  • Role of stakeholders: shareholders vote and bear the loss; creditors and debenture-holders agree to sacrifices; the Tribunal protects creditors; the board drafts and implements the scheme.

Worked example

Example: internal reconstruction

10,000 equity shares of ₹100 are reduced to ₹40 each (reduction ₹6,00,000); debenture holders give up ₹50,000. Capital Reduction A/c is credited ₹6,50,000.

It is used to write off the debit balance of profit and loss ₹4,00,000, goodwill ₹1,00,000, preliminary expenses ₹20,000 and ₹80,000 overvaluation of plant — ₹6,00,000 in all.

The remaining ₹50,000 goes to capital reserve. Because capital is reduced, a special resolution and the Tribunal's confirmation are needed (Section 66).

How to write the answer

Meaning → steps or provisions as a list → the reconstruction account → a revised Balance Sheet heading list → conclusion.
