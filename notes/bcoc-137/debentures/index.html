Issue & Redemption of Debentures

Moderate Asked in 4 of 9 papers · avg 8 marks

Meaning and types of debentures, issue at par, premium and discount and redeemable at par or premium, issue as purchase consideration, and methods of redemption.

In your IGNOU bookUnit 5 Issues and Redemption of Debentures

Key points

  • Debenture (Sec 2(30)): an instrument acknowledging a debt of the company, usually carrying fixed interest and secured by a charge.
  • Types: secured/unsecured, redeemable/irredeemable, convertible/non-convertible, registered/bearer, first/second.
  • Issue entries: Bank Dr; Discount on issue Dr; Loss on issue of debentures Dr (for premium payable on redemption) To Debentures To Securities premium To Premium on redemption of debentures.
  • Issued for purchase of assets: Assets Dr To Vendor; Vendor Dr To Debentures (and securities premium or with discount).
  • Redemption methods: lump sum at maturity; instalments (by drawing lots); purchase in the open market and cancellation; conversion into shares; sinking fund.
  • Debenture redemption reserve and investment of a part of debentures maturing in the year are required for some companies under the Companies (Share Capital and Debentures) Rules.

Worked examples

Example: issue at a discount, redeemable at a premium

1,000 debentures of ₹100 are issued at 5% discount, redeemable at 10% premium.

Bank A/c Dr 95,000

Loss on Issue of Debentures A/c Dr 15,000 (discount 5,000 + premium payable 10,000)

To Debentures A/c 1,00,000

To Premium on Redemption of Debentures A/c 10,000.

The loss on issue is written off over the life of the debentures.

Example: debentures issued to a vendor

Machinery worth ₹1,90,000 is bought and paid for by issuing ₹100 debentures at a 5% discount. Number issued = 1,90,000 ÷ 95 = 2,000 debentures.

Machinery A/c Dr 1,90,000 To Vendor 1,90,000; then Vendor A/c Dr 1,90,000 and Discount on Issue A/c Dr 10,000 To Debentures A/c 2,00,000.

How to solve

  1. Write the face value, issue price and redemption value for each case.
  2. Discount or premium on issue = face value − issue price.
  3. Premium on redemption payable = redemption value − face value; record it at issue (debit loss on issue).
  4. Pass the application/allotment (or vendor) entry for each case.

Formulas

Loss on issue = Discount on issue + Premium payable on redemption

How to write the answer

Journal entries with narrations; separate the entries for each case.

Common mistakes

  • Forgetting to record the premium payable on redemption as a liability at the time of issue.
