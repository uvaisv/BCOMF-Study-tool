Issue, Forfeiture & Reissue of Shares

High Asked in 7 of 9 papers · avg 13 marks

Stages of issue of shares and journal entries, pro-rata allotment, calls in arrears, forfeiture and reissue, kinds of shares, allotment rules and book building.

In your IGNOU bookUnit 2 Accounting for Share Capital

Key points

  • Stages: application → allotment → calls (first, second, final); each stage has an 'amount due' entry and a 'receipt' entry.
  • Pro-rata allotment: excess application money is adjusted against allotment (and calls, if the articles allow) and the rest refunded.
  • Forfeiture: Share Capital Dr (called-up value) To Share Forfeited (amount received) To unpaid allotment/calls. Reissue: Bank Dr, Share Forfeited Dr (discount) To Share Capital; the discount cannot exceed the amount forfeited. The balance on forfeited shares reissued goes to capital reserve.
  • Kinds of shares (Sec 43): equity (with voting rights, or with differential rights) and preference shares.
  • Valid allotment: by proper authority (board), within reasonable time, communicated, absolute, after minimum subscription is received (Sec 39), against an application.
  • Book building: price discovered from investors' bids within a price band; steps — appoint book-running lead manager, file red herring prospectus, bidding period, record demand, fix cut-off price, allot and refund.

Worked example

Example: pro-rata allotment, forfeiture and reissue

Shares of ₹10: ₹3 on application, ₹4 on allotment, ₹3 on call. Allotment is pro-rata. A holder applied for 300 shares and got 200.

Excess application money 100 × ₹3 = ₹300 is adjusted against allotment (due 200 × 4 = ₹800), so they pay ₹500. They fail to pay the call.

Forfeiture: Share Capital Dr 2,000 (200 × ₹10) To Call A/c 600 To Share Forfeited A/c 1,400 (the ₹600 application + ₹800 allotment already received).

Reissue at ₹8 fully paid: Bank Dr 1,600, Share Forfeited Dr 400 To Share Capital 2,000.

Balance ₹1,000 on Share Forfeited → capital reserve.

How to solve

  1. Make a table of shares applied, allotted and rejected; compute excess money per category.
  2. Pass entries for application money received and its transfer (to capital, allotment and refund).
  3. Allotment due and received (less excess application money adjusted); calls due and received.
  4. For defaulters, compute unpaid amounts on each stage.
  5. Forfeiture entry at called-up value; reissue entry; capital reserve for the gain on shares reissued.
  6. Prepare the Balance Sheet share capital note (subscribed and fully paid, forfeited shares).

Formulas

Capital reserve = Amount forfeited on reissued shares − Discount on reissue

How to write the answer

Journal entries with narrations → the share capital note in the Balance Sheet.

Common mistakes

  • Transferring the whole forfeited amount to capital reserve when only some shares are reissued.
