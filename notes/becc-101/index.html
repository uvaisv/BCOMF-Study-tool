Exam tips

  • Section A: a demand–supply equation question (find equilibrium P* and Q*) and a production question (three stages, isoquants) appear almost every time — both are safe.
  • Section B: price, income and substitution effects (Hicks/Slutsky, Giffen goods) and the labour-market minimum-wage numerical keep returning.
  • Section C is compulsory: it usually asks market failure/externalities, oligopoly (kinked demand, Stackelberg) or rent — prepare these three.
  • Draw a labelled diagram for every answer that has a curve in it — axes, curves, equilibrium point. Diagrams carry a large share of the marks.
  • For equilibrium questions: set Qd = Qs, solve for P*, then substitute to get Q*. Sketch both lines.
  • For elasticity: write the formula, substitute, and state whether demand is elastic (> 1), unitary (= 1) or inelastic (< 1).
