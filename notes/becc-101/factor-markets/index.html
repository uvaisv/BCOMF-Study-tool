Labour Market & Rent

High Asked in 11 of 11 papers · avg 9 marks

Labour demand/supply numericals with minimum wage, backward-bending supply, wage differences, and theories of rent.

In your IGNOU bookBlock 5 · Unit 13 Factor Market and Pricing DecisionsBlock 5 · Unit 14 Labour MarketBlock 5 · Unit 15 Land Market

Key points

  • Labour supply from labour–leisure choice; backward-bending at high wages when the income effect outweighs the substitution effect.
  • A minimum wage above equilibrium reduces employment to labour demanded at that wage (unemployment = Ls − Ld).
  • Wage differences: skill and training, risk, non-monetary benefits, trade unions, discrimination, immobility.
  • Ricardian rent: payment for the original, indestructible powers of the soil; arises from differences in fertility and scarcity.
  • Modern theory: economic rent = earnings above transfer earnings, for any factor.
  • Quasi-rent (Marshall): short-run surplus earned by machines and other factors fixed in the short run.
  • Marginal productivity theory: a factor's reward is set by its marginal product; firms hire a factor until its marginal revenue product (MRP) equals its price. Under perfect competition MRP = VMP = MP × price of output.
  • Demand for a factor is derived demand — it comes from the demand for the goods it helps to make.
  • Labour unions bargain for higher wages; a union wage above equilibrium works like a minimum wage — higher pay for those employed but fewer jobs.

Worked examples

Example: wage and a minimum wage

Ld = 100 − 2W and Ls = 20 + 2W → equilibrium wage ₹20, employment 60.

A minimum wage of ₹25: firms hire only 50 (Ld), while 70 want work (Ls) → 20 unemployed.

Marginal productivity: if the 5th worker adds 4 units, each sold at ₹50, their MRP is ₹200; at a wage of ₹200 the firm hires up to the 5th worker.

Example: economic rent

A popular singer earns ₹10 lakh a year; their best alternative, teaching music, pays ₹3 lakh (transfer earnings). Economic rent = 10 − 3 = ₹7 lakh — the surplus above what keeps them in this job.
