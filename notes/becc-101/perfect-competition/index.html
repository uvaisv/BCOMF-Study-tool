Perfect Competition

Critical Asked in 9 of 11 papers · avg 14 marks

Short- and long-run equilibrium, shutdown point, supply curve, efficiency and the role of marginal cost.

In your IGNOU bookBlock 4 · Unit 9 Perfect Competition: Firm and Industry Equilibrium

Key points

  • Features: many buyers and sellers, homogeneous product, free entry and exit, perfect knowledge; firm is a price-taker (P = AR = MR).
  • Equilibrium: MC = MR and MC rising.
  • Short run: firm may earn profit or loss; it continues if P ≥ AVC. Shutdown point: P = minimum AVC.
  • Supply curve of the firm = rising part of MC above minimum AVC.
  • Long run: P = MR = MC = minimum LAC — normal profit, optimal capacity, productive and allocative efficiency (P = MC).
  • A firm operating at a loss may stay open in the short run if it covers variable costs and part of fixed costs.
  • Break-even point: P = minimum ATC (normal profit only). Market supply is the horizontal sum of the firms' supply curves.

Worked example

Example: a price-taking firm

TC = 100 + 10q + q², so MC = 10 + 2q. The market price is ₹50.

Set P = MC: 50 = 10 + 2q → q = 20. TR = 1,000; TC = 100 + 200 + 400 = 700 → profit ₹300.

ATC = 100/q + 10 + q is lowest at q = 10, where it is ₹30. At P = ₹30 the firm earns only normal profit (break-even). AVC = 10 + q is at least ₹10, so the firm shuts down if the price falls below ₹10.

How to solve

  1. Profit maximisation: set P = MC, solve for q, check MC is rising; profit = TR − TC.

Formulas

Equilibrium: P = MC
Shutdown: P < min AVC
