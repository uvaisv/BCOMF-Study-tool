PPC, Scarcity & Basic Concepts

High Asked in 11 of 11 papers · avg 12 marks

Production possibility curve, central problems, scarcity and choice, micro vs macro, positive vs normative.

In your IGNOU bookBlock 1 · Unit 1 Introduction to Economics and Economy

Key points

  • PPC: combinations of two goods an economy can produce with given resources and technology, fully and efficiently used.
  • Concave to the origin because opportunity cost increases; a straight line if cost is constant.
  • Point inside = unemployment or inefficiency; on the curve = efficient; outside = unattainable.
  • Shifts outward with more resources or better technology; unemployment does not shift it — the economy moves inside.
  • Central problems: what, how and for whom to produce, plus efficiency and growth.
  • Micro vs macro: individual units vs the whole economy. Positive (what is) vs normative (what ought to be). Static vs dynamic analysis. Stock (at a point) vs flow (over time).
  • Scarcity: wants are unlimited but resources are limited and have alternative uses — so every society must choose, and every choice has an opportunity cost.

Worked example

Example: a PPC schedule and increasing opportunity cost

Butter 0, 1, 2, 3, 4, 5 with guns 15, 14, 12, 9, 5, 0.

Each extra unit of butter costs 1, 2, 3, 4, then 5 guns — rising opportunity cost, so the PPC is concave to the origin.

Producing 2 butter and 8 guns is inside the curve (it could make 12 guns) → unemployment or waste. 4 butter and 9 guns is outside → unattainable with present resources; better technology would shift the curve out.
