YouTube, AdSense & Digitisation

High Asked in 6 of 9 papers · avg 10 marks

YouTube channels and revenue, Google AdSense, digitisation's impact on business, AI.

In your IGNOU bookBlock 4 · Unit 14 Multimedia, Video-Making and YouTubeBlock 2 · Unit 6 IT Security Measures in Business (6.9 Digitization)

Key points

  • Multimedia combines text, images, sound, animation and video; animation creates the illusion of movement from still images, and PowerPoint has built-in animation tools.
  • YouTube is a video-sharing site. To upload: sign in → click Upload → choose the video and privacy setting → manage the upload.
  • Creating a channel: sign in with a Google account → create channel → add name, logo and description → upload and customise.
  • Google AdSense (2003) places ads on websites and YouTube and pays the owner for clicks or views; it is free and easy to join, but has strict policies and earns little without traffic.
  • YouTube pays creators through the YouTube Partner Programme, which has minimum subscriber and watch-time conditions.
  • Digitisation: converting information and processes to digital form — faster service, lower cost, online markets and data-based decisions.
  • AI now automates animation and rendering of visual effects.

Worked example

Example: a teacher starts a YouTube channel

She signs in with a Google account, creates the channel 'Easy Accounts', adds a logo and description, and uploads short lessons, choosing 'Public' as the privacy setting.

Once the channel meets the YouTube Partner Programme conditions on subscribers and watch time, ads (through AdSense) can be shown on her videos and she earns a share of the ad revenue.
