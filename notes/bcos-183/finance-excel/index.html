Ratios, Capital Budgeting & Depreciation

Critical Asked in 6 of 9 papers · avg 15 marks

Ratio analysis, NPV and IRR (with Excel syntax), depreciation methods, loan and lease statements, payroll.

In your IGNOU bookBlock 5 · Unit 20 Creating Business Spreadsheets

Key points

  • Loan statement: prepared by the lender — loan amount, interest rate, period and instalments (Excel =PMT gives the EMI). Lease statement: the periodic payments by the lessee (tenant) to the lessor (owner).
  • Ratio analysis uses the trading account, profit and loss account and balance sheet to judge the firm's position.
  • Current ratio = current assets ÷ current liabilities (2:1 is the usual norm). Quick ratio = quick assets ÷ current liabilities (1:1).
  • Gross profit ratio = GP ÷ net sales × 100; net profit ratio = NP ÷ net sales × 100; inventory turnover = cost of goods sold ÷ average inventory.
  • Payroll statement: employee name, code, category, basic pay, allowances and perquisites, deductions, tax and net salary.
  • Capital budgeting evaluates long-term projects by their cash inflows and outflows. NPV = present value of inflows − outlay (accept if positive); IRR is the rate at which NPV = 0 (accept if above the cost of capital).
  • In Excel: =NPV(rate, later cash flows) + the initial outlay (entered as negative); =IRR(range including the outlay).
  • Depreciation: straight line (same amount each year, =SLN) and diminishing balance (fixed % of the reducing balance, =DB).

Worked examples

Example: EMI with PMT

A loan of ₹1,00,000 at 10% a year, repaid monthly over 12 months:

=PMT(10%/12, 12, -100000) gives an EMI of ₹8,791.59.

Example: NPV and IRR in Excel

Outlay ₹1,00,000 in B1 (entered as −100000); inflows of ₹45,000 in B2:B4 for 3 years; cost of capital 10%.

=NPV(10%, B2:B4) + B1 = 1,11,908 − 1,00,000 = ₹11,908 → positive, so accept.

=IRR(B1:B4) ≈ 16.6%, above the 10% cost of capital.

Depreciation of a ₹1,00,000 asset, scrap ₹10,000, life 5 years: =SLN(100000, 10000, 5) = ₹18,000 a year.

Formulas

Current ratio = CA / CL
NPV = Σ CFₜ/(1 + r)ᵗ − C₀
=PMT(rate, nper, pv)

How to write the answer

Common mistakes

  • Forgetting that Excel's NPV() does not include the year-0 outlay.
