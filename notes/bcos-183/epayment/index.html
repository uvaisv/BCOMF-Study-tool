E-payment, Plastic Money & Wallets

High Asked in 8 of 9 papers · avg 11 marks

E-payment methods, plastic money, NEFT vs RTGS, digital wallets, QR codes, B2B vs B2C.

In your IGNOU bookBlock 2 · Unit 8 Plastic Money, E-Wallets and Online Pay

Key points

  • Plastic money: credit, debit, smart, charge and stored-value cards, named after the plastic they are made of; money evolved from barter to coins, paper, cheques and cards.
  • E-payment: paying for goods and services electronically without cash or cheques.
  • Methods in the book: QR code (2-D barcode scanned to pay), UPI (virtual address, no account number needed), digital wallets, USSD (*99# on basic phones), MMID (7-digit mobile money ID), IMPS, AEPS (pay with Aadhaar), NFC, internet banking and cards.
  • E-wallet: a virtual wallet loaded with money from cards or bank for merchant payments and transfers.
  • Growth drivers: Digital India, demonetisation, cheap smartphones and data, better payment infrastructure and wallet promotions.
  • NEFT settles in batches; RTGS settles each payment in real time and is meant for large amounts (minimum ₹2 lakh).
  • Safety: never share OTP or PIN, use secure (https) sites, check merchant details, turn on alerts.
  • B2B e-commerce: business sells to business; B2C: business sells to consumers.

Worked examples

Example: choosing a payment method

Paying a vegetable seller ₹60 → scan their QR code with a UPI app.

Sending ₹20,000 to a relative at night → IMPS or UPI, which work 24×7 and credit at once.

Paying a supplier ₹5 lakh → RTGS (for ₹2 lakh and above; settled individually in real time).

A farmer with a basic phone and no internet → dial *99# (USSD) to transfer money.

Example: B2B vs B2C

A textile mill ordering dyes from a chemical company's online portal → B2B. A student buying earphones on a shopping app → B2C.

How to write the answer

Meaning → methods as headed points → merits and precautions → conclusion.
