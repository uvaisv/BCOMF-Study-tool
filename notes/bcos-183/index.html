Exam tips

  • Cyber security (CIA principles, authentication vs authorisation, CAPTCHA, GDPR) appears in every paper — the most reliable topic.
  • 'Compare' and 'short notes' questions (4–5 parts × 4–5 marks) reward broad revision: learn one-line differences for every pair in the Key points.
  • Mail merge, operating systems and e-payment methods are asked most sessions.
  • For ratios, write the formula, then an example with figures, then what a high or low value means.
  • For NPV/IRR, show the Excel syntax: =NPV(rate, value1, …) + initial outlay, and =IRR(values).
