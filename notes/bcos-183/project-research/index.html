DPR, Research Proposal & Data Analysis

Moderate Asked in 5 of 9 papers · avg 7 marks

Detailed project report, research proposal components, data collection, frequency distribution, correlation and regression.

In your IGNOU bookBlock 3 · Unit 12 Creating Business Documentation (12.6 Detailed project report)Block 4 · Unit 15 Creating Business PresentationBlock 5 · Unit 19 Advanced Options in Spreadsheets (19.5–19.9)

Key points

  • A detailed project report (DPR) is the base document for investment, approval, planning and implementation of a project.
  • DPR structure in the book: title page; name, affiliation, date; acknowledgement; contents; abbreviations; executive summary; introduction; background; technical and financial analysis; recommended action plan; appendices.
  • Business presentations inform, educate, motivate and persuade; types: group, presentation aid, persuasive and informative.
  • A research proposal presentation covers title, abstract, literature review, objectives, research methodology, references and annexes.
  • Project presentation steps: analyse the audience → select the topic → define the objective → prepare the body → prepare the introduction and conclusion.
  • Excel's Data Analysis ToolPak gives frequency distribution, descriptive statistics (mean, median, mode, SD, range, variance), correlation, regression and hypothesis tests (t-tests, z-test).
  • Correlation measures the strength of a linear relationship between two variables; regression shows how the dependent variable relates to independent variables.

Worked examples

Example: a DPR for a small bakery unit

Executive summary (the project in one page) → introduction and background (demand for bakery products in the town) → technical analysis (site, ovens, capacity, staff) → financial analysis (project cost ₹25 lakh, funding, projected sales and profit, break-even, loan repayment) → recommended action plan with a timeline → appendices (quotations, licences).

Example: Excel Data Analysis ToolPak

Marks of 60 students in column A → Data → Data Analysis → Descriptive Statistics gives the mean, median, mode, standard deviation and range in one table. Choosing Correlation with study hours in column B shows how strongly hours and marks move together.

How to write the answer

Meaning → components as a numbered list → conclusion.
