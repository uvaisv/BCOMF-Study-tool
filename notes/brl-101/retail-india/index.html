Growth, Trends & Challenges of Retail in India

High Asked in 5 of 8 papers · avg 13 marks

Factors driving retail growth in India, global and Indian trends, challenges, future, liberalisation, rural retail.

In your IGNOU bookUnit 1 Retail ScenarioUnit 9 Rural Retailing and E-Tailing (Rural retailing)

Key points

  • Growth factors: rising incomes, young population, urbanisation, nuclear families, working women, credit and digital payments, FDI and liberalisation, growth of malls and e-commerce.
  • Trends: omnichannel, e-commerce and quick commerce, private labels, digital payments (UPI), data analytics, experiential stores, rural retail, sustainability.
  • Global trends: consolidation, omnichannel, automation, personalisation, green retail.
  • Challenges: high real-estate cost, poor infrastructure and supply chain, skilled-manpower shortage, complex regulations, competition from unorganised and online retail, shrinkage.
  • Liberalisation (1991 onwards): FDI in single- and multi-brand retail, entry of global players, better supply chains.
  • Rural retailing: large untapped market; e.g. ITC e-Choupal, Hariyali Kisaan Bazaar.
  • The book notes that retail is India's biggest employer after agriculture and that organised retail is still a small share (just over 10%) of sales; policy has tried to protect small unorganised retailers from big foreign chains.
  • Foreign entry has been bumpy: Walmart's venture with Bharti and Shoprite's with Nirmal Lifestyle broke up, while Indian players such as Reliance Retail grew fast. Congestion, high city rents, and labour and land laws hold organised retail back.

Worked example

Example: a trend in practice — omnichannel and quick commerce

A customer checks a shirt on a retailer's app, tries it in the store, finds her size out of stock, orders it from the store's tablet for home delivery, and pays by UPI. Meanwhile her groceries arrive in 15 minutes from a quick-commerce dark store. Rising incomes, smartphones and digital payments make both possible, while high rents and supply-chain gaps remain challenges.
