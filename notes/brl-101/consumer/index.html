Retail Consumer Behaviour

Critical Asked in 6 of 8 papers · avg 20 marks

Buyer vs consumer, factors influencing retail consumers, buying decision process, situational variables, store image, CRM.

In your IGNOU bookUnit 2 Retail Consumer

Key points

  • Buyer purchases; consumer uses — may be different people.
  • Factors: cultural (culture, sub-culture, class), social (family, reference groups, role and status), personal (age, occupation, income, lifestyle), psychological (motivation, perception, learning, attitude).
  • Buying decision process: need recognition → information search → evaluation of alternatives → purchase decision → post-purchase behaviour (e.g. buying a new car).
  • Situational variables: physical surroundings, social surroundings, time, task (gift or self), mood.
  • Store image: how consumers see a store — merchandise, price, service, ambience, location.
  • Customer relationship marketing: keeping customers through loyalty programmes, personalised offers and service.
  • Buying roles: initiator, influencer, decider, buyer and user — often the household, not one person, is the real target.
  • The book's four situational dimensions: physical setting, social setting, temporal (time) setting and task setting. Every store must communicate its own individuality (store image) to its target customers.

Worked example

Example: situational variables change the purchase

The same shopper buys a cheap umbrella at a station kiosk when it suddenly rains (time pressure, physical surroundings), but a branded gift set after comparing stores when shopping for a friend's wedding (task: a gift, with social surroundings — friends along). The store's image — clean, helpful staff, fair prices — decides where she shops in the second case.
