Security, Finance & Performance in Retail

Moderate Asked in 4 of 8 papers · avg 9 marks

Security arrangements and shrinkage, financial planning, performance measurement and retail audit.

In your IGNOU bookUnit 6 Retail Operations and Store ManagementUnit 7 Legal Environment and Security Issues (Security issues)

Key points

  • Shrinkage: loss of stock through shoplifting, employee theft, administrative errors, vendor fraud and damage.
  • Security measures: CCTV, EAS tags and gates, security guards, mirrors, locked cases, staff checks, cash controls — they deter theft and reduce losses.
  • Financial planning: budgeting sales, costs and capital; inventory valuation; working capital and cash flow; pricing for margin.
  • Performance measures: sales per sq ft, GMROI, inventory turnover, conversion rate, average transaction value, same-store sales growth.
  • Retail audit: systematic check of operations, stock, finance and compliance.
  • Retail audits in the book: store operations audit, inventory audit, financial (commercial) audit and customer-service audit — done internally and externally.
  • CRM keeps customers coming back through membership cards, loyalty and referral programmes; performance is tracked with KRAs and score cards.

Worked example

Example: retail performance measures

Annual sales ₹1.2 crore from a 4,000 sq ft store → ₹3,000 per sq ft.

Gross margin ₹30 lakh on average inventory (at cost) of ₹10 lakh → GMROI 3 (each rupee of stock earns ₹3 of margin).

On one day 1,000 people enter and 250 buy → conversion rate 25%; sales ₹5 lakh ÷ 250 bills → average transaction value ₹2,000.

Shrinkage from shoplifting is controlled with EAS tags, CCTV and bag checks.

How to write the answer

Meaning → measures as headed points → conclusion.
