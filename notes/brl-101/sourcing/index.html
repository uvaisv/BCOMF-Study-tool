Sourcing & Supplier Selection

Moderate Asked in 3 of 8 papers · avg 8 marks

Meaning of sourcing, global sourcing decisions, evaluating and selecting suppliers.

In your IGNOU bookUnit 4 Sourcing and Merchandising

Key points

  • Sourcing: finding and choosing suppliers of merchandise.
  • Global sourcing factors: cost and exchange rates, quality, lead time, duties and trade rules, political stability, logistics, supplier reliability.
  • Supplier evaluation criteria: price, quality, reliability of delivery, financial stability, capacity, flexibility, after-sales support, ethics and compliance.
  • Comparative evaluation: weighted scoring of suppliers on these criteria.

Worked example

Example: weighted scoring of two suppliers

Weights: price 40%, quality 30%, delivery 20%, service 10%.

Supplier A scores 8, 7, 9, 6 → 3.2 + 2.1 + 1.8 + 0.6 = 7.7.

Supplier B scores 9, 6, 6, 8 → 3.6 + 1.8 + 1.2 + 0.8 = 7.4.

A wins although B is cheaper, because of better quality and delivery.

How to write the answer

Meaning → factors as headed points → a small weighted-score table → conclusion.
