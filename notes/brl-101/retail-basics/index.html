Retailing: Meaning, Functions & Structural Change

Critical Asked in 4 of 8 papers · avg 12 marks

Meaning and functions of a retailer, success factors, theories of structural change, careers in retail.

In your IGNOU bookUnit 1 Retail ScenarioUnit 10 Emerging Trends and Careers in Retail Industry (Careers in retail)

Key points

  • Retailing: selling goods and services directly to final consumers for personal use.
  • Functions: assortment, breaking bulk, holding stock, services (credit, delivery), information to producers and customers, creating convenience.
  • Success factors: understanding customers, right location, merchandise and price, service, technology, cost control.
  • Theories of structural change: wheel of retailing (new formats enter low-cost, trade up, leave space for new low-cost entrants), retail accordion (general → specialised → general assortment), retail life cycle (innovation, growth, maturity, decline), dialectic process (thesis + antithesis → synthesis).
  • Employment options: store operations (sales associate, store manager), merchandising and buying, visual merchandising, supply chain, marketing, e-commerce, HR and finance.
  • Newer retail careers named in the book: category management, data analytics, CRM and loyalty, trend analysis and competition screening, plus the growing online-retail roles.

Worked example

Example: the wheel of retailing

A discount chain enters with bare stores, few services and very low prices. As it grows, it adds air-conditioning, trial rooms, home delivery and a loyalty programme — costs and prices rise. That leaves room for a new low-price entrant (now often an online seller) to repeat the cycle.
