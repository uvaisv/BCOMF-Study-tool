Retail Laws, GST & VAT

High Asked in 6 of 8 papers · avg 11 marks

Acts governing retail, Consumer Protection Act, GST and its benefits, VAT.

In your IGNOU bookUnit 7 Legal Environment and Security Issues

Key points

  • Acts: Consumer Protection Act (1986, replaced in 2019), Shops and Establishments Act, Legal Metrology Act (weights and measures), Food Safety and Standards Act, Sale of Goods Act, GST Act, Trade Marks Act, labour laws.
  • Consumer Protection Act: consumer rights (safety, information, choice, to be heard, redressal, education); consumer commissions at district, state and national levels.
  • GST (2017): one indirect tax replacing VAT, excise and service tax. Benefits for retail: no cascading tax, input tax credit, simpler compliance, easier inter-state movement, lower logistics cost.
  • VAT: state tax on value added at each stage (replaced by GST).

Worked example

Example: input tax credit under GST

A retailer buys a mixer for ₹1,000 + 18% GST (₹180) and sells it for ₹1,500 + 18% GST (₹270). It pays the government only ₹270 − ₹180 = ₹90, the tax on its value addition of ₹500. Before GST, taxes at different stages could pile up as tax on tax (cascading).

A customer sold a mixer that breaks in a week can complain to the District Consumer Commission under the Consumer Protection Act, 2019.

How to write the answer

List the acts → explain three or four in detail with relevance to retail → conclusion.
