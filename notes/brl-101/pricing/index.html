Retail Pricing

Low Asked in 4 of 8 papers · avg 5 marks

Methods of setting retail prices, EDLP, psychological pricing.

In your IGNOU bookUnit 4 Sourcing and Merchandising (Pricing strategies)

Key points

  • Methods: cost-oriented (mark-up), demand-oriented, competition-oriented.
  • Strategies: everyday low pricing (EDLP — DMart, Walmart), high-low pricing (frequent promotions), odd/psychological pricing (₹99), prestige pricing, loss leaders, bundle pricing, price lining.
  • Psychological pricing: prices set to influence perception — ₹499 feels cheaper than ₹500.

Worked example

Example: mark-up and pricing strategies

Cost ₹400; mark-up 25% on cost → price ₹500. The same ₹100 is a 20% mark-up on the selling price (100 ÷ 500).

Psychological pricing: ₹499 instead of ₹500. Loss leader: sugar sold at cost to bring shoppers in. EDLP: steady low prices every day. High-low: higher regular prices with frequent sales. Bundle: shampoo + conditioner pack at ₹299.

Meaning → methods with examples → conclusion.
