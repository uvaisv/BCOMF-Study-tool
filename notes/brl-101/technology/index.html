Technology in Retailing

Critical Asked in 6 of 8 papers · avg 15 marks

Applications and trends of technology in retail, POS, SEO/SEM, harmful impacts and precautions, choosing technology.

In your IGNOU bookUnit 8 Technology in Retailing

Key points

  • Applications: POS systems and billing, barcodes and RFID, inventory management, ERP, CRM and loyalty, e-commerce and mobile apps, digital payments, self-checkout, CCTV and EAS tags, data analytics.
  • Trends: AI and personalisation, AR/VR try-ons, omnichannel, cashless and contactless payment, electronic shelf labels, robots in warehouses.
  • POS software: records sales, prints bills, updates stock, gives sales reports.
  • SEO (search engine optimisation): free ranking in search results. SEM (search engine marketing): paid ads in search results.
  • Harmful impact: job losses, high costs, data privacy and cyber risks, over-dependence, digital divide. Remedies: staff training, data security, phased adoption.
  • Factors for choosing technology: cost, scalability, ease of use, integration, vendor support, security.
  • The book warns that retail technology dates quickly, so a retailer must weigh its needs against the risk of buying something that is soon obsolete.

Worked example

Example: technology from shelf to customer

RFID tags let staff count the whole store's stock in minutes; the POS system bills, updates stock and shows the day's best sellers; low stock triggers an automatic reorder in the ERP; the loyalty app sends a personalised coupon. Online, the store appears in free search results through SEO and buys search ads (SEM) during the festival sale.

How to write the answer

Meaning → applications as headed points with retailer examples → precautions → conclusion.
