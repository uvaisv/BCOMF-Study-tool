Salesmanship & Qualities of a Salesperson

Critical Asked in 9 of 9 papers · avg 16 marks

Types of salesmanship, creative selling, role, qualities, duties and skills of a (modern) salesperson; careers.

In your IGNOU bookBlock 1 · Unit 2 SalesmanshipBlock 4 · Unit 14 Career Opportunities in Personal Selling

Key points

  • Salesmanship is the art of persuading people to buy goods or services that satisfy their needs.
  • Types in the book: wholesaler's salesman; manufacturer's salesman (missionary, technical, merchandising, sales promotion); retailer's salesman (indoor and outdoor); specialty salesman (costly durables like cars, furniture, appliances).
  • Creative salesmanship creates demand for old and new products by arousing wants the buyer did not feel; the creative selling process runs from finding prospects and their needs to closing and follow-up.
  • Role of a salesperson: link between firm and customer, creates new customers, sells more to existing ones, solves problems, gives service and gathers market information.
  • Qualities: physical (health, appearance), mental (alertness, memory, imagination), social (courtesy, tact), character (honesty, loyalty) and knowledge of product, company and customer.
  • Why choose a sales career: earning potential, flexibility, job security, satisfaction, a stepping stone to management and in-demand skills. Big sales industries: IT, real estate, pharma, healthcare, food and financial services.
  • Changing role: with digital marketing and e-commerce the salesperson is becoming a trusted adviser and consultant, travelling less and using online audio and video calls more.

Worked examples

Example: creative salesmanship

A water-purifier salesperson meets a family who buy bottled water and never thought of a purifier. By showing them a TDS test of their tap water and working out that the purifier pays for itself in a year compared with bottled water, the salesperson creates a want the family did not feel before.

Example: types of salesmen

A person selling a manufacturer's cement to dealers → manufacturer's salesman. One who explains a new medicine to doctors without taking orders → missionary salesman. An assistant at a clothes shop counter → retailer's indoor salesman. A car showroom executive → specialty salesman.
