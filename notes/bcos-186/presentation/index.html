Sales Presentation, Demonstration & Objections

Critical Asked in 8 of 9 papers · avg 16 marks

Presentation and demonstration steps and strategies, and handling objections.

In your IGNOU bookBlock 3 · Unit 9 Sales Presentation and DemonstrationBlock 3 · Unit 8 Sales Process (Handling objections)

Key points

  • A sales presentation is a talk giving information about a product to persuade people to buy; a demonstration shows the product working.
  • Steps: attract attention → create interest → arouse desire → build conviction → get action.
  • Types of presentation: canned (memorised), organised and tailored (fitted to the customer).
  • Strategies: stimulus–response, AIDA, need identification and satisfaction, consultative selling, professional selling and depth selling.
  • Types of prospects: sceptical, procrastinator, silent, opinionated, talkative, timid and cautious, methodical, impulsive and 'chip on the shoulder' — each needs a different handling.
  • Methods of handling objections: 'yes, but' (indirect denial), counterbalance (offset a valid objection with an advantage), denial (firmly deny a wrong one), question method, failure-to-hear (deliberately ignore) and boomerang (turn the objection into a reason to buy).
  • Rules: never argue, pause before answering, don't over-answer, find out if you don't know, stay confident.

Worked examples

Example: AIDA in a mixer-grinder demonstration

Attention: grinds ice in 10 seconds in front of the customer. Interest: explains the three jars and the motor's warranty. Desire: lets the customer try making chutney. Conviction: shows reviews and the ISI mark. Action: 'I can deliver it today with a festival discount.'

Example: handling objections

'Your price is higher than the online price.' — Yes-but: 'Yes, it is ₹300 more, but you get free installation and a local service centre.'

Boomerang: 'I'm too busy to learn a new software.' — 'That's exactly why it helps: it saves you two hours a day.'

Counterbalance: 'It is heavier than others.' — 'It is, because of the copper motor, which lasts twice as long.'

How to write the answer

Meaning → steps or strategies as headed points, each with one product example → conclusion.

Common mistakes

  • Arguing with the prospect is itself a listed mistake — mention 'never argue' when writing on objections.
