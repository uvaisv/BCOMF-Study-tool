Selling Process, Prospecting & Qualifying

Critical Asked in 7 of 9 papers · avg 17 marks

Steps of the personal selling process; prospecting vs qualifying; types of prospects.

In your IGNOU bookBlock 3 · Unit 8 Sales Process

Key points

  • A sales process is the set of steps a salesperson follows to turn a prospect into a customer; it varies by industry (cars vs insurance).
  • Seven steps: prospecting and qualifying → pre-approach → approach → presentation or demonstration → handling objections → closing → follow-up.
  • Qualifying: a prospect must have the need, the authority, the ability (money) and the eligibility to buy.
  • Prospecting methods: centre of influence, spotters, advertising, endless chain (referrals), observation, cold canvassing, company records and retailers.
  • Pre-approach: learn the prospect's needs, behaviour, likes, preferences and status; it saves time by focusing on real prospects, not mere suspects.
  • Approach methods: introductory, customer-benefit, compliment, reference, interactive, dramatic and sample approach.
  • Objections are a sign of interest — a request for more information, time or assurance.

Worked example

Example: the seven steps in insurance selling

Prospecting: an agent gets names of newly married couples from existing clients (endless chain) and qualifies them — they need cover, can pay and decide themselves.

Pre-approach: learns about their jobs and family plans.

Approach: a reference approach — 'Your friend Ravi suggested I meet you.'

Presentation: shows how a term plan protects the family, with figures.

Objections: 'Premium is too high' → shows the cost per day.

Closing: 'Shall we start the policy from this month?'

Follow-up: helps with medical tests and sends a reminder before each premium.
