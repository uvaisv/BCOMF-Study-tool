Exam tips

  • The same questions repeat almost word for word: buyer behaviour factors, buying motives, sales reports, motivating salespeople, objection handling and closing techniques.
  • Q8 (any two short notes of four) is a safe pick — sales manual, prospecting and ethics come up again and again.
  • Almost every question says 'with examples' — give one Indian example (a product you know) in every point.
