Sales Reports, Documents & Sales Manual

Critical Asked in 9 of 9 papers · avg 23 marks

Sales reports (objectives, features, types), documents salespeople prepare, sales manual, order book.

In your IGNOU bookBlock 4 · Unit 11 Report and Documents in Sales

Key points

  • Sales reports record what salespeople do and achieve, and help managers plan, control and evaluate.
  • Types of documents: progress or call report (each call, orders, competing brands), sales work plan report (accounts, routes, travel ahead), expense report (for reimbursement), new business report, lost sales report, and complaints and adjustments report.
  • Features of a good report: accurate, timely, brief and clear, complete, in a standard format and factual.
  • Sales manual: a guide to the company's best policies, practices, procedures and standards for salespeople; a key training resource that must be kept up to date.
  • Order book: records orders taken — customer, items, quantity, price and delivery terms.
  • Cash memo: given by the seller when goods are sold for cash — date, serial number, names and addresses, description, rate, discount, tax, total in words and figures, signature. It differs from a money receipt, which only acknowledges cash received.
  • Tour diary: a small book of the sales visits made during a period — dates, places, purpose and results.

Worked examples

Example: a day's call report

Date, territory, shops visited (8), orders booked (5 shops, ₹42,000), competing brands seen on display, a complaint about late delivery at one shop, and the plan for tomorrow. The manager uses these reports to check effort, spot problems and plan stock.

Example: cash memo

A customer buys two shirts for cash. The shop gives a cash memo showing its serial number and date, the shop's name and address, the items, rate, discount, GST and total in words and figures, signed by the seller. It serves as proof of purchase for an exchange or a warranty claim.

How to write the answer

Meaning → objectives or contents as headed points → a sample format (columns of a call report) → benefits → conclusion.
