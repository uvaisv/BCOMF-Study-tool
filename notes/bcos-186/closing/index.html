Closing the Sale

Moderate Asked in 5 of 9 papers · avg 8 marks

Closing techniques, trial close and closing cues.

In your IGNOU bookBlock 3 · Unit 10 Concluding

Key points

  • Closing is the outcome of the whole selling process — getting the order after objections are answered. The salesperson must know both how and when to close.
  • Closing cues: buying signals such as questions on price, delivery or guarantee, handling the product closely, or nodding.
  • Trial close: a question used after the presentation to check if the prospect likes the product, whether objections are cleared and whether they are ready; if an indirect trial close fails, try a direct close.
  • Closing techniques in the book: choice, minor-point, assumptive, stimulus–response, summary, special-deal and turnover close.
  • Closing is not the end: follow-up calls calm the buyer's anxiety and start a long-term relationship (relationship marketing).

Worked example

Example: buying signals and closing techniques

The customer asks, 'Does it come in blue, and how soon can you deliver?' — buying signals.

Choice close: 'Would you like the blue one or the grey one?' Assumptive close: 'I'll schedule delivery for Saturday.' Summary close: 'So you get the larger capacity, the 5-year warranty and free installation.' Special-deal close: 'The exchange offer ends today.'

After the sale, a follow-up call a week later reassures the buyer and opens the door to referrals.
