Buyer Behaviour

Critical Asked in 8 of 9 papers · avg 15 marks

Buyer vs consumer, factors affecting buyer behaviour, why understanding it matters.

In your IGNOU bookBlock 2 · Unit 6 Buyer Behaviour

Key points

  • Buyer behaviour: how people decide what, when, where and how to buy.
  • Buyer vs consumer: the buyer purchases; the consumer uses (a parent buys toys, the child uses them) — they may be the same person or different people.
  • Factors: cultural (culture, sub-culture, social class), social (family, reference groups, roles and status), personal (age, occupation, income, lifestyle, personality) and psychological (motivation, perception, learning, beliefs and attitudes).
  • Why a salesperson must know it: to spot needs, choose the right approach, expect objections, build lasting relationships and sell more.

Worked example

Example: buyer vs consumer, and influences

A mother buys a school bag for her 8-year-old: she is the buyer, the child is the consumer, and the child's classmates (a reference group) influence which design is wanted. The mother's budget (personal) and her perception of which brand lasts longer (psychological) decide the final choice. A salesperson must satisfy both the child and the mother.
