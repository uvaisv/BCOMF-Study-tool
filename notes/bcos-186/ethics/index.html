Ethics in Selling

Moderate Asked in 7 of 9 papers · avg 8 marks

Ethical and legal issues in selling, importance of ethics, sales code of conduct.

In your IGNOU bookBlock 4 · Unit 12 Ethics in Selling

Key points

  • Ethics means values and principles of right and wrong; legal issues are those set by government law. Some unethical selling is also illegal.
  • Common issues: misrepresenting or hiding facts, price discrimination, bribes, tie-in sales, false promises or exaggeration (puffery) and invasion of privacy.
  • Bribes are covered by the Contract Act, 1872 and the Sale of Goods Act, 1930.
  • Pressure to meet targets is the main reason salespeople take shortcuts.
  • Compliance techniques: hire the right salespeople, set a code of conduct for ethical selling, train people in ethical selling, and avoid exaggerated or unverified claims.
  • Why it matters: customer trust, long-term relationships, company reputation and avoiding legal trouble.

Worked example

Example: ethical or not?

Telling a customer that a phone is waterproof when it is only splash-resistant → misrepresentation (unethical, and may be illegal). Saying 'our best phone ever!' → puffery, mild, but claims should not be misleading. Offering a purchase officer a gift to win a large order → a bribe. Selling a customer's phone number to another company → invasion of privacy.

A firm prevents such behaviour through a code of conduct, training, and targets that do not push staff to cut corners.
