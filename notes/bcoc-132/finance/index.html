Financial Management & Sources of Finance

Moderate Asked in 7 of 11 papers · avg 7 marks

Functions and objectives of financial management, wealth maximisation, venture capital, lease, shares.

In your IGNOU bookBlock 5 · Unit 19 Financial Management

Key points

  • Financial management: planning, organising, directing and controlling the raising and use of a firm's funds.
  • Functions in the book: estimate capital needs, decide capital composition, choose sources of funds, invest funds, dispose of surplus, manage cash and exercise financial control.
  • Objectives: regular and adequate funds, best use of funds, fair return. The debate is profit maximisation vs wealth maximisation; shareholder wealth maximisation is treated as the main goal.
  • Sources of funds: equity shares (ownership, permanent capital), debentures (long-term borrowing at a fixed rate), venture capital (investment in start-ups with long-term growth potential) and lease financing (use of an asset without owning it — like a hire agreement).
  • Primary market: new securities issued for the first time (e.g. IPO), raising funds for companies. Secondary market: existing securities are traded.
  • SEBI regulates the securities market to protect investors, develop the market and regulate it.

Worked example

Example: choosing sources of finance

A company needs ₹10 crore for a new plant. It raises ₹6 crore through equity shares (permanent capital, no fixed charge) and ₹4 crore through debentures at 9% (fixed interest, but cheaper and no loss of control). Its delivery trucks are taken on lease instead of being bought.

The new shares are sold through an IPO in the primary market; afterwards investors trade them on the stock exchange — the secondary market, regulated by SEBI.

How to write the answer

Definition → functions or objectives as headed points → conclusion.
