Co-operative Organisation

Moderate Asked in 5 of 11 papers · avg 7 marks

Meaning, features, types, merits, limitations and objectives of co-operatives.

In your IGNOU bookBlock 2 · Unit 5 Forms of Business Organisation-I (5.5)

Key points

  • A cooperative is a voluntary association of people who are not financially strong on their own and come together for self-help and mutual help — not mainly for profit.
  • Features: voluntary and open membership, democratic control, service motive, separate entity on registration, limited liability, state help and control.
  • Merits: easy to form, limited liability, government assistance, open membership.
  • Demerits: lack of business skill, weak mutual interest, no secrecy, rivalry among members.
  • The book suits the cooperative form to cases where the interest of a particular section of society has to be looked after.

Worked example

Example: a milk producers' cooperative

Small dairy farmers in a village each have two or three cows and are exploited by middlemen. They form a cooperative society, each buying a share. Every member has one vote whatever their shareholding (democratic control).

The society collects milk, tests it, sells it to a dairy and pays members fairly — service, not profit, is the aim. Anyone in the village who keeps cows may join (open membership).
