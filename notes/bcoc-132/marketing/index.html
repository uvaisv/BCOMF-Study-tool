Marketing (Concepts, PLC, Pricing)

High Asked in 7 of 11 papers · avg 16 marks

Marketing concepts, marketing mix, product life cycle and pricing — asked in almost every paper.

In your IGNOU bookBlock 5 · Unit 18 Marketing Management

Key points

  • Marketing (Kotler): 'human activity directed at satisfying needs and wants through exchange processes' — finding consumer needs, turning them into products and moving them to consumers profitably.
  • Five marketing concepts: production, product, selling, marketing and societal.
  • Selling converts the product into cash; marketing is the whole process of identifying, meeting and satisfying consumer needs.
  • Marketing matters to the business, consumers, the economy and society; in a developing economy its benefits are not fully realised.
  • Marketing mix (the book's four Ps): product, price, promotion and physical distribution (place).
  • Product life cycle: introduction → growth → maturity → decline (obsolescence); knowing the stage helps plan strategy.
  • Prices depend on cost, demand and competition, giving cost-oriented, demand-oriented and competition-oriented pricing.

Worked examples

Example: a new ready-to-cook millet mix through its life cycle

Introduction: few buyers, heavy promotion and free samples, low or no profit.

Growth: sales rise fast, competitors enter, more shops stock it.

Maturity: sales level off; the firm offers new flavours and price packs to defend its share.

Decline: tastes change and sales fall; the firm cuts costs or withdraws the product.

Example: selling concept vs marketing concept

Selling concept: a firm has unsold stock of heavy steel cupboards and pushes them through discounts and aggressive salesmen.

Marketing concept: the firm first finds that young renters want light, foldable cupboards, designs one, prices it for them and sells it online — satisfying the need at a profit.

How to write the answer

Definition → a PLC curve diagram (sales vs time) → each stage with features and strategy → conclusion.
