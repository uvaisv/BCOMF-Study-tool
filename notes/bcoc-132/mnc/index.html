Multinational Corporations

Low Asked in 2 of 11 papers · avg 4 marks

Why firms become multinational; issues, trends and controversies of MNCs.

In your IGNOU bookBlock 2 · Unit 8 International Business: Multinational Corporation

Key points

  • International business: trade in goods, services and aids to trade between two or more countries.
  • Importance: spreads technology, drives globalisation, better use of resources, faster growth, more goods and jobs, lower costs and more government revenue.
  • MNC: a company with subsidiaries and affiliates in many countries, under central control, aiming at global profit maximisation.
  • Why firms become multinational: economies of scale, a growing world market, response to foreign competition, lower costs, avoiding tariffs and using technological strength.
  • Features: large size, large foreign sales, many products, command over technology and innovation, a common pool of resources and a common strategy for affiliates.
  • Issues and controversies: conflict with host-country interests, high profit orientation, reluctance to transfer technology, restrictive practices, transfer pricing, few top posts for locals, balance-of-payments strain, weak local linkages, influence over host governments.

Worked example

Example: how an MNC works and where it clashes

A car maker headquartered abroad sets up a subsidiary in India, sells cars here and exports parts to its other plants — decisions on models and technology come from head office (central control).

Benefits to India: jobs, technology, tax revenue and exports. Issues: it may buy parts from its own foreign units at high prices (transfer pricing) to show lower profit in India, and keep top posts and key technology at home.

How to write the answer

Definition → reasons → merits and demerits → conclusion.
