Communication

Moderate Asked in 5 of 11 papers · avg 6 marks

Process, barriers and making communication effective.

In your IGNOU bookBlock 4 · Unit 14 Communication and Coordination

Key points

  • Communication: exchange of facts, ideas, opinions or feelings between two or more persons. It is two-way and is complete only when the receiver understands and responds.
  • Elements: communicator, encoding, message, medium, decoding, receiver and feedback.
  • Channels: formal (follows the chain of command) and informal (the grapevine, based on social relations).
  • By direction: vertical (downward from superiors, upward from subordinates), horizontal or lateral (between equals) and diagonal (different levels and departments).
  • Importance: better job performance, willing cooperation, better decisions, makes delegation possible, aids coordination and builds morale.
  • Barriers in the book: too many organisational layers, language, status, physical distance, emotional and psychological barriers.
  • Coordination: harmonising group efforts for unity of action towards a common purpose; it is continuous and a responsibility of every manager. Objectives: reconcile goals, achieve all goals, harmonious relations, economy and efficiency.

Worked example

Example: the communication process and its channels

A sales manager writes an email (encoding) telling salespeople a price change (message) through the company mail (medium). Salespeople read and interpret it (decoding) and reply with questions (feedback).

Downward: the manager's instruction. Upward: a salesperson's report. Horizontal: the sales and production managers sort out a delivery date. Grapevine: a rumour about bonuses spreads over tea.

A barrier: technical jargon the new salespeople don't understand.
