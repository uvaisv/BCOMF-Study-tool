Business, Commerce, Trade & Profession

High Asked in 7 of 11 papers · avg 11 marks

Business vs profession vs employment, commerce, trade and aids to trade, industry, objectives of business.

In your IGNOU bookBlock 1 · Unit 1 Introduction to Business

Key points

  • Human activities are economic (done to earn a living) or non-economic (done out of love, duty, religion or social feeling).
  • Economic activities are of three kinds: business (producing or exchanging goods and services for profit), profession (personal, specialised service needing training, e.g. doctor, lawyer) and employment (working for an employer under a contract or service rules).
  • Features of business: deals in goods or services, production and/or exchange, regular dealings, profit motive, risk and enterprise.
  • Objectives of business go beyond profit: economic, social and human objectives.
  • Business = industry + commerce. Industry: extractive (mining), genetic (breeding plants and animals), manufacturing and construction.
  • Commerce = trade (buying and selling) + aids to trade — transport, warehousing, banking, insurance and advertising — which remove hindrances and keep goods moving.
  • Four basic forms of business organisation: sole proprietorship, partnership, company and cooperative society.
  • An entrepreneur conceives the business idea, gathers the resources and brings the organisation into being; common traits are independence, hard work, drive to achieve, open mind and optimism.
  • MSMEs are grouped into manufacturing enterprises and service enterprises (services are measured by investment in equipment).

Worked example

Examples: classify the activity

A doctor running a clinic → profession. A nurse working in a hospital for a salary → employment. A shopkeeper selling groceries → business (trade). A farm growing flower seedlings to sell → genetic industry. A mother cooking for her family → non-economic activity.

Aids to trade in one sale: a Kerala spice trader's goods travel by truck (transport), wait in a warehouse (warehousing), are insured (insurance), paid for through a bank (banking) and promoted online (advertising).

How to write the answer

Definition → classification chart → table comparing business, profession and employment → conclusion.
