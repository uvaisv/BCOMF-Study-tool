Human Resource Management

Low Asked in 7 of 11 papers · avg 7 marks

Functions of HRM, challenges for HR managers, HR planning, employer–employee relations, team building.

In your IGNOU bookBlock 5 · Unit 20 Human Resource ManagementBlock 4 · Unit 17 Team Building

Key points

  • HRM: all activities for acquiring, using, maintaining and developing an organisation's human resources.
  • Functions: managerial (planning, organising, directing, controlling), operative (procurement, development, compensation, integration, maintenance, separation) and advisory (to top management and department heads).
  • Human resource planning: forecasting the organisation's future need for people and planning how to meet it; job analysis finds the tasks and skills a job requires.
  • Skills of HR professionals include interpersonal, communication, negotiation, problem-solving and conflict management, ethical, strategic and technology skills.
  • Challenges: responding to market change, managing HR globally, CSR and sustainability, using technology, controlling costs while keeping talent, demographic and diversity changes.
  • Employee empowerment gives staff at all levels authority to decide on their own; employee engagement is being psychologically involved in and committed to the job.
  • Team: two or more people interacting to reach a common goal; can be formal (command team, committee, quality circle) or informal.
  • Stages of team development: forming → storming → norming → performing → adjourning. Team effectiveness means meeting the team's objectives and its ability to survive.

Worked example

Example: HR in a new hospital wing

HR planning: the wing needs 40 nurses in six months; 10 can be moved from other wards, so 30 must be hired. Job analysis lists the tasks and skills each nursing post needs.

Operative functions follow: recruit and select (procurement), train (development), fix pay (compensation), look after welfare and safety (maintenance).

The new team goes through forming and storming (role clashes) before norming and performing.

How to write the answer

Definition → functions as headed points → conclusion.
