Decision-Making

Moderate Asked in 5 of 11 papers · avg 7 marks

Meaning, relationship with planning, and the steps of scientific decision-making.

In your IGNOU bookBlock 3 · Unit 9 Planning and Decision Making (9.10 Decision making)

Key points

  • A decision is a choice among alternatives about what should or should not be done (Drucker; McFarland).
  • Three elements: an act of choice, possible alternatives, and a conclusion (picking the best).
  • Process: identify the problem or opportunity → explore alternatives → evaluate them → select the best → implement → follow up.
  • A problem endangers the organisation's ability to reach its objectives; an opportunity offers a chance to exceed them.
  • Planning and decision-making go together: planning is itself a series of decisions about the future.

Worked example

Example: a shop's sales are falling

Problem: sales down 20% since a supermarket opened nearby.

Alternatives: cut prices, start home delivery, or stock specialty local products.

Evaluate: price cuts hurt margin; delivery needs a boy and a bike (low cost); specialty products need new suppliers.

Select: start home delivery through phone orders. Implement it, then follow up by checking sales after two months.

How to write the answer

Definition → flow chart of steps → one example → conclusion.
