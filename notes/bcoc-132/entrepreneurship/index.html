Innovation, Skill Development & Start-ups

High Asked in 7 of 11 papers · avg 13 marks

Innovation types and process, skill development initiatives, Start-up India, Make in India, MSMEs.

In your IGNOU bookBlock 1 · Unit 2 Technological Innovation and Skill Development

Key points

  • Creativity is having new, useful ideas; invention is a new product or process idea; innovation turns an idea or invention into a good or service customers will pay for.
  • Types of innovation in the book: incremental, disruptive, architectural and radical.
  • Make in India: government drive to get firms (including through FDI) to set up manufacturing in India, for jobs and GDP growth. 'Made in India' is the tag on goods made with Indian land, labour, capital, enterprise and technology.
  • Digital India (2015): aims to make India a digital nation, speed up work and link rural and urban areas.
  • Skill development is led by the Ministry of Skill Development and Entrepreneurship (MSDE): Indian Institute of Skills, Pradhan Mantri Kaushal Vikas Yojana, National Apprenticeship Promotion Scheme, dual system of training, space-based distance learning.
  • Startup India (2016) builds a friendly ecosystem for start-ups. Its action plan has three pillars: simplification and handholding; funding support and incentives; industry-academia partnership and incubation.
  • An incubator is an organisation that helps start-ups grow faster and succeed (space, guidance, access to funds).

Worked example

Example: from idea to start-up

Creativity: a student notices farmers selling vegetables cheaply because they spoil. Invention: a low-cost solar cold box. Innovation: turning it into a product farmers rent per day, so they can sell later at a better price.

The founder registers under Startup India, joins a college incubator for space and mentoring, and trains local technicians through a skill-development scheme.
