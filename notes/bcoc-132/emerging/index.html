E-commerce & Emerging Trends

High Asked in 7 of 11 papers · avg 12 marks

E-commerce vs traditional commerce, m-commerce, Digital India, outsourcing, franchising.

In your IGNOU bookBlock 1 · Unit 4 Emerging Opportunities in Business

Key points

  • The internet has changed how firms operate and talk to customers. Internet of Things: a network of devices that connect and exchange data.
  • Emerging trends listed in the book: automation, blockchain, artificial intelligence, machine learning, social shopping, robotics, e-tailing and retail entrepreneurship.
  • Technology brings both opportunities and threats; benefits include lower costs, smoother communication and room to expand.
  • E-commerce: doing business, including customer service, over the internet; it gives access to large markets. Features include real-time shopping, mobile apps, multi-channel selling, big data, personalisation, push notifications and social media.
  • M-commerce is e-commerce on mobile devices. An e-wallet is a virtual wallet you load with money to make digital payments.
  • Franchising: the franchisor (owner of the original business) sells the right to use its brand and system to a franchisee.
  • Outsourcing: contracting a third party to do certain functions to cut costs. Offshoring: moving a business process from one country to another.

Worked example

Example: one small shop, many trends

A handloom shop sells on its own website and on an app (e-commerce, m-commerce), accepts payment through e-wallets and UPI, and sends push notifications about new arrivals.

It outsources delivery to a courier company and its bookkeeping to an accountant's firm. Later it lets a trader in another city open a shop under its name and design (franchising), in return for a fee.

How to write the answer

Definition → comparison table (traditional vs e-commerce) → merits and limitations → conclusion.
