Transportation & Warehousing

Moderate Asked in 4 of 9 papers · avg 4 marks

Modes of transportation, types and functions of warehouses, and documents used in transportation and warehousing.

In your IGNOU bookBlock 2 · Unit 7 Transportation, Warehousing and StorageBlock 3 · Unit 13 Effective Logistics Management: Challenges (13.3 Efficient transportation)

Key points

  • Modes of transport: road (flexible, door to door, short distances), rail (bulk, long distances, cheap), water — inland, coastal and ocean (cheapest, slow), air (fastest, costly; for high-value and perishable goods), pipeline (oil and gas) and multimodal transport using containers.
  • Choosing a mode: cost, speed, reliability, capacity, nature of goods, distance, safety and accessibility.
  • Functions of warehouses: storage, consolidation, break-bulk, cross-docking, value-added services (packing, labelling), price stabilisation, risk bearing and financing through warehouse receipts.
  • Types of warehouses: private, public, bonded, government (Central and State Warehousing Corporations), cooperative, cold storage, distribution centres and automated warehouses.
  • Transport documents: bill of lading (sea), airway bill (air), lorry receipt or consignment note (road), railway receipt, e-way bill (GST), delivery challan, invoice, packing list, certificate of origin, insurance certificate, shipping bill.
  • Warehouse documents: warehouse receipt, goods received note (GRN), stock or bin card, material issue slip, delivery order, dock receipt.

Worked example

Example: choosing a mode and the documents

Perishable flowers from Bengaluru to Dubai → air (fast; airway bill). Coal from Odisha to a power plant in Punjab → rail (bulk, long distance; railway receipt). Furniture from a Jodhpur factory to a Delhi shop → road (door to door; lorry receipt and an e-way bill for goods over ₹50,000). Cars exported to Africa → sea (cheapest for heavy loads; bill of lading).

A cross-docking warehouse unloads incoming trucks and reloads goods onto outbound trucks the same day, without storing them.

How to write the answer

Meaning → modes or types as a table (mode, advantage, limitation) → conclusion.
