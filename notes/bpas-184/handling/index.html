Material Handling & Packaging

High Asked in 8 of 9 papers · avg 7 marks

Material handling systems and their significance, types of material handling equipment, and packaging and disposal of packaging waste.

In your IGNOU bookBlock 2 · Unit 6 Material Handling and Packaging

Key points

  • Material handling: moving, storing, protecting and controlling materials inside factories, warehouses and distribution centres.
  • Significance: cuts handling cost and damage, speeds the flow of goods, improves use of space, raises productivity, improves safety and reduces worker fatigue.
  • Principles: plan the system, standardise, minimise handling (work principle), ergonomics, unit loads, space utilisation, see it as a system, automate where it pays, consider the environment and life-cycle cost.
  • Equipment types: transport equipment (conveyors, cranes, forklifts, pallet jacks, automated guided vehicles); positioning equipment (lift tables, hoists); unit-load equipment (pallets, containers, shrink wrap); storage equipment (racks, shelves, bins, mezzanines, automated storage and retrieval systems); identification and control (barcodes, RFID).
  • Material handling systems: manual, mechanised and automated (AS/RS, AGVs, robots).
  • Packaging protects goods, makes handling easier, identifies the product and promotes it. Disposing of packaging waste: reduce, reuse, recycle, recover energy, compost, and use landfill only as a last resort; producers carry responsibility under extended producer responsibility (EPR) rules.

Worked example

Example: applying material-handling principles

A warehouse moves goods in loose cartons by hand. It shifts to palletised unit loads moved by forklifts and pallet jacks, cutting the number of handlings, damage and worker strain. Fast-moving items are placed near the dispatch bay to reduce travel. Later, an automated storage and retrieval system (AS/RS) is added for small, high-value parts.

Meaning → significance → types of equipment as headings with an example each → conclusion.
